Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q1. A किसी काम को 12 दिन में और B उसी काम को 18 दिन में पूरा कर सकता है। दोनों मिलकर यह काम कितने दिन में पूरा करेंगे?

  1. 15 दिन
  2. 6 दिन
  3. 8 दिन
  4. 7.2 दिन
उत्तर और व्याख्या देखें

(D) 7.2 दिन

व्याख्या: A एक दिन में काम का 1/12 और B 1/18 भाग करता है। दोनों मिलकर एक दिन में = 1/12 + 1/18 = 3/36 + 2/36 = 5/36 भाग। इसलिए कुल समय = 36/5 = 7.2 दिन। 15 दिन सिर्फ़ 12 और 18 का औसत है – यह आम ग़लती है।

⚡ शॉर्टकट ट्रिक: मिलकर समय = (A × B)/(A + B) = (12 × 18)/(12 + 18) = 216/30 = 7.2 दिन।

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q2. रवि 6 किमी उत्तर की ओर चलता है, फिर दाएँ मुड़कर 8 किमी चलता है। अब वह अपने शुरुआती स्थान से कितनी दूर है?

  1. 10 किमी
  2. 14 किमी
  3. 2 किमी
  4. 12 किमी
उत्तर और व्याख्या देखें

(A) 10 किमी

व्याख्या: उत्तर से दाएँ मुड़ने पर वह पूर्व की ओर चलता है। दोनों रास्ते समकोण बनाते हैं, इसलिए दूरी = √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10 किमी। 14 किमी कुल चली गई दूरी है, सीधी दूरी नहीं।

⚡ शॉर्टकट ट्रिक: 6, 8, 10 त्रिक 3-4-5 का दोगुना है, इसलिए उत्तर सीधे 10 किमी।

MathsTime and work1/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q1. A can finish a piece of work in 12 days and B can finish it in 18 days. In how many days will they finish it working together?

  1. 15 days
  2. 6 days
  3. 8 days
  4. 7.2 days
Show answer and explanation

(D) 7.2 days

Explanation: In one day A does 1/12 and B does 1/18 of the work. Together = 1/12 + 1/18 = 3/36 + 2/36 = 5/36 of the work per day. So the time = 36/5 = 7.2 days. 15 days is just the average of 12 and 18 – a common mistake.

⚡ Shortcut: Together time = (A × B)/(A + B) = (12 × 18)/(12 + 18) = 216/30 = 7.2 days.

ReasoningDirection sense2/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q2. Ravi walks 6 km towards the North, then turns right and walks 8 km. How far is he now from his starting point?

  1. 10 km
  2. 14 km
  3. 2 km
  4. 12 km
Show answer and explanation

(A) 10 km

Explanation: Turning right from North means he walks East. His two paths make a right angle, so the distance = √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10 km. 14 km is the total distance walked, not the straight-line distance.

⚡ Shortcut: 6, 8, 10 is the 3-4-5 triplet × 2, so the answer is 10 km straight away.

General ScienceChemistry: common compounds3/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q3. What is the chemical formula of baking soda?

  1. Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
  2. NaCl
  3. NaHCO₃
  4. CaOCl₂
Show answer and explanation

(C) NaHCO₃

Explanation: Baking soda is sodium hydrogen carbonate (sodium bicarbonate), NaHCO₃. Na₂CO₃·10H₂O is washing soda, NaCl is common salt and CaOCl₂ is bleaching powder.

HistoryModern India: Indian National Congress4/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q4. Who founded the Indian National Congress in 1885?

  1. W.C. Bonnerjee
  2. A.O. Hume
  3. Dadabhai Naoroji
  4. Surendranath Banerjee
Show answer and explanation

(B) A.O. Hume

Explanation: Allan Octavian Hume, a retired British civil servant, founded the Congress in 1885. Its first session was held in Bombay (December 1885) with 72 delegates and was presided over by W.C. Bonnerjee – so Bonnerjee was the first President, not the founder.

GeographyIndian rivers5/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q5. Which is the longest river of peninsular India?

  1. Krishna
  2. Narmada
  3. Mahanadi
  4. Godavari
Show answer and explanation

(D) Godavari

Explanation: The Godavari (about 1,465 km) rises at Trimbakeshwar near Nashik (Maharashtra) and falls into the Bay of Bengal. The Krishna (about 1,400 km) is second; the Narmada (about 1,312 km) is the longest west-flowing river; the Mahanadi is shorter (about 851 km).
