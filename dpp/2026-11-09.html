Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q1. एक दुकानदार किसी वस्तु पर क्रय मूल्य से 25% अधिक मूल्य अंकित करता है और 10% की छूट देता है। उसका लाभ प्रतिशत क्या है?

  1. 15%
  2. 12.5%
  3. 10%
  4. 13.5%
उत्तर और व्याख्या देखें

(B) 12.5%

व्याख्या: मान लीजिए क्रय मूल्य = 100 है। तब अंकित मूल्य = 125 और विक्रय मूल्य = 125 × 0.9 = 112.5 होगा। इसलिए लाभ = 12.5%।

⚡ शॉर्टकट ट्रिक: 25 − 10 − (25 × 10)/100 = 12.5%।

रीज़निंगसंख्या श्रृंखला2/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q2. दी गई श्रृंखला में अगला पद ज्ञात कीजिए: 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ?

  1. 46
  2. 47
  3. 48
  4. 49
उत्तर और व्याख्या देखें

(C) 48

व्याख्या: श्रृंखला का हर पद n² − 1 है: 2²−1 = 3, 3²−1 = 8, 4²−1 = 15, 5²−1 = 24, 6²−1 = 35। इसलिए अगला पद 7² − 1 = 48 है।

⚡ शॉर्टकट ट्रिक: अंतर 5, 7, 9, 11 हैं, इसलिए अगला अंतर 13 होगा: 35 + 13 = 48।

MathsPercentage / Profit and loss1/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q1. A shopkeeper marks an article 25% above its cost price and gives a discount of 10%. What is his profit percentage?

  1. 15%
  2. 12.5%
  3. 10%
  4. 13.5%
Show answer and explanation

(B) 12.5%

Explanation: Let the cost price = 100. Marked price = 125, selling price = 125 × 0.9 = 112.5. So the profit is 12.5%.

⚡ Shortcut: Net % = 25 − 10 − (25 × 10)/100 = 12.5%.

ReasoningNumber series2/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q2. Find the next term in the series: 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ?

  1. 46
  2. 47
  3. 48
  4. 49
Show answer and explanation

(C) 48

Explanation: Each term is n² − 1: 2²−1 = 3, 3²−1 = 8, 4²−1 = 15, 5²−1 = 24, 6²−1 = 35. So the next term is 7² − 1 = 48.

⚡ Shortcut: The differences are 5, 7, 9, 11, so the next difference is 13: 35 + 13 = 48.

General SciencePhysics: units3/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q3. What is the SI unit of power?

  1. Watt
  2. Joule
  3. Newton
  4. Pascal
Show answer and explanation

(A) Watt

Explanation: Power = work ÷ time, so 1 watt = 1 joule per second. Joule is the unit of work or energy, newton of force and pascal of pressure.

  2. Madras – Arakkonam

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q5. Which Article of the Indian Constitution lists the Fundamental Duties?

  1. Article 21A
  2. Article 51A
  3. Article 32
  4. Article 14
Show answer and explanation

(B) Article 51A

Explanation: Fundamental Duties were added in Part IV-A by the 42nd Amendment (1976); there are now 11 of them. Article 21A is the right to education, 32 the right to constitutional remedies and 14 the right to equality.
