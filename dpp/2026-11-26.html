गणितक्षेत्रमिति (आयत)1/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q1. एक आयत की लंबाई और चौड़ाई का अनुपात 5 : 3 है और उसका परिमाप 64 सेमी है। आयत का क्षेत्रफल क्या है?

  1. 192 सेमी²
  2. 256 सेमी²
  3. 240 सेमी²
  4. 160 सेमी²
उत्तर और व्याख्या देखें

(C) 240 सेमी²

व्याख्या: मान लीजिए लंबाई = 5x और चौड़ाई = 3x। परिमाप = 2(5x + 3x) = 16x = 64, इसलिए x = 4। लंबाई = 20 सेमी, चौड़ाई = 12 सेमी, क्षेत्रफल = 20 × 12 = 240 सेमी²। 256 सेमी² वह ग़लती है जिसमें इसे 64 ÷ 4 = 16 सेमी भुजा वाला वर्ग मान लिया जाता है।

⚡ शॉर्टकट ट्रिक: आधा परिमाप = 32 = 5 + 3 = 8 भाग, इसलिए 1 भाग = 4। भुजाएँ = 20 और 12 → क्षेत्रफल = 240 सेमी²।

रीज़निंगसंख्या श्रृंखला (×2 − 1)2/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q2. श्रृंखला में अगली संख्या ज्ञात कीजिए: 3, 5, 9, 17, 33, ?

  1. 49
  2. 57
  3. 66
  4. 65
उत्तर और व्याख्या देखें

(D) 65

व्याख्या: हर पद = पिछला पद × 2 − 1: 3 × 2 − 1 = 5, 5 × 2 − 1 = 9, 9 × 2 − 1 = 17, 17 × 2 − 1 = 33, इसलिए 33 × 2 − 1 = 65। 66 वह ग़लती है जिसमें 33 को सिर्फ़ दोगुना किया जाता है, और 49 में 32 की जगह फिर से 16 जोड़ दिया जाता है।

⚡ शॉर्टकट ट्रिक: अंतर 2, 4, 8, 16 हैं (हर बार दोगुना), इसलिए अगला अंतर 32 होगा: 33 + 32 = 65।

Q5. भारत में हर साल 26 नवंबर को संविधान दिवस मनाया जाता है, क्योंकि 1949 में इसी दिन:

  1. भारत का संविधान लागू हुआ था
  2. संविधान सभा की पहली बैठक हुई थी

(C) संविधान सभा ने भारत के संविधान को अपनाया था

MathsMensuration (rectangle)1/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q1. The length and breadth of a rectangle are in the ratio 5 : 3 and its perimeter is 64 cm. What is its area?

  1. 192 cm²
  2. 256 cm²
  3. 240 cm²
  4. 160 cm²
Show answer and explanation

(C) 240 cm²

Explanation: Let length = 5x and breadth = 3x. Perimeter = 2(5x + 3x) = 16x = 64, so x = 4. Length = 20 cm, breadth = 12 cm, area = 20 × 12 = 240 cm². 256 cm² is the mistake of treating it as a square of side 64 ÷ 4 = 16 cm.

⚡ Shortcut: Half the perimeter = 32 = 5 + 3 = 8 parts, so 1 part = 4. Sides = 20 and 12 → area = 240 cm².

ReasoningNumber series (×2 − 1)2/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q2. Find the next number in the series: 3, 5, 9, 17, 33, ?

  1. 49
  2. 57
  3. 66
  4. 65
Show answer and explanation

(D) 65

Explanation: Each term = previous term × 2 − 1: 3 × 2 − 1 = 5, 5 × 2 − 1 = 9, 9 × 2 − 1 = 17, 17 × 2 − 1 = 33, so 33 × 2 − 1 = 65. 66 is the mistake of only doubling 33, and 49 comes from adding 16 again instead of 32.

⚡ Shortcut: The differences are 2, 4, 8, 16 (doubling), so the next difference is 32: 33 + 32 = 65.

General ScienceChemistry: acids in nature3/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q3. The burning pain of an ant sting is caused mainly by which acid?

  1. Formic acid (methanoic acid)
  2. Acetic acid
  3. Citric acid
  4. Lactic acid
Show answer and explanation

(A) Formic acid (methanoic acid)

Explanation: Ants inject formic acid (methanoic acid, HCOOH) when they sting; it is also found in nettle leaves. Rubbing a mild base such as baking soda neutralises it. Acetic acid is in vinegar, citric acid in lemons and oranges, and lactic acid in curd and sour milk.

Q4. The Durand Cup, one of the oldest tournaments of its kind in Asia, is associated with which sport?

  1. Hockey
  2. Football
  3. Cricket
  4. Golf
Show answer and explanation

(B) Football

Explanation: The Durand Cup is a football tournament first held in 1888, named after Sir Henry Mortimer Durand. It is Asia's oldest club football tournament. Hockey trophies include the Beighton Cup, and cricket the Ranji Trophy.

PolityConstitution Day (26 November)5/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q5. Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) is observed in India every year on 26 November because on this day in 1949:

  1. the Constitution of India came into force
  2. the Constituent Assembly held its first meeting
  3. the Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution of India
  4. India became a Republic
Show answer and explanation

(C) the Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution of India

Explanation: On 26 November 1949 the Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution; it came into force on 26 January 1950, when India became a Republic (Republic Day). The Assembly first met on 9 December 1946. The Government has observed 26 November as Constitution Day since 2015 (earlier it was marked as National Law Day).
