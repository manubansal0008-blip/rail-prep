गणितचक्रवृद्धि ब्याज1/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q1. ₹10,000 पर 10% वार्षिक दर से 3 वर्ष का चक्रवृद्धि ब्याज (वार्षिक संयोजन) कितना होगा?

  1. ₹3,000
  2. ₹3,310
  3. ₹3,100
  4. ₹3,410
उत्तर और व्याख्या देखें

(B) ₹3,310

व्याख्या: मिश्रधन = 10,000 × (1.1)³ = 10,000 × 1.331 = ₹13,310। चक्रवृद्धि ब्याज = 13,310 − 10,000 = ₹3,310। ₹3,000 साधारण ब्याज है (10,000 × 10 × 3 / 100), जो आम ग़लती है।

⚡ शॉर्टकट ट्रिक: ₹10,000 पर 10% से हर साल का ब्याज: 1,000 + 1,100 + 1,210 = ₹3,310 (हर साल पिछले साल से 10% ज़्यादा)।

रीज़निंगरक्त संबंध2/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q2. एक लड़के की ओर इशारा करते हुए रीता ने कहा, "यह मेरे दादा के इकलौते बेटे का बेटा है।" लड़के का रीता से क्या संबंध है?

  1. भाई
  2. चचेरा भाई
  3. चाचा
  4. भतीजा
उत्तर और व्याख्या देखें

(A) भाई

व्याख्या: रीता के दादा का इकलौता बेटा रीता के पिता हैं। रीता के पिता का बेटा रीता का भाई है। 'चचेरा भाई' ग़लत है, क्योंकि चचेरा भाई चाचा का बेटा होता है और दादा का केवल एक ही बेटा है।

⚡ शॉर्टकट ट्रिक: पीछे से हल करें: दादा का इकलौता बेटा = पिता → पिता का बेटा = भाई।

सामान्य विज्ञानभौतिकी: प्रकाश (गोलीय दर्पण)3/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q3. कार और मोटरसाइकिल में पीछे देखने वाले (साइड) दर्पण के रूप में किसका उपयोग होता है?

  1. समतल दर्पण
  2. अवतल दर्पण
  3. उत्तल लेंस
  4. उत्तल दर्पण

(D) उत्तल दर्पण

MathsCompound interest1/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q1. What is the compound interest on ₹10,000 for 3 years at 10% per annum, compounded annually?

  1. ₹3,000
  2. ₹3,310
  3. ₹3,100
  4. ₹3,410
Show answer and explanation

(B) ₹3,310

Explanation: Amount = 10,000 × (1.1)³ = 10,000 × 1.331 = ₹13,310. Compound interest = 13,310 − 10,000 = ₹3,310. ₹3,000 is the simple interest (10,000 × 10 × 3 / 100), the common mistake.

⚡ Shortcut: Year-wise interest on ₹10,000 at 10%: 1,000 + 1,100 + 1,210 = ₹3,310 (each year 10% more than the last).

ReasoningBlood relation2/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q2. Pointing to a boy, Rita said, "He is the son of the only son of my grandfather." How is the boy related to Rita?

  1. Brother
  2. Cousin
  3. Uncle
  4. Nephew
Show answer and explanation

(A) Brother

Explanation: The only son of Rita's grandfather is Rita's father. The son of Rita's father is Rita's brother. 'Cousin' is wrong because a cousin is the child of an uncle or aunt, and the grandfather has only one son.

⚡ Shortcut: Solve from the end: grandfather's only son = father → father's son = brother.

General SciencePhysics: light (spherical mirrors)3/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q3. Which of the following is used as a rear-view (side) mirror in cars and motorcycles?

  1. Plane mirror
  2. Concave mirror
  3. Convex lens
  4. Convex mirror
Show answer and explanation

(D) Convex mirror

Explanation: A convex mirror always forms a virtual, erect and diminished image and gives a much wider field of view, so the driver can see more of the traffic behind. A concave mirror is used in torches, headlights and by dentists; a plane mirror gives a small field of view; a lens is not a mirror.

RailwaysIndian Railways: Chenab Rail Bridge4/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q4. The Chenab Rail Bridge, the world's highest railway arch bridge, is located in which district of Jammu and Kashmir?

  1. Udhampur
  2. Anantnag
  3. Reasi
  4. Ramban
Show answer and explanation

(C) Reasi

Explanation: The Chenab Bridge, between Bakkal and Kauri, is in Reasi district. Its arch rises 359 m above the river bed and it is part of the Udhampur–Srinagar–Baramulla Rail Link (USBRL); PM Modi inaugurated it on 6 June 2025. The Chenab also flows through Ramban, a common trap.

EconomyIndian currency: ₹1 note5/5

Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)

Q5. Whose signature appears on the ₹1 currency note in India?

  1. Governor of the Reserve Bank of India
  2. Finance Secretary, Government of India
  3. Union Finance Minister
  4. Deputy Governor of the RBI
Show answer and explanation

(B) Finance Secretary, Government of India

Explanation: The ₹1 note is issued by the Government of India (Ministry of Finance), not by the RBI, so it carries the signature of the Finance Secretary. All other banknotes (₹2 and above) are issued by the RBI and signed by the RBI Governor.
