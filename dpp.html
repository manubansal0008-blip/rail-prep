Daily Practice Problems (DPPs)

5 new practice questions every day in the pattern of previous RRB NTPC and Group D papers: Maths, Reasoning, General Science and General Knowledge. Pick an answer to see if it is right, with an explanation.

These are Rail Prep's own practice questions written in the pattern of previous RRB papers (marked "Rail Prep practice (PYQ pattern)"). They are not real RRB past-paper questions. For real past papers with the official answer key see Past Papers.
