L&T delivers 'Samarthak', Navy's first indigenous Multi-Purpose Vessel

Larsen & Toubro (L&T) delivered Samarthak, the first indigenous Multi-Purpose Vessel (MPV) of the Indian Navy, on 27 August 2026. Built at L&T's Kattupalli shipyard with over 75% indigenous content, the ship will be used for trials and evaluation, maritime surveillance, offshore patrolling and Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (HADR). The name Samarthak means 'The Proponent'.

  • Navy Day is observed on 4 December, marking Operation Trident in the 1971 war.

Centre releases buffer onions at ₹35 a kg to check festive-season prices

The Department of Consumer Affairs said on 27 August 2026 that onion supply is adequate and that it is making a calibrated, targeted release of buffer stock to curb seasonal price rises. Buffer onions are being sold at ₹35 per kg through NCCF, NAFED, Safal and Kendriya Bhandar outlets. Against a 2026-27 target of 2.00 lakh tonnes of rabi onion for the Price Stabilisation Fund (PSF) buffer, about 1.21 lakh tonnes have been procured, and the Central Warehousing Corporation has been used for storage for the first time.

NPCIL to set up design unit to help private firms build heavy-water reactors

Nuclear Power Corporation of India Ltd (NPCIL) is setting up a new design vertical to give technical support to private companies and State governments entering nuclear power, the Indian Express reported on 27 August 2026. The unit will focus on India's indigenous Pressurised Heavy Water Reactor (PHWR) technology and offer design review and quality assurance. The move follows the SHANTI Act of December 2025, which opened the sector to private players; India's nuclear capacity is about 8.8 GWe against a target of 100 GWe by 2047.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • NPCIL, set up in 1987 and headquartered in Mumbai, is a public sector undertaking under the Department of Atomic Energy.
  • India's three-stage nuclear power programme, planned by Homi J. Bhabha, aims finally to use the country's large thorium reserves.

  4. Molten Salt Reactor (MSR)

Heatwave and lightning notified as natural calamities; States can use disaster funds

The Union Home Ministry has notified heatwaves and lightning as natural calamities, raising the list of notified disasters to 14 and letting States use their State Disaster Response Fund (SDRF) and State Disaster Mitigation Fund (SDMF) for them, The Hindu reported on 27 August 2026. The change follows the Sixteenth Finance Commission, which recommended ₹2.04 lakh crore for State disaster funds for 2026-27 to 2030-31. Earlier, heatwave spending was treated as a 'local disaster' and capped at 10% of a State's yearly SDRF allocation.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) was set up under the Disaster Management Act, 2005 and is chaired by the Prime Minister.
  • The Finance Commission is set up under Article 280 of the Constitution; the Sixteenth Finance Commission was chaired by Arvind Panagariya.

  3. Heatwave and lightning
  4. Pest attack and hailstorm
Show answer

Prime Minister Narendra Modi spoke to Nepal's Prime Minister Balendra Shah and offered all assistance after a flash flood on the Bhotekoshi river in Rasuwa district on 26 August 2026, and India airlifted about 10 tonnes of relief material, including medicines, on an IAF C-130J aircraft. निजी कंपनियों को हेवी-वॉटर रिएक्टर बनाने में मदद के लिए NPCIL डिज़ाइन यूनिट बनाएगा

इंडियन एक्सप्रेस में 27 अगस्त 2026 को छपी ख़बर के अनुसार न्यूक्लियर पावर कॉरपोरेशन ऑफ़ इंडिया लिमिटेड (NPCIL) एक नया डिज़ाइन विभाग बना रहा है, जो परमाणु बिजली क्षेत्र में आने वाली निजी कंपनियों और राज्य सरकारों को तकनीकी मदद देगा। यह यूनिट भारत की स्वदेशी प्रेशराइज़्ड हेवी वॉटर रिएक्टर (PHWR) तकनीक पर ध्यान देगी और डिज़ाइन समीक्षा व गुणवत्ता आश्वासन में सहायता करेगी। यह कदम दिसंबर 2025 के SHANTI अधिनियम के बाद उठाया गया है, जिसने इस क्षेत्र को निजी कंपनियों के लिए खोला; भारत की परमाणु क्षमता करीब 8.8 GWe है और 2047 तक 100 GWe का लक्ष्य है।

  • होमी जे. भाभा द्वारा तैयार भारत के तीन-चरणीय परमाणु कार्यक्रम का अंतिम लक्ष्य देश के बड़े थोरियम भंडार का उपयोग करना है।

छोटा सवाल: NPCIL की नई डिज़ाइन यूनिट (अगस्त 2026) निजी कंपनियों को मुख्य रूप से किस रिएक्टर तकनीक में मदद देगी?

  2. फ़ास्ट ब्रीडर रिएक्टर (FBR)
  3. बॉयलिंग वॉटर रिएक्टर (BWR)
  4. मोल्टन सॉल्ट रिएक्टर (MSR)
उत्तर देखें

(A) प्रेशराइज़्ड हेवी वॉटर रिएक्टर (PHWR) यह यूनिट परमाणु बिजली में आने वाली निजी कंपनियों और राज्यों को भारत की स्वदेशी PHWR तकनीक देगी।
