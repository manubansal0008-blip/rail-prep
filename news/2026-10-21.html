Police Commemoration Day: Amit Shah pays tribute at National Police Memorial

Union Home Minister Amit Shah paid homage to police martyrs at the National Police Memorial, Chanakyapuri, New Delhi, on Police Commemoration Day, 21 October 2026. The names of 154 police personnel who died on duty in the past year were added, and the memorial now honours 36,852 police personnel. He also flagged off a 'Fight Against Drugs' motorcycle rally that will reach Ekta Nagar, Gujarat, for the Ekta Parade on 31 October.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • On 21 October 1959, 10 policemen were killed in an ambush by Chinese troops at Hot Springs, Ladakh; the day has been observed every year since.
  • The Prime Minister dedicated the National Police Memorial in New Delhi to the nation on Police Commemoration Day 2018.

Show answer

(C) Hot Springs, Ladakh On 21 October 1959, 10 policemen were killed in a Chinese ambush at Hot Springs, Ladakh. Police Commemoration Day is observed on 21 October in their memory.

Financial inclusion saturation campaign begins, runs till 31 December

A nationwide financial inclusion saturation campaign of the Department of Financial Services runs from 21 October to 31 December 2026. It covers 2.70 lakh Gram Panchayats and Urban Local Bodies and aims to enrol every eligible person in PM Jan Dhan Yojana, PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana, PM Suraksha Bima Yojana and Atal Pension Yojana. Camps will also do re-KYC of accounts, update nominations and spread awareness about digital fraud and unclaimed deposits.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • PM Jan Dhan Yojana was launched on 28 August 2014.
  • PMJJBY (life cover), PMSBY (accident cover) and Atal Pension Yojana were launched together on 9 May 2015.

Quick question: Which of these schemes is NOT one of the four covered by the financial inclusion saturation campaign (21 Oct–31 Dec 2026)?

  1. PM Mudra Yojana
  2. PM Jan Dhan Yojana
  3. Atal Pension Yojana
  4. PM Suraksha Bima Yojana
Show answer

(A) PM Mudra Yojana The campaign covers PMJDY, PMJJBY, PMSBY and Atal Pension Yojana. PM Mudra Yojana is not one of the four.

Source: PIB (Ministry of Finance), 21 Oct 2026

SIMBEX-2026 between India and Singapore begins at Visakhapatnam

The 33rd edition of the Singapore-India Maritime Bilateral Exercise (SIMBEX-2026) began at Visakhapatnam on 21 October 2026 and runs till 26 October. Singapore's ship RSS Supreme arrived for the exercise, and the opening ceremony was held on board INS Nilgiri and INS Kamorta. The harbour phase is on 21–23 October and the sea phase in the Bay of Bengal on 24–26 October.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • SIMBEX began in 1994 as 'Exercise Lion King'.
  • Visakhapatnam is the headquarters of the Indian Navy's Eastern Naval Command.

Show answer

(B) Exercise Lion King SIMBEX began in 1994 as Exercise Lion King. Its 33rd edition, SIMBEX-2026, began at Visakhapatnam on 21 October 2026.

International4/5

India to host first SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum, says Rajnath Singh in Dushanbe

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh represented India at the 25th meeting of the SCO Council of Heads of Government in Dushanbe, Tajikistan, on 20 October 2026. He announced that India will host the first SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum by the end of 2026, an idea proposed by PM Narendra Modi at the 2025 Tianjin summit. The meeting adopted a joint communique covering the SCO budget for 2027 and an action plan for 2026–2030 on trade and economic cooperation.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The SCO was set up in 2001; India became a full member in 2017. Its secretariat is in Beijing.
  • The SCO's Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure (RATS) is based in Tashkent, Uzbekistan.

  4. Dushanbe

Asian Para Games: Simran and Deepthi Jeevanji win gold with Games records

At the Asian Para Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, on 20 October 2026, Simran won the women's 200m T12 gold in 24.80 seconds and Deepthi Jeevanji won the women's 400m T20 gold in 56.36 seconds, both Games records. Pranav Soorma won the men's discus F51/52/53 gold with an Asian record, and Devender Kumar led an Indian sweep in the men's discus F44/64. India's tally rose to 35 medals: 9 gold, 11 silver and 15 bronze.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The first Asian Para Games were held in Guangzhou, China, in 2010.
  • Deepthi Jeevanji won bronze in the 400m T20 at the Paris 2024 Paralympics.

  • SCO का क्षेत्रीय आतंकवाद-रोधी ढाँचा (RATS) उज़्बेकिस्तान के ताशकंद में है।
