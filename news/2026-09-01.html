Navy commissions INS Nipun, second Nistar-class diving support vessel, in Mumbai

The Indian Navy commissioned INS Nipun at the Naval Dockyard, Mumbai, on 31 August 2026 in the presence of Chief of the Naval Staff Admiral Krishna Swaminathan. Built by Hindustan Shipyard Limited (HSL), Visakhapatnam, it is the second Diving Support Vessel of the Nistar class and will serve under the Western Naval Command. The ship supports saturation, air and heliox diving and can operate the Navy's Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicle (DSRV) for submarine rescue; its motto is 'Far Beneath, Few Beyond'.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The first ship of the class, INS Nistar, is based at Visakhapatnam on the east coast; INS Nipun gives the west coast its own deep-diving and submarine rescue platform.
  • Hindustan Shipyard Limited is a Defence PSU at Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh; the Western Naval Command has its headquarters in Mumbai.

(C) Nistar class INS Nipun is the second Nistar-class Diving Support Vessel; INS Nistar, the first, is based at Visakhapatnam.

Gross GST collection rises 14.8% to ₹1.99 lakh crore in August

Gross GST collection in August 2026 rose 14.8% year-on-year to about ₹1.99 lakh crore, according to data released on 1 September 2026. Net collection after refunds was ₹1.68 lakh crore, up 8.3%, while refunds jumped 68% to ₹31,795 crore. GST on imports grew 29% to ₹62,604 crore and domestic revenue rose 9.3% to over ₹1.37 lakh crore.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • GST came into force on 1 July 2017 through the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016.
  • The GST Council (Article 279A) is chaired by the Union Finance Minister; its 57th meeting was scheduled for 12 September 2026 in New Delhi.

Bihar and Jharkhand sign MoU on sharing Sone river water

Bihar and Jharkhand signed an MoU on sharing the waters of the Sone river in New Delhi on 31 August 2026 in the presence of Union Home Minister Amit Shah, ending a dispute of nearly 25 years. Jharkhand Chief Minister Hemant Soren, Bihar Chief Minister Samrat Choudhary and Jal Shakti Minister C.R. Patil were present. The pact will provide irrigation and drinking water to districts such as Bhojpur, Buxar, Rohtas, Kaimur, Aurangabad, Arwal, Gaya and Patna in Bihar and Palamu and Garhwa in Jharkhand.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Sone rises near Amarkantak in Madhya Pradesh and joins the Ganga near Patna; the Indrapuri (Sone) Barrage in Rohtas district was commissioned in 1968.
  • Jharkhand was carved out of Bihar on 15 November 2000; Amit Shah called this the fourth inter-state water agreement of 2026.

Centre notifies Semicon 2.0 scheme with ₹1.27 lakh crore outlay

The Union government notified the Semicon India Programme 2.0 with an outlay of ₹1.27 lakh crore on 31 August 2026, Electronics and IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw announced in New Delhi. The scheme has six pillars covering 10 categories and gives fiscal and infrastructure support across the chip value chain, from design and wafer fabrication to packaging, equipment, materials, R&D and talent. The India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) is the nodal agency, and applications will be accepted for three years.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The first Semicon India Programme and the India Semiconductor Mission were launched in December 2021 with an outlay of ₹76,000 crore.
  • The Union Cabinet approved Semicon 2.0 in July 2026; the scheme comes under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).

Science / Space5/5

C-DOT unveils 14 indigenous quantum-safe communication products

The Centre for Development of Telematics (C-DOT) unveiled 14 indigenously developed quantum products on its 43rd Foundation Day in New Delhi on 31 August 2026. They include Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) systems such as Q-AKSHAY and Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) encryptors such as Q-SETU, Q-MAHASETU, Q-AMOGH (up to 200 Gbps) and the defence-grade Q-PARAKRAM. The products aim to protect India's telecom and defence networks from future attacks by quantum computers.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • C-DOT, set up in 1984, is the telecom R&D centre of the Department of Telecommunications, Ministry of Communications.
  • The National Quantum Mission was approved in 2023 with an outlay of ₹6,003.65 crore for 2023–24 to 2030–31.

  4. 14
