80th Independence Day: Vande Mataram sung at Red Fort for the first time; PM gives 'Sapt Dhara' call

India celebrated its 80th Independence Day on 15 August 2026. For the first time, the national song Vande Mataram was sung at the Red Fort ceremony, marking its 150 years, and this year's celebrations honoured Yuva Shakti (youth power). In his 13th Independence Day speech, PM Narendra Modi gave a call for 'Sapt Dhara', seven streams of strength for Viksit Bharat by 2047: manufacturing, agriculture, technology, Gati Shakti, defence, green and blue economy, and soft power.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Vande Mataram was written by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay and appears in his novel Anandamath.
  • The flag hoisting was synchronised with a 21-gun salute, and two Mi-17 helicopters of the IAF showered flower petals.

  4. Jana Gana Mana
Show answer

(B) Vande Mataram For the first time since Independence, the national song Vande Mataram was sung at the Red Fort on 15 August, marking its 150 years.

Hockey World Cup: India men beat Wales 3-1 in their opener on Independence Day

The Indian men's hockey team began its FIH Hockey World Cup campaign with a 3-1 win over Wales in a Pool D match at Amstelveen, Netherlands, on 15 August 2026. Captain Harmanpreet Singh scored two goals from penalty corners and Sanjay Rana scored one, while Sam Welsh scored the only goal for Wales. The men's and women's World Cups are being held together in Belgium and the Netherlands.

Devdutt Padikkal hits maiden Test century as India reach 288/2 in Galle

Devdutt Padikkal scored his maiden Test century, remaining unbeaten on 131 off 178 balls, as India reached 288 for 2 on the opening day of the first Test against Sri Lanka at the Galle International Stadium on 15 August 2026. Batting at number three, he shared a 150-run stand with K.L. Rahul, who retired hurt on 77 with cramps. Captain Shubman Gill won the toss and chose to bat; Yashasvi Jaiswal was run out for 32.

Karnataka to teach AI from Class 6 and set up India's first government-owned AI university

In his Independence Day address in Bengaluru on 15 August 2026, Karnataka Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar announced that Artificial Intelligence (AI) will be taught from Class 6 in all schools of the state, and launched the AI Akshara Abhiyan and Coding Gurukula. He said the country's first government-owned AI university will come up in Bengaluru. He also set a goal of a three-trillion-dollar state economy by 2047 and announced filling 72,000 vacant government posts.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Chief Minister hoisted the national flag at the Field Marshal Manekshaw Parade Ground in Bengaluru.
  • Bengaluru, the capital of Karnataka, is often called the Silicon Valley of India.

'Suryapath Tiranga' global relay hoists the Tricolour across 21 countries

Under the Har Ghar Tiranga campaign, the 'Suryapath Tiranga' Global Relay on 15 August 2026 carried the Tricolour around the world along the path of the rising sun. It began at 1:30 am IST in Suva, Fiji, and moved westward through Indian missions in New Zealand, Australia, Japan, South-East Asia and South Asia, ending in Qatar at 10:30 am IST. In about nine hours the flag was hoisted at missions and posts in 21 countries.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Suva is the capital of Fiji, a Pacific island nation with a large community of Indian origin.
  • The Har Ghar Tiranga campaign was started in 2022 under Azadi Ka Amrit Mahotsav.
