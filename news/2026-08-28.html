Indian women beat Germany 3-1 to finish fifth at Hockey World Cup, best since 1974

The Indian women's hockey team beat Germany 3-1 in the fifth-place classification match of the FIH Women's World Cup at Amstelveen, Netherlands, on 27 August 2026. Ishika (43rd minute) and Navneet Kaur (44th and 47th) scored for India, while Lynn Krings replied for Germany. Captain Salima Tete's side, coached by Sjoerd Marijne and with 11 World Cup debutants, recorded India's best finish since fourth place in the first World Cup in 1974.

Defence2/5

Rajnath Singh lays foundation of ₹472-crore tank overhaul facility at Jabalpur

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh laid the foundation stone of a ₹472-crore tank overhaul facility at Vehicle Factory Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh, in the presence of Chief Minister Mohan Yadav, The Hindu reported on 28 August 2026. The facility will overhaul 80 T-72 (Ajeya) and T-90 (Bhishma) tanks a year. The Army needs about 230 tank overhauls a year, of which the Heavy Vehicles Factory, Avadi, handles 150.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Vehicle Factory Jabalpur is a unit of Armoured Vehicles Nigam Limited (AVNL), headquartered at Avadi, Chennai.
  • AVNL is one of the seven Defence PSUs formed from the Ordnance Factory Board in October 2021.

Economy / Banking3/5

PM Jan Dhan Yojana completes 12 years with 59.09 crore accounts

The Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), India's financial inclusion mission, completed 12 years on 28 August 2026. As of 19 August 2026, 59.09 crore accounts had been opened, 32.92 crore of them belonging to women, and deposits had grown from ₹15,670 crore in March 2015 to ₹3,16,514 crore. Account holders get a zero-balance account, a RuPay debit card with ₹2 lakh accident insurance (for accounts opened after August 2018) and an overdraft of up to ₹10,000.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • PMJDY was launched on 28 August 2014 and is run by the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance.
  • The RuPay card network is run by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), headquartered in Mumbai.

International4/5

WHO declares Uganda free of Ebola after 42 days without a new case

The World Health Organization (WHO) declared Uganda free of Ebola after 42 days passed without a new case, The Hindu reported on 28 August 2026. The 42-day wait equals two maximum incubation periods of Ebola Virus Disease. The outbreak had also affected parts of neighbouring Democratic Republic of Congo.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The WHO was set up on 7 April 1948, the date now observed as World Health Day; its headquarters is in Geneva, Switzerland.
  • Kampala is the capital of Uganda; the Ebola virus is named after the Ebola river in the Democratic Republic of Congo.

  2. 42 days
  3. 60 days
  4. 90 days

(B) 42 days WHO waits 42 days, two maximum incubation periods of Ebola, before declaring an outbreak over.

Panchayati Raj Ministry's DAANVEER drive invites public to donate computers to Gram Panchayats

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj has called for public participation in DAANVEER, a people-led computer crowdsourcing initiative to strengthen digital infrastructure in Gram Panchayats, according to a release reported on 28 August 2026. Citizens can donate desktop computers directly to verified Gram Panchayats through the Meri Panchayat App and DigiHaat, an ONDC-enabled digital marketplace. As of 27 August 2026, 75 donations had been made.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions; National Panchayati Raj Day is observed on 24 April.
  • The Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC) is an initiative of the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT).
