National Sports Awards 2026: 17 sportspersons named for Arjuna Award

The National Sports Awards 2026 were announced on 18 August 2026, with 17 sportspersons selected for the Arjuna Award and no Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award this time. Nirmala Sitharaman chairs 3rd NICDIT apex meeting on industrial corridors

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman chaired the third meeting of the apex authority of the National Industrial Corridor Development and Implementation Trust (NICDIT) on 17 August 2026. The programme now has 20 projects across 13 States and 7 corridors, and four smart industrial cities – Dholera, Shendra-Bidkin, Greater Noida and Vikram Udyogpuri – are already in production. So far 469 plots covering about 5,348 acres have been allotted, with investment potential of about ₹2.21 lakh crore and employment potential of about 1.29 lakh.

📌 Remember for the exam

Earthquake swarm: 24 tremors recorded around Nashik and Palghar in Maharashtra

At least 24 tremors were recorded between 25 July and 12 August 2026 in Maharashtra, mainly around Nashik and Palghar districts, putting the spotlight on 'earthquake swarms'. A swarm is a cluster of quakes in a small area over a short time without one clear main shock. Possible causes include movement of underground fluids or magma, slow-slip events and stress on old faults; a swarm does not reliably predict a bigger earthquake.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • In India, earthquakes are monitored by the National Center for Seismology (NCS) under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.
  • Earlier earthquake swarms in Maharashtra have been recorded near Dahanu (Palghar) and in Nanded.
