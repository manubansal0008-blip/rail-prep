Indian Air Force celebrates its 94th anniversary

The Indian Air Force marked its 94th anniversary on Air Force Day, 8 October 2026, with a parade at Hindon Air Base in Ghaziabad. Chief of the Air Staff Air Chief Marshal A.P. Singh addressed the celebrations and called the IAF 'the custodian of the skies'. The theme was 'Indian Air Force – Infallible, Impervious and Precise', and the main aerial display is to be held over Varanasi.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Indian Air Force was set up on 8 October 1932; that is why Air Force Day is observed on 8 October.
  • The IAF motto 'Nabha Sparsham Deeptam' ('Touch the sky with glory') is taken from the Bhagavad Gita.

IISc's Prof. Mahesh Kakde wins the Ramanujan Prize 2026

Prof. Mahesh Kakde of the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, has won the 2026 Ramanujan Prize for young mathematicians from developing countries, as reported on 7 October 2026. He was honoured for his work in algebraic number theory, including results on the Gross–Stark and Brumer–Stark conjectures linked to Hilbert's 12th problem. He is the fifth Indian to win the prize, which carries 10,000 US dollars.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Ramanujan Prize was started in 2005; it is given by the ICTP (Trieste, Italy) with the International Mathematical Union and India's Department of Science and Technology.
  • Earlier Indian winners include Sujatha Ramdorai (2006), Amalendu Krishna (2015), Ritabrata Munshi (2018) and Neena Gupta (2021).

  2. Quantum physics
  3. Organic chemistry
  4. Astrophysics
Show answer

(A) Algebraic number theory Prof. Mahesh Kakde of IISc Bengaluru won the Ramanujan Prize 2026 for his contributions to algebraic number theory.

Defence3/5

Coast Guard holds 12th national search and rescue exercise SAREX-2026 off Paradip

The Indian Coast Guard conducted the 12th National Maritime Search and Rescue Exercise, SAREX-2026, off the Paradip coast in Odisha on 7–8 October 2026. The drill simulated a mass rescue after a passenger aircraft with 228 people ditched at sea, and involved 15 Coast Guard ships, Navy ships and IAF C-130J and Mi-17 aircraft. Its theme was 'Leveraging Technology to Strengthen SAR Preparedness and Maritime Safety', and 32 observers from 30 countries took part.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Indian Coast Guard was set up in 1977 and became a formal armed force under the Coast Guard Act, 1978; it works under the Ministry of Defence.
  • The Coast Guard is India's national coordinating authority for maritime search and rescue; its Director General is Paramesh Sivamani.

NCRTC and IGNCA sign MoU to bring art and heritage to Namo Bharat stations

The National Capital Region Transport Corporation (NCRTC) signed an MoU with the Indira Gandhi National Centre for the Arts (IGNCA) on 8 October 2026 to bring art, culture and heritage to Namo Bharat stations. Plans include art exhibitions, heritage showcases, literary events and workshops at stations on the Delhi–Meerut corridor. The corridor now carries about 1.1 lakh commuters a day and has recorded more than 4.5 crore journeys.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Delhi–Meerut Namo Bharat corridor is India's first Regional Rapid Transit System (RRTS); it is built and run by NCRTC.
  • IGNCA is an autonomous body under the Ministry of Culture, set up in 1987.

Government scheme5/5

JOSH teams launched to take the fight against TB to the community

Union Health Minister J.P. Nadda launched JOSH (Joint Squad for Health) teams under the TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 8 October 2026. The teams bring together officers, MY Bharat volunteers and NCC cadets to spread TB awareness and support patients. The first phase covers 55 districts in 11 States/UTs, with 6,347 JOSH squads, and is run by the Health Ministry with the Ministries of Youth Affairs & Sports and Defence.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Tuberculosis is caused by the bacterium Mycobacterium tuberculosis; World TB Day is observed on 24 March.
  • NCC (National Cadet Corps) works under the Ministry of Defence; MY Bharat is a platform of the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports.

IISc के प्रो. महेश काकडे को रामानुजन पुरस्कार 2026

भारतीय विज्ञान संस्थान (IISc), बेंगलुरु के प्रो. महेश काकडे को विकासशील देशों के युवा गणितज्ञों के लिए दिया जाने वाला 2026 का रामानुजन पुरस्कार मिला है; इसकी ख़बर 7 अक्टूबर 2026 को आई। उन्हें बीजीय संख्या सिद्धांत (algebraic number theory) में काम के लिए चुना गया, जिसमें हिल्बर्ट की 12वीं समस्या से जुड़े ग्रॉस–स्टार्क और ब्रूमर–स्टार्क अनुमानों पर उनके नतीजे शामिल हैं। यह पुरस्कार पाने वाले वे पाँचवें भारतीय हैं; इसमें 10,000 अमेरिकी डॉलर की राशि मिलती है।

  • पहले के भारतीय विजेताओं में सुजाता रामदोरई (2006), अमलेंदु कृष्णा (2015), रितब्रत मुंशी (2018) और नीना गुप्ता (2021) शामिल हैं।

नमो भारत स्टेशनों पर कला और विरासत के लिए NCRTC और IGNCA में समझौता

राष्ट्रीय राजधानी क्षेत्र परिवहन निगम (NCRTC) ने 8 अक्टूबर 2026 को इंदिरा गांधी राष्ट्रीय कला केंद्र (IGNCA) के साथ एक MoU पर हस्ताक्षर किए, ताकि नमो भारत स्टेशनों पर कला, संस्कृति और विरासत को यात्रियों तक पहुँचाया जा सके। योजना में दिल्ली–मेरठ कॉरिडोर के स्टेशनों पर कला प्रदर्शनियाँ, विरासत प्रदर्शन, साहित्यिक कार्यक्रम और कार्यशालाएँ शामिल हैं। इस कॉरिडोर पर अब रोज़ लगभग 1.1 लाख यात्री सफ़र करते हैं और अब तक 4.5 करोड़ से ज़्यादा यात्राएँ हो चुकी हैं।

  • IGNCA संस्कृति मंत्रालय के अधीन एक स्वायत्त संस्था है, जिसकी स्थापना 1987 में हुई थी।
