Tanvi Patri wins Youth Olympics badminton silver; first Indian woman in YOG singles final

Fifteen-year-old Odisha shuttler Tanvi Patri won silver in women's singles badminton at the Youth Olympic Games 2026 in Dakar, Senegal, on 5 November 2026. She lost the final to China's junior world champion Zhang Yi Xin 15–21, 21–13, 26–24 after taking the first game and fighting back from 10–19 down in the decider. Patri became the first Indian woman to reach a Youth Olympics badminton singles final and join past medallists HS Prannoy and Lakshya Sen.

Defence2/5

Indian Navy takes delivery of indigenous ASW shallow-water craft Amini from GRSE

Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers (GRSE), Kolkata, delivered the Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft (ASW SWC) Amini to the Indian Navy on 5 November 2026, the Defence Ministry said via PIB on 6 November 2026. Built to Indian Register of Shipping rules, Amini is among the Navy's largest waterjet-propelled warships and carries lightweight torpedoes, indigenous rocket launchers and sonars. Named after Amini Island in Lakshadweep, it has over 80% indigenous content and continues the legacy of the earlier Petya-class INS Amini decommissioned in 2002.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Amini was designed and built by GRSE, Kolkata, for anti-submarine and coastal roles.
  • The craft has over 80 percent indigenous content.

Ashwini Vaishnaw launches Semicon 2.0 portal; 12 more ECMS projects worth ₹7,503 crore approved

On 6 November 2026, Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw launched the Semicon 2.0 Web Application Portal for Pillars 2, 3 and 4 under the Semicon India Programme, and announced 12 further Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS) approvals. The batch projects ₹7,503 crore investment, ₹39,045 crore production and about 7,127 direct jobs across seven states. Science / Space5/5

PSA Office and DPIIT link Startup India with I-STEM for shared research equipment access

On 6 November 2026 the Office of the Principal Scientific Adviser and DPIIT launched API integration between the Startup India portal and the Indian Science, Technology and Engineering Facilities Map (I-STEM). Chaired by PSA Prof. Ajay Kumar Sood, the link lets DPIIT-recognised startups discover and request access to scientific testing facilities nationwide. As of that date I-STEM listed 31,540 equipment items across 4,044 registered institutions; Single Sign-On is planned in later phases while host labs still approve each request.
