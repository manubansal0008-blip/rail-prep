18th BRICS Summit held at Bharat Mandapam under India's chairship

India hosted the 18th BRICS Summit at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 12–13 September 2026, its fourth BRICS chairship, with the theme 'Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability'. Leaders including China's President Xi Jinping, on his first India visit since 2019, Russia's President Vladimir Putin and Iran's President Masoud Pezeshkian attended, and the summit ended with the adoption of the New Delhi Declaration. India's deliverables included a BRICS Startup Innovation Fund and a BRICS MSME Cooperation Portal; about 350 preparatory meetings were held during the year.

Hindi Diwas observed on 14 September

Hindi Diwas is observed across India on 14 September 2026 to promote the use of Hindi in government work and public life. The day marks 14 September 1949, when the Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi in the Devanagari script as the official language of the Union. Ministries, banks and offices hold Hindi fortnight (Hindi Pakhwada) programmes around the day.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Article 343 of the Constitution says the official language of the Union shall be Hindi in Devanagari script, with the international form of Indian numerals.
  • World Hindi Day (Vishwa Hindi Diwas) is a separate observance, held on 10 January.

Gandhinagar to host World Circular Economy Forum 2026, first in South Asia

India will host the World Circular Economy Forum (WCEF) 2026 at Gandhinagar, Gujarat, from 15 to 18 September 2026, the first time the forum is being held in South Asia, the government said on 13 September. The theme is 'Circular Economy: Transition for People and Prosperity', and more than 2,000 stakeholders from over 65 countries are expected. It is organised by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), Finland's innovation fund Sitra, the Government of Finland, the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, the Government of Gujarat and the Gujarat Pollution Control Board.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • A circular economy aims to keep materials in use through reuse, repair and recycling instead of the 'take-make-dispose' model.
  • The Central Pollution Control Board was set up in 1974 under the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act; Gandhinagar is the capital of Gujarat.
