Suruchi Singh and Kamaljeet win 10m air pistol mixed team gold at Asian Games

Suruchi Singh and Kamaljeet won gold in the 10m air pistol mixed team event at the 20th Asian Games in Nagoya, Japan, on 25 September 2026 with 484.6, an Asian Games record. Delhi Cabinet approves DURGA scheme for 1,300 pink e-autos for women and transgender persons

The Delhi Cabinet chaired by Chief Minister Rekha Gupta has approved the first phase of the DURGA (Driving Upliftment and Rozgar for Women/Transgender Green E-Auto) scheme, as reported on 25 September 2026. Under it, 1,300 pink e-autos will be given — 1,170 to women and 130 to transgender persons — with up to 90 for women and 10 for transgender beneficiaries in each revenue district. Women applicants must be Delhi residents aged 20–40 with family income up to ₹5 lakh, and the e-autos cannot be sold or transferred for three years.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Applicants need a voter ID, a light motor vehicle (LMV) licence and a PSV badge; applications are made online.
  • Delhi is the National Capital Territory (NCT) under Article 239AA of the Constitution.

BIS certification made mandatory for smartphone screen protectors from 1 April 2027

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has made Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) certification compulsory for smartphone screen protectors from 1 April 2027 under standard IS 19348:2025, as reported on 24–25 September 2026. Screen protectors become the 66th product category under the compulsory registration framework for electronics. The aim is to ensure that such products sold in India meet Indian quality and safety standards.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • BIS was set up as a statutory body in 1986, now works under the BIS Act, 2016, and is headquartered in New Delhi.
  • BIS comes under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution.

Antyodaya Diwas marks Pt Deendayal Upadhyaya's birth anniversary on 25 September

Antyodaya Diwas was observed on 25 September 2026, the 110th birth anniversary of Pandit Deendayal Upadhyaya, to highlight service to the poorest and most disadvantaged. 'Antyodaya' means the uplift of the last person in society. 25 September is also observed as World Pharmacists Day.
