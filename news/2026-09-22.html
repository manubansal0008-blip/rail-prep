India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement to come into force on 20 October 2026

Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal announced in New Delhi on 21 September 2026 that the India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement (FTA) will come into force on 20 October 2026. New Zealand will remove tariffs on 100% of Indian exports, helping textiles, leather, footwear, engineering goods, pharmaceuticals and processed foods, while India has kept dairy, sugar and edible oils out of its tariff concessions. New Zealand plans to invest US$20 billion in India, and the deal offers 5,000 temporary employment visas for skilled Indians and 1,000 working holiday visas a year for Indian youth.

  • Wellington is the capital of New Zealand and its currency is the New Zealand dollar.

ICICI Prudential Life Insurance renamed ICICI Life Insurance from 22 September

ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company changed its name to ICICI Life Insurance Company Limited with effect from 22 September 2026, after approval from the Registrar of Companies, Central Processing Centre, Ministry of Corporate Affairs. The change follows the restructuring of UK-based Prudential plc's India business: Prudential plans to buy a 75% stake in Bharti Life Insurance and is being reclassified from promoter to investor in ICICI Life. The company will inform IRDAI to get a revised certificate of registration.

World Rhino Day observed on 22 September with theme 'Keep the Five Alive'

World Rhino Day was observed on 22 September 2026 with the theme 'Keep the Five Alive', focusing on habitat protection, anti-poaching work and stopping the illegal trade in rhino horn. The five surviving species are the Sumatran, Javan, Black, Greater One-Horned and White rhinos, and all five are listed in Appendix I of CITES. Assam is home to about 80% of the world's Greater One-Horned rhinos, and Kaziranga National Park alone holds about 70% of them.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • World Rhino Day began as a WWF South Africa initiative in 2010 and was first celebrated internationally in 2011.
  • Kaziranga National Park in Assam is a UNESCO World Heritage Site (1985); the Greater One-Horned rhino is listed as Vulnerable by IUCN.
