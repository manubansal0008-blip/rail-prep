Defence Ministry signs ₹53,866.50 crore contracts for 8 Next Generation Corvettes

The Ministry of Defence signed contracts worth ₹53,866.50 crore on 2 November 2026 at Kartavya Bhawan-2, New Delhi, for eight Next Generation Corvettes (NGCs) for the Indian Navy, in the presence of Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh. Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE), Kolkata will build five ships and Goa Shipyard Limited (GSL) three. The warships are designed by the Indian Navy and will be used for anti-surface and anti-submarine warfare, coastal defence and maritime surveillance.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The project is expected to create about 72 lakh person-days of work in the shipyards and about 460 lakh person-days in ancillary industries and MSMEs over ten years.
  • GRSE is based in Kolkata and Goa Shipyard Limited at Vasco da Gama, Goa; both are Defence PSUs under the Ministry of Defence. Navy Day is observed on 4 December.

(A) Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE) GRSE, Kolkata will build five corvettes and Goa Shipyard Limited the remaining three, under contracts worth ₹53,866.50 crore.

India win men's 50m pistol team gold at ISSF World Championships in Doha

The Indian trio of Ravinder Singh, Saurabh Chaudhary and Amanpreet Singh won gold in the men's 50m pistol team event at the ISSF World Championships in Doha, Qatar, on 3 November 2026 with a total of 1659, ahead of China (1648) and South Korea. Ravinder Singh also won silver in the men's 50m pistol individual event. The women's 10m air rifle team of Elavenil Valarivan, Sonam Uttam Maskar and Shambhavi Shravan Kshirsagar took silver.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • ISSF (International Shooting Sport Federation) was founded in 1907 and has its headquarters in Munich, Germany.
  • Doha is the capital of Qatar; Russia's Anton Aristarkhov won the men's 50m pistol individual gold.

PM inaugurates Pune Metro Line 3 from Hinjewadi to Shivajinagar

Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 3 November 2026 inaugurated metro services between Hinjewadi and Shivajinagar in Pune (Maharashtra) and launched Pune Metro projects worth about ₹21,800 crore. The 23.3 km elevated Line 3 (Pink Line) has 23 stations and links the Hinjewadi IT hub with the District Court at Shivajinagar, with an end-to-end journey time of about 45 minutes. It was built under a public-private partnership (PPP) by Pune IT City Metro Rail Ltd, a joint venture of Tata Group's TRIL Urban Transport and Siemens.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Pune Metro Line 3 was the first contract awarded under the New Metro Rail Policy of 2017, on a 35-year design-build-finance-operate-transfer basis.
  • India's first metro rail service started in Kolkata in 1984.

Iliana Iotova becomes Bulgaria's first elected woman President

Iliana Iotova won Bulgaria's presidential runoff held on 1 November 2026 with 57.58% of the vote, defeating former interim Prime Minister Andrey Gyurov (39.47%), according to near-final results released on 2 November. She becomes the first woman to be elected President of Bulgaria. Iotova, a former Vice President, had been acting President since January 2026, when Rumen Radev resigned to contest the parliamentary election.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • In the first round on 25 October 2026, Iotova led with 34.6% and Gyurov got 32%, so a runoff was held.
  • Bulgaria's capital is Sofia; it joined the European Union in 2007 and adopted the euro on 1 January 2026.

Dinesh V. Ganvir takes charge as 55th Director General of Geological Survey of India

Dinesh Vitthalrao Ganvir has assumed charge as the 55th Director General of the Geological Survey of India (GSI), as reported on 1 November 2026. A geologist who joined GSI in 1994, he earlier served as Additional Director General and Head of the Central Region, Nagpur, and as National Mission Head-II. He said his priority will be stepping up exploration of critical and strategic minerals and preparing auction-ready mineral blocks.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • GSI was founded in 1851 and is headquartered in Kolkata; it works under the Union Ministry of Mines.
  • Before becoming DG, Ganvir worked on gold and diamond exploration in Maharashtra and on conservation studies of the Bhimbetka Rock Shelters and Ellora Caves.
