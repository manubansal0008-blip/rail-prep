Sagarmala Finance Corporation launches India's first blue bond

Sagarmala Finance Corporation Ltd (SMFCL) launched India's first blue bond on 28 September 2026. The 10-year bond aims to raise ₹600 crore with a green shoe option of ₹500 crore, and is rated AA+ by ICRA and CARE. The money will fund maritime lending, greenfield ports and coastal roads.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Blue bonds raise money for ocean and water-related projects; Seychelles issued the world's first sovereign blue bond in 2018.
  • SMFCL is a Mini Ratna (Category-I) CPSE under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, headquartered in New Delhi.

CJI Surya Kant opens India's first Victim Rights Centre in Patna

Chief Justice of India Surya Kant inaugurated India's first Victim Rights Centre at the Bihar State Legal Services Authority in Patna, as reported on 29 September 2026. It gives victims single-window legal, medical, psychological and social help, keeps case records so victims need not repeat statements, and tracks each case until relief is delivered. He also launched a transgender helpline under the SITARA scheme.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Article 39A of the Constitution (added by the 42nd Amendment, 1976) provides for free legal aid.
  • Legal services authorities work under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987; the CJI is the Patron-in-Chief of NALSA.
