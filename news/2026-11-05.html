Indian Railways records its highest-ever October freight loading of 145.07 million tonnes

Indian Railways loaded 145.07 million tonnes (MT) of freight in October 2026, its highest ever for the month and 8.32% more than the 133.93 MT of October 2025, the Ministry of Railways said on 4 November 2026. Domestic coal loading averaged 469.8 rakes a day, up 19.5%, while food grain loading grew 26.3%. Total loading for April–October 2026 reached 978.78 MT, up 4.67%, and passenger journeys in October rose to 64.72 crore from 63.52 crore.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Coal is the single largest commodity in Indian Railways' freight basket, mainly carried to power plants.
  • Dedicated Freight Corridors are built and run by DFCCIL (Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd), set up in 2006 under the Ministry of Railways.

Cabinet approves two-year extension for 75% of Agniveers

The Union Cabinet on 4 November 2026 approved an additional two-year engagement period, with higher pay, for the 75% of Agniveers who were due to leave after their four-year term. As before, 25% will be enrolled into the regular forces on merit; after the two-year extension, those willing and meeting the conditions can also be enrolled as regular soldiers. The decision comes as the first batch of Agniveers nears the end of its four-year tenure.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • Defence Minister Rajnath Singh called the extension a way to prepare the country for the changing nature of warfare.

Cabinet clears ₹14,500 crore VGF scheme for 92 GWh of grid-scale energy storage

The Union Cabinet chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 4 November 2026 approved a Viability Gap Funding (VGF) scheme of ₹14,500 crore to build 92 gigawatt-hours (GWh) of grid-scale energy storage between 2026-27 and 2030-31. The capacity will include 60 GWh of pumped storage, 30 GWh of battery energy storage systems (BESS) and 2 GWh of emerging technologies, attracting an estimated investment of about ₹97,600 crore. Battery projects must meet a minimum 40% domestic content requirement.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Energy storage helps supply solar and wind power round the clock; India is estimated to need 411 GWh of grid-scale storage by 2031-32.
  • Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said nearly 52% of India's power generation capacity now comes from non-fossil fuel sources.

  4. 30 GWh
Show answer

(A) 92 GWh The ₹14,500 crore scheme supports 92 GWh: 60 GWh pumped storage, 30 GWh BESS and 2 GWh emerging technologies.

Democrats win control of both houses of the US Congress in midterm elections

The Democratic Party won control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate in the United States midterm elections held on 3 November 2026, a setback for President Donald Trump's Republican Party. As counting continued on 4 November, Democrats had won 228 House seats and 54 Senate seats, with some races still undecided. Democrats flipped several Republican-held Senate seats, including Texas, where James Talarico defeated Ken Paxton.

Virat Kohli passes Sachin Tendulkar for most ODI runs in run chases as India beat New Zealand

Virat Kohli scored 74 off 51 balls to lead India to a five-wicket win over New Zealand in the rain-hit first ODI in Auckland on 4 November 2026. New Zealand were all out for 185, and India chased down the revised DLS target of 143 in 32 overs in just 22.4 overs; Arshdeep Singh took four wickets. With this innings Kohli has 8,741 runs in ODI run chases, overtaking Sachin Tendulkar's 8,720.

  • The DLS (Duckworth–Lewis–Stern) method is used to set revised targets in rain-affected limited-overs matches; India now lead the five-match series 1-0.

भारतीय रेलवे ने अक्टूबर में अब तक की सबसे ज़्यादा 145.07 मिलियन टन माल ढुलाई की

रेल मंत्रालय ने 4 नवंबर 2026 को बताया कि भारतीय रेलवे ने अक्टूबर 2026 में 145.07 मिलियन टन (MT) माल लदान किया, जो किसी भी अक्टूबर का अब तक का सबसे ऊँचा आँकड़ा है और अक्टूबर 2025 के 133.93 MT से 8.32% ज़्यादा है। घरेलू कोयले का लदान औसतन 469.8 रेक प्रतिदिन रहा, जो 19.5% अधिक है, जबकि खाद्यान्न लदान 26.3% बढ़ा। अप्रैल–अक्टूबर 2026 में कुल लदान 4.67% बढ़कर 978.78 MT पहुँच गया और अक्टूबर में यात्रियों की संख्या 63.52 करोड़ से बढ़कर 64.72 करोड़ हो गई।

  • डेडिकेटेड फ्रेट कॉरिडोर का निर्माण और संचालन DFCCIL (डेडिकेटेड फ्रेट कॉरिडोर कॉरपोरेशन ऑफ इंडिया लिमिटेड) करता है, जिसकी स्थापना 2006 में रेल मंत्रालय के अधीन हुई थी।
