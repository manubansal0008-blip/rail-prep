World Humanitarian Day and World Photography Day observed on 19 August

World Humanitarian Day was observed on 19 August 2026 to honour aid workers who help people caught in conflicts and disasters, and to highlight the growing risks they face. The same day is also marked as World Photography Day, celebrating photography as a medium of creativity, communication and historical record.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The UN General Assembly designated 19 August as World Humanitarian Day in 2008; it recalls the 19 August 2003 bombing of the UN office at the Canal Hotel in Baghdad, which killed 22 people including UN envoy Sergio Vieira de Mello.
  • World Photography Day recalls 19 August 1839, when the French government announced the daguerreotype photographic process as a free gift to the world.

Mahadayi dispute: Centre says no environmental clearance proposal pending for Kalasa-Banduri

The Mahadayi (Mhadei) river water dispute between Karnataka and Goa was back in the news in August 2026 after the Centre said no environmental clearance proposal for Karnataka's Kalasa-Banduri Nala project is pending. It had returned Karnataka's wildlife-clearance proposal in July 2026 because the matter is sub judice before the Supreme Court. Karnataka wants the project to supply drinking water to Belagavi, Dharwad, Gadag and Bagalkot, while Goa opposes it over downstream flow and ecology.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Mahadayi rises in Belagavi district of Karnataka and flows west into Goa, where it is called the Mandovi.
  • The Mahadayi Water Disputes Tribunal was set up under the Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956; its award was notified in 2018.

  3. Kali
  4. Mandovi

(D) Mandovi The Mahadayi rises in Belagavi, Karnataka, and is called the Mandovi in Goa.

Oil spill threatens Iran's Hara mangrove forest near Qeshm Island

An oil spill near Qeshm Island was reported in August 2026 to be threatening the Hara mangrove forests in Hormozgan Province of southern Iran. The forests lie around Qeshm Island and the Khuran Strait in the northern Persian Gulf and form the largest mangrove habitat in the Persian Gulf. The dominant species is the grey mangrove, Avicennia marina.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Hara protected area is a Ramsar wetland, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve and an Important Bird Area.
  • Mangroves protect coasts from storms, serve as fish nurseries and store 'blue carbon'; India's largest mangrove forest is the Sundarbans in West Bengal.

(B) Iran Hara lies in Hormozgan Province of southern Iran, around Qeshm Island in the Persian Gulf.

Science / Space4/5

NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope readied for launch

NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope was in the news in August 2026 ahead of its scheduled launch on 30 August 2026 aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy from Kennedy Space Center, Florida. It will operate from the Sun–Earth L2 point, about 1.5 million km from Earth, on a mission of about five years to study dark energy, dark matter, exoplanets and the evolution of galaxies. Its Wide Field Instrument has a field of view at least 100 times larger than that of the Hubble telescope.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The telescope is named after Nancy Grace Roman, NASA's first chief astronomer.
  • The James Webb Space Telescope also operates around the Sun–Earth L2 point; India's Aditya-L1 studies the Sun from the L1 point.

Quick question: From which point in space will NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope operate?

  1. Low Earth orbit
  2. Sun–Earth L1 point
  3. Sun–Earth L2 point
  4. Lunar orbit
Show answer

(C) Sun–Earth L2 point Roman will work from the Sun–Earth L2 point, about 1.5 million km from Earth, like the James Webb telescope.

Source: IASbaba Daily Current Affairs, 18 Aug 2026

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें

तेल रिसाव से क़ेश्म द्वीप के पास ईरान के हारा मैंग्रोव वन को ख़तरा

अगस्त 2026 में बताया गया कि क़ेश्म द्वीप के पास तेल रिसाव से दक्षिणी ईरान के होर्मोज़गान प्रांत के हारा मैंग्रोव वनों को ख़तरा है। ये वन उत्तरी फ़ारस की खाड़ी में क़ेश्म द्वीप और ख़ुरान जलडमरूमध्य के आसपास फैले हैं और फ़ारस की खाड़ी का सबसे बड़ा मैंग्रोव क्षेत्र हैं। यहाँ की प्रमुख प्रजाति ग्रे मैंग्रोव (Avicennia marina) है।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • हारा संरक्षित क्षेत्र रामसर आर्द्रभूमि, यूनेस्को बायोस्फ़ीयर रिज़र्व और महत्वपूर्ण पक्षी क्षेत्र है।
  • मैंग्रोव तटों को तूफ़ान से बचाते हैं, मछलियों की नर्सरी का काम करते हैं और 'ब्लू कार्बन' जमा करते हैं; भारत का सबसे बड़ा मैंग्रोव वन पश्चिम बंगाल का सुंदरबन है।

छोटा सवाल: अगस्त 2026 में तेल रिसाव से ख़तरे में आए हारा मैंग्रोव वन किस देश में हैं?

  1. ओमान
  2. ईरान
  3. क़तर
  4. सऊदी अरब
