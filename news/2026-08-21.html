India and ADB sign $230 million loan to modernise Chennai's water supply and sewerage

The Government of India and the Asian Development Bank (ADB) signed a $230 million loan on 21 August 2026 for the Chennai Climate-Resilient Water Security and Sewerage Project, which ADB had approved on 3 July 2026. Saurabh Singh, Deputy Secretary in the Department of Economic Affairs, and Mio Oka, Country Director of ADB's India Resident Mission, signed the agreement. The project will lay over 170 km of water and sewer pipes, upgrade 7 water pumping stations and 38 sewer pumping stations, and make Chennai the first Indian city with a closed-loop ring-main water supply system.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Asian Development Bank was set up in 1966 and has its headquarters in Manila, Philippines.
  • The project is aligned with AMRUT 2.0 (Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation), which was launched on 1 October 2021; Chennai is the capital of Tamil Nadu.

Territorial Army launches national hackathon 'Terrier Cyber Quest 2026'

The Territorial Army launched Terrier Cyber Quest 2026 (TCQ 3.0), a national-level hackathon run with CyberPeace, on 21 August 2026. It seeks indigenous solutions in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and threat mitigation through three tracks: Bug Hunting, AI Kavach and Creators Challenge. Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand assure India a women's doubles medal at BWF World Championships

Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand beat China's Jia Yi Fan and Zhang Shuxian 16-21, 21-15, 21-13 in the quarter-finals of the BWF World Championships in New Delhi on 21 August 2026. Reaching the semi-finals assured the pair of at least a bronze medal, where they will face China's top seeds Liu Sheng Shu and Tan Ning. They are the first Indian women's doubles pair to assure a World Championships medal since Jwala Gutta and Ashwini Ponnappa won bronze in London in 2011.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The 2026 BWF World Championships (17–23 August) are being held in New Delhi; India last hosted the event in Hyderabad in 2009.
  • The Badminton World Federation (BWF) has its headquarters in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia; Gayatri is the daughter of former All England champion and national coach Pullela Gopichand.

World observes International Day of Remembrance of and Tribute to the Victims of Terrorism

The International Day of Remembrance of and Tribute to the Victims of Terrorism was observed on 21 August 2026. The UN day honours people killed, injured or affected by terrorist attacks and stresses support for survivors and their families. This year's observance focused on victim-centred remembrance and on protecting victims from retraumatisation and online abuse.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The day was established by UN General Assembly resolution 72/165 of 2017 and was first observed in 2018.
  • The UN Office of Counter-Terrorism (UNOCT) was set up in 2017; 21 August is also observed as World Senior Citizen's Day.
