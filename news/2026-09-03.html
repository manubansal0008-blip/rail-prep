PM Modi holds talks with Belgian PM Bart De Wever; both aim to double trade in five years

Prime Minister Narendra Modi held talks with Belgian Prime Minister Bart De Wever at Hyderabad House, New Delhi, on 3 September 2026, during De Wever's official visit to India from 2 to 4 September. It is the first visit to India by a Belgian Prime Minister in about two decades. The two sides exchanged MoUs in defence, renewable energy and fighting crime, agreed to double bilateral trade within five years and launched an India–Belgium Investment Fast-Track Mechanism.

Shakti Sharma becomes first non-medical woman officer to reach Air Vice Marshal rank

Shakti Sharma of the Indian Air Force's Education Branch became the first woman officer from a non-medical branch of the Indian armed forces to reach the rank of Air Vice Marshal (AVM) or equivalent, the Defence Ministry announced. She took charge as Assistant Chief of the Air Staff (Education) at Air Headquarters on 1 September 2026. Commissioned on 18 June 1994, she was earlier the first woman officer of the three services to command a Sainik School, as Principal of Sainik School, Kapurthala.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Air Vice Marshal is a two-star rank, equal to Major General in the Army and Rear Admiral in the Navy.
  • The Indian Air Force was established on 8 October 1932, which is observed as Air Force Day.

  4. Logistics Branch

(A) Education Branch She is from the Education Branch and took charge as Assistant Chief of the Air Staff (Education) on 1 September 2026.

India Post Payments Bank launches DakPay Sound Box on 9th Foundation Day

India Post Payments Bank (IPPB) marked its 9th Foundation Day on 1 September 2026 by launching the DakPay Sound Box for merchants, a Digital Insurance Technology Platform and a Digital Mutual Fund Platform. IPPB had 13.25 crore customers as of 31 March 2026, of whom 77% are in rural areas and 49% are women. It works through about 1.65 lakh post offices and nearly 3 lakh postal employees.

Smriti Mandhana becomes highest run-scorer in women's international cricket

Smriti Mandhana scored 124 off 64 balls against Hong Kong, China in the Women's Asia Cup on 3 September 2026 to become the leading run-scorer in women's international cricket. Her career tally of 10,880 runs across formats went past Mithali Raj's 10,868. It was her 18th international century, the most by any woman, overtaking Australia's Meg Lanning, as India posted 193/5 and won the match.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • The International Cricket Council (ICC) is headquartered in Dubai, UAE.
