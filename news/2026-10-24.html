Railway Minister to open Satna–Devendra Nagar and Rewa–Churhat rail sections in Madhya Pradesh

Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw is to inaugurate the Satna–Devendra Nagar (53 km) and Rewa–Churhat (65 km) rail sections in Madhya Pradesh on 24 October 2026, together more than 100 km of new line. Defence3/5

Indigenous anti-tank loitering munition Nagastra 2 delivered to the Army

Solar Defence and Aerospace Limited (SDAL), a subsidiary of Solar Industries, has delivered the indigenous anti-tank loitering munition 'Nagastra 2' to the Indian Army. The man-portable 'kamikaze' drone completed technical trials at the Mahajan Field Firing Range, Rajasthan, in August 2026. It carries a 4 kg tandem warhead that can pierce 650 mm of armour and has a range of 25 km in recover-and-reuse mode and 50 km in fire-and-forget mode.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • A loitering munition hovers over a target area before diving onto the target, which is why it is called a 'kamikaze drone'.
  • Solar Industries is headquartered in Nagpur, Maharashtra.

International4/5

UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2026 calls for 45% emission cuts by 2035

The UN Environment Programme (UNEP) has released its 17th Emissions Gap Report, titled 'The Point of Return'. It says global greenhouse gas emissions were 58.4 billion tonnes CO2-equivalent in 2025, only 0.1 billion tonnes below 2024, and that temporarily crossing 1.5°C of warming now looks unavoidable. To bring warming back to 1.5°C by 2100, emissions must fall 18% by 2030, 45% by 2035 and 69% by 2040 from 2025 levels.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • UNEP was set up in 1972 and is headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya.
  • The 1.5°C goal comes from the Paris Agreement on climate change (2015).

  4. Paris
Show answer

(C) Nairobi The UN Environment Programme (UNEP), founded in 1972, is headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya. Its 2026 Emissions Gap Report is titled 'The Point of Return'.
