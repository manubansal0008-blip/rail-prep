India signs deal with US to buy Javelin anti-tank missiles

India signed a Letter of Offer and Acceptance (LOA) with the United States on 28 August 2026 to procure Javelin anti-tank guided missiles under the US Foreign Military Sales (FMS) route, The Hindu reported on 29 August. The FMS route is a government-to-government mechanism. Javelin, a man-portable 'fire-and-forget' missile, is made by the Javelin Joint Venture of RTX (formerly Raytheon) and Lockheed Martin.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • A 'fire-and-forget' missile guides itself to the target after launch, so the soldier can take cover immediately.
  • India's indigenous anti-tank guided missile family, Nag, was developed by DRDO under the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme.

Forex reserves hit all-time high of $729.33 billion

India's foreign exchange reserves rose to a record $729.33 billion as of 21 August 2026, above the previous peak of $728.49 billion on 27 February, the Indian Express reported on 29 August. The rise was driven by deposits under the RBI's concessional swap window for FCNR(B) (Foreign Currency Non-Resident Bank) deposits, opened on 8 June, which drew $65.4 billion by 21 August. Under the window the RBI bears the exchange-rate risk, letting banks offer attractive rates to NRIs.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • India's forex reserves are held by the RBI and include foreign currency assets, gold, Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) and the reserve tranche position with the IMF.
  • FCNR(B) deposits are kept by NRIs in foreign currency, so the depositor does not bear the exchange-rate risk.

India and Canada hold first Economic and Financial Dialogue, aim to conclude CEPA by end-2026

India and Canada held their inaugural Economic and Financial Dialogue in Toronto, co-chaired by Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman and Canada's Finance Minister François-Philippe Champagne, the Indian Express reported on 29 August 2026. The two sides aim to conclude negotiations on a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) by the end of 2026, and India said it was ready to begin talks on a Bilateral Investment Treaty. They also welcomed wider use of UPI in Canada and set a goal of CAD 70 billion in bilateral trade by 2030.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Ottawa is the capital of Canada; the dialogue followed a commitment made by PM Narendra Modi and Canadian PM Mark Carney in New Delhi in March 2026.
  • UPI (Unified Payments Interface) was developed by the National Payments Corporation of India and launched in 2016.

Nilekani-led task force invites suggestions on reforming public examinations

The High-Powered Task Force (HPTF) on reforms in public examinations, chaired by Nandan Nilekani, has invited suggestions from students, parents, teachers, institutions, examination bodies and experts, according to a release reported on 29 August 2026. The task force is looking at the conduct, security, design and governance of public examinations, including those held by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Suggestions can be sent till 13 September 2026.

📌 Remember for the exam
