Glasgow Commonwealth Games end; India finishes 4th with 39 medals

The 23rd Commonwealth Games in Glasgow, Scotland (23 July–2 August 2026) ended with India fourth in the medal table with 39 medals — 13 gold, 17 silver and 9 bronze. Boxing gave India a record seven gold medals, won by Ankush Panghal, Sachin Siwach, Jaismine Lamboria, Preeti Pawar, Sakshi Chaudhary, Priya Ghanghas and Arundhati Choudhary. Judokas Asmita Dey and Harsh Singh won India's first-ever Commonwealth Games judo golds, and Gulveer Singh became the first Indian track and field athlete to win two medals at one edition (10,000m silver and 5,000m bronze).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Ahmedabad (Gujarat) will host the 2030 Commonwealth Games, the centenary edition; India earlier hosted the Games in New Delhi in 2010.
  • The first Commonwealth Games, then called the British Empire Games, were held in Hamilton, Canada, in 1930.

PM launches 100-week 'Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat Sankalp Abhiyan'

Prime Minister Narendra Modi launched the 'Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat Sankalp Abhiyan' on 2 August 2026 through a nationwide video conference that connected young people at more than 28,000 locations. Wildlife panel gives in-principle nod to third rail line through Satpura–Melghat tiger corridor

The Standing Committee of the National Board for Wildlife (SC-NBWL) has given in-principle wildlife clearance, with conditions, for a third railway line between Jujharpur (Betul district) and Chichonda (Narmadapuram district) in Madhya Pradesh, it was reported on 2 August 2026. The Central Railway project, part of capacity expansion on the Itarsi–Nagpur route, needs over 200 hectares of forest land in the corridor linking the Satpura and Melghat tiger reserves. All mitigation steps, including wildlife overpasses, must follow a scientific study by the State Forest Research Institute, Jabalpur.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Satpura Tiger Reserve is in Narmadapuram district of Madhya Pradesh; Melghat Tiger Reserve in Amravati district, Maharashtra, was among the first nine tiger reserves under Project Tiger (1973).
  • Central Railway has its headquarters at Mumbai CSMT (Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus).

Varsha Ashok Aglawe becomes first woman Director General of Geological Survey of India

Varsha Ashok Aglawe has taken charge as the 54th Director General of the Geological Survey of India (GSI), becoming the first woman to head the organisation in its 176-year history, GSI announced on 31 July 2026. A palaeontologist with over 30 years of experience, she joined GSI in 1992 after working with the Indian Bureau of Mines and was earlier Additional Director General and Head of GSI's Eastern Region in Kolkata. She succeeds Asit Saha.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • GSI was set up in 1851, mainly to find coal for the railways; it is headquartered in Kolkata and is an attached office of the Ministry of Mines.
  • The Indian Bureau of Mines, also under the Ministry of Mines, is headquartered in Nagpur.

ग्लासगो राष्ट्रमंडल खेल ख़त्म; भारत 39 पदकों के साथ चौथे स्थान पर

स्कॉटलैंड के ग्लासगो में हुए 23वें राष्ट्रमंडल खेल (23 जुलाई–2 अगस्त 2026) ख़त्म हो गए और भारत 39 पदकों — 13 स्वर्ण, 17 रजत और 9 कांस्य — के साथ पदक तालिका में चौथे स्थान पर रहा। मुक्केबाज़ी में भारत ने रिकॉर्ड सात स्वर्ण जीते, जो अंकुश पंघाल, सचिन सिवाच, जैस्मिन लम्बोरिया, प्रीति पवार, साक्षी चौधरी, प्रिया घणघस और अरुंधति चौधरी ने दिलाए। जूडो में अस्मिता डे और हर्ष सिंह ने राष्ट्रमंडल खेलों में भारत के पहले स्वर्ण जीते, जबकि गुलवीर सिंह एक ही संस्करण में दो पदक (10,000 मीटर में रजत और 5,000 मीटर में कांस्य) जीतने वाले पहले भारतीय ट्रैक एंड फ़ील्ड एथलीट बने।

  • पहले राष्ट्रमंडल खेल, जिन्हें तब ब्रिटिश एम्पायर गेम्स कहा जाता था, 1930 में कनाडा के हैमिल्टन में हुए थे।

भारतीय भूवैज्ञानिक सर्वेक्षण (GSI) ने 31 जुलाई 2026 को बताया कि वर्षा अशोक अगलावे ने उसकी 54वीं महानिदेशक के रूप में पदभार संभाल लिया है; 176 साल के इतिहास में संस्था की कमान संभालने वाली वे पहली महिला हैं। 30 साल से ज़्यादा अनुभव वाली जीवाश्म विज्ञानी (पेलियोन्टोलॉजिस्ट) अगलावे भारतीय खान ब्यूरो में काम करने के बाद 1992 में GSI में आईं और इससे पहले कोलकाता में GSI के पूर्वी क्षेत्र की प्रमुख व अपर महानिदेशक थीं। उन्होंने असित साहा की जगह ली है।
