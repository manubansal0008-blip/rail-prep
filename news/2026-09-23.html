President Murmu presents 72nd National Film Awards; Dadasaheb Phalke Award to Anant Nag

President Droupadi Murmu presented the 72nd National Film Awards (for films of 2024) at Ekta Nagar, Kevadia, Gujarat, on 22 September 2026 — the first time the ceremony was held there, outside the national capital. Veteran actor Anant Nag received the Dadasaheb Phalke Award for 2024. 'Article 370' won Best Feature Film, Yami Gautam won Best Actress, and the Best Actor award was shared by Mammootty ('Bramayugam') and Kartik Aaryan ('Chandu Champion').

📌 Remember for the exam

  • Ekta Nagar (Kevadia) in Narmada district, Gujarat, is home to the Statue of Unity of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.

Defence3/5

MoD signs ₹810.79 crore contract with BDL for 160 SAT-SAAW glide bombs for IAF

The Ministry of Defence signed a contract worth ₹810.79 crore with Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL) in New Delhi on 23 September 2026 for 160 Satellite Smart Anti-Airfield Weapons (SAT-SAAW) and related equipment for the Indian Air Force, under the Buy (Indian-IDDM) category. SAT-SAAW is a DRDO-designed precision-guided glide bomb with about 60% indigenous content, used to destroy enemy airfields from long stand-off range. It can be launched from Jaguar, Hawk and Su-30 MKI aircraft, and deliveries are due in 2027-28 and 2028-29.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Bharat Dynamics Limited was set up in 1970 and is headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana.
  • IDDM stands for Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured, the top procurement category under the Defence Acquisition Procedure.

Economy / Banking4/5

Fitch raises India's FY27 GDP growth forecast to 6.9%

Fitch Ratings raised India's GDP growth forecast for 2026-27 (FY27) by 50 basis points to 6.9% from 6.4% in its Global Economic Outlook – September 2026, released on 23 September. It cited India's 7.8% year-on-year growth in the April–June quarter (Q1 FY27), but expects momentum to slow in the second half due to weaker manufacturing and services, poor monsoon rainfall and rising prices. Fitch expects headline inflation to rise to 5.5% by December 2026 and ease to 4.2% by the end of 2027.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Moody's had raised its FY27 forecast for India to 7% from 6% on 18 September 2026.
  • Under the RBI's inflation-targeting framework, the CPI inflation target is 4% with a tolerance band of ±2%.

International5/5

Vice-President inaugurates 9th India International Water Week in New Delhi

Vice-President C. P. Radhakrishnan inaugurated the 9th India International Water Week (IIWW) 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 22 September 2026. The five-day event (22–26 September), organised by the Ministry of Jal Shakti on the theme 'Climate Resilient Water Management', has the Netherlands and Germany as partner countries and 14 Indian states as partner states, with more than 5,000 delegates. The 2nd International WASH (water, sanitation and hygiene) Conference is being held alongside it from 22 to 24 September.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Ministry of Jal Shakti was formed in 2019 by merging the Ministry of Water Resources and the Ministry of Drinking Water and Sanitation.
  • World Water Day is observed on 22 March every year.
