Government scheme1/5

Shivraj Singh Chouhan launches NMMS 2.0 attendance app for VB-G RAM G

Rural Development Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan has launched NMMS 2.0, an upgraded attendance app for workers under the VB-G RAM G rural employment scheme. Developed with IIT Madras, it adds AI-based verification, device-level security and offline attendance that uploads after the muster roll is closed.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • NMMS stands for National Mobile Monitoring System; it records worksite attendance with geo-tagged photos.
  • The VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 replaced MGNREGA and guarantees 125 days of wage employment to rural households.

Economy / Banking2/5

RBI revises leverage ratio rules for banks under Basel III

The Reserve Bank of India has revised the leverage ratio framework for banks to align it with the Basel Committee's 2017 standard, effective 1 April 2027. The minimum leverage ratio will be 4% for Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs) and 3.5% for other banks.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Leverage ratio = Tier 1 capital ÷ total exposure; it limits how much a bank can borrow against its capital.
  • The Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS) is based at the Bank for International Settlements in Basel, Switzerland.

Quick question: Under the RBI's revised framework, what is the minimum leverage ratio for Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs)?

  1. 4%
  2. 3%
  3. 4.5%
  4. 5%
Show answer

(A) 4% From 1 April 2027, D-SIBs must keep a minimum leverage ratio of 4%; other banks 3.5%.

C-DOT and IIT (BHU) to set up Centre of Excellence for 6G and quantum technologies

The Centre for Development of Telematics (C-DOT) and IIT (BHU) Varanasi have signed an MoU to set up C-DOT's sixth Centre of Excellence. It will work on 5G-Advanced and 6G, artificial intelligence, quantum technologies and cybersecurity.

Defence5/5

Home Ministry and Army to train 600 Civil Defence master trainers in 12 States/UTs

The Ministry of Home Affairs, through its Directorate General of Fire Services, Civil Defence and Home Guards, will train 600 Civil Defence master trainers together with the Indian Army. Fifty trainers each from 12 States and Union Territories will attend a three-day course by the second week of November 2026. Each master trainer will then train 100 Civil Defence volunteers.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Civil Defence in India works under the Civil Defence Act, 1968.
  • Civil Defence, Fire Services and Home Guards come under the Ministry of Home Affairs.

  2. 300
  3. 600
  4. 1,200

RBI ने बेसल III के तहत बैंकों के लीवरेज अनुपात नियम बदले

भारतीय रिज़र्व बैंक ने बैंकों के लीवरेज अनुपात ढाँचे को बेसल समिति के 2017 मानक के अनुरूप बदल दिया है, जो 1 अप्रैल 2027 से लागू होगा। घरेलू प्रणालीगत रूप से महत्वपूर्ण बैंकों (D-SIB) के लिए न्यूनतम लीवरेज अनुपात 4% और बाक़ी बैंकों के लिए 3.5% होगा।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • लीवरेज अनुपात = टियर 1 पूँजी ÷ कुल एक्सपोज़र; इससे तय होता है कि बैंक अपनी पूँजी के मुक़ाबले कितना उधार ले सकता है।
  • बैंकिंग पर्यवेक्षण की बेसल समिति (BCBS) स्विट्ज़रलैंड के बेसल में बैंक फ़ॉर इंटरनेशनल सेटलमेंट्स में है।

छोटा सवाल: RBI के संशोधित ढाँचे में घरेलू प्रणालीगत रूप से महत्वपूर्ण बैंकों (D-SIB) का न्यूनतम लीवरेज अनुपात कितना है?

  1. 4%
  2. 3%
  3. 4.5%
  4. 5%
उत्तर देखें

(A) 4% 1 अप्रैल 2027 से D-SIB को कम से कम 4% और बाक़ी बैंकों को 3.5% लीवरेज अनुपात रखना होगा।
