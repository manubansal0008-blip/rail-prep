International Day of Peace observed on 21 September

The International Day of Peace was observed worldwide on 21 September 2026 with the theme 'Invest in Peace – For Everyone, Everywhere, Every Day'. The theme presents peace-building as a daily duty of every person, not only of governments and international bodies. The UN marks the day by calling for a halt to fighting, humanitarian access and dialogue in armed conflicts.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The UN General Assembly declared the day in 1981; in 2001 it fixed 21 September as a day of non-violence and ceasefire.
  • On this day the UN Secretary-General rings the Peace Bell at UN Headquarters in New York; the bell was donated by the United Nations Association of Japan.

18th India–Mongolia exercise Nomadic Elephant begins in Pithoragarh

The 18th edition of the India–Mongolia joint military exercise NOMADIC ELEPHANT began on 21 September 2026 at the Foreign Training Node in Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand, and will run till 3 October. Each side has sent 45 personnel; the training focuses on counter-insurgency operations in semi-urban and mountainous terrain under a UN mandate. Drills include cordon and search, room intervention, special heliborne operations, cyber security and the use of drones and counter-drone systems.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Nomadic Elephant is an annual platoon-level exercise held alternately in India and Mongolia; the previous edition was held in Ulaanbaatar in May–June 2025.
  • Ulaanbaatar is the capital of Mongolia; Pithoragarh district of Uttarakhand borders Nepal and China.
