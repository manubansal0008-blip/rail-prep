Cabinet raises MSP for rabi crops; wheat at ₹2,610 per quintal

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs, chaired by PM Narendra Modi, on 30 September 2026 raised the Minimum Support Prices (MSP) of all six rabi crops for Rabi Marketing Season 2027-28. Wheat MSP goes up by ₹25 to ₹2,610 per quintal, while safflower gets the biggest rise of ₹675 to ₹7,215. Rapeseed-mustard rises by ₹413 to ₹6,613 and masur (lentil) by ₹390 to ₹7,390.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • MSP is fixed by the Centre on the recommendations of the Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP).
  • The six rabi crops with MSP are wheat, barley, gram, masur (lentil), rapeseed-mustard and safflower.

Quick question: Which rabi crop got the highest absolute MSP increase for Rabi Marketing Season 2027-28?

  1. Wheat
  2. Rapeseed-mustard
  3. Masur (lentil)
  4. Safflower
Show answer

(D) Safflower Safflower MSP was raised by ₹675 to ₹7,215 per quintal – the biggest increase; wheat rose by ₹25 to ₹2,610.

Skytrax 2026: Singapore Airlines named World's Best Airline; Air India top Indian carrier at 57th

Singapore Airlines was named the World's Best Airline at the Skytrax World Airline Awards 2026, presented at a ceremony in London. Qatar Airways came second, followed by Cathay Pacific, ANA and Turkish Airlines. Air India was the highest-ranked Indian airline at 57th, up 27 places, while IndiGo was 61st and won Best Low-Cost Airline in India & South Asia.

Delhi launches 'Delhi Udyog Mitra' single-window business portal

Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta launched the Delhi Udyog Mitra portal on 28 September 2026, a single-window platform for business licences and NOCs, under the Ease of Doing Business initiative. It now offers more than 50 services from 15 departments and will grow to over 100 services across 21 departments, benefiting more than 8 lakh traders and MSMEs. The portal is linked with the National Single Window System (NSWS).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The National Single Window System (NSWS) for investor approvals was launched in September 2021 under DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
  • Rekha Gupta is Delhi's fourth woman Chief Minister, after Sushma Swaraj, Sheila Dikshit and Atishi.

Hyderabad start-up TakeMe2Space plans India's first orbital data centre mission

Hyderabad-based space start-up TakeMe2Space plans to launch India's first networked orbital data centre mission in 2028 on a SpaceX Falcon 9 rideshare, as reported on 28 September 2026. Two 100-kg satellites will go to a sun-synchronous orbit at about 500 km, each carrying 10 Nvidia Thor GPUs and 100 TB of storage, to process Earth-observation data in space itself.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • IN-SPACe, set up in 2020 and headquartered in Ahmedabad, authorises private space activities in India.
  • A sun-synchronous orbit is a near-polar orbit in which a satellite passes over a place at the same local time every day.

Defence PSU HAL signs ₹2,491 crore MoU for 30 helicopters with SkyPulse

Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) announced on 26 September 2026 that it has signed an MoU with SkyPulse Solutions IFSC Pvt Ltd for a ₹2,491 crore programme to acquire 30 HAL-built single- and twin-engine helicopters. Ten will be delivered in 2027–2029 and 20 from 2030 onwards, for air ambulance (HEMS), disaster response, search and rescue, tourism and UDAN services.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • HAL was founded in 1940 as Hindustan Aircraft by Walchand Hirachand; it is headquartered in Bengaluru and works under the Ministry of Defence.
  • HAL became a Maharatna company in October 2024.

प्रधानमंत्री नरेंद्र मोदी की अध्यक्षता वाली आर्थिक मामलों की मंत्रिमंडलीय समिति ने 30 सितंबर 2026 को रबी विपणन सत्र 2027-28 के लिए सभी छह रबी फ़सलों का न्यूनतम समर्थन मूल्य (MSP) बढ़ाया। गेहूँ का MSP ₹25 बढ़कर ₹2,610 प्रति क्विंटल हो गया, जबकि कुसुम (सैफ़्लावर) में सबसे ज़्यादा ₹675 की बढ़ोतरी हुई और यह ₹7,215 हो गया। रेपसीड-सरसों ₹413 बढ़कर ₹6,613 और मसूर ₹390 बढ़कर ₹7,390 हो गई।

  • MSP वाली छह रबी फ़सलें हैं – गेहूँ, जौ, चना, मसूर, रेपसीड-सरसों और कुसुम।

  1. गेहूँ

  3. मसूर
  4. कुसुम (सैफ़्लावर)
