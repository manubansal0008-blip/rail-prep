Cabinet approves 8 railway multitracking projects worth ₹20,804 crore

The Union Cabinet on 9 September 2026 approved eight railway multitracking projects worth about ₹20,804 crore, covering around 1,196 km. Three projects (about 656 km, ₹10,783 crore) cover 14 districts in West Bengal, Jharkhand, Odisha, Madhya Pradesh and Chhattisgarh, including the Kharagpur–Jharsuguda and Katni–Pendra Road fourth lines; five projects (about 540 km, ₹10,021 crore) cover 17 districts in Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka and Telangana, including Arakkonam–Renigunta and Whitefield–Bangarapet third and fourth lines. They will serve about 2,100 villages (about 52 lakh people).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Multitracking means adding a third or fourth line next to existing tracks so that more trains can run on busy routes.
  • Ashwini Vaishnaw is the Union Minister of Railways; the Railway Board is the apex body of Indian Railways in New Delhi.

Quick question: The eight railway multitracking projects approved by the Cabinet on 9 September 2026 cover about how many kilometres?

  1. 656 km
  2. 2,100 km
  3. 1,196 km
  4. 540 km
Show answer

(C) 1,196 km The eight projects worth about ₹20,804 crore cover around 1,196 km (about 656 km + 540 km) in nine states.

Source: All India Radio News, 10 Sep 2026 · Business Standard, 9 Sep 2026

Economy / Banking2/5

RBI recognises Unified Fintech Forum as second fintech SRO

The Reserve Bank of India has recognised the Unified Fintech Forum (UFF), formerly the Digital Lenders Association of India, as a Self-Regulatory Organisation in the FinTech sector (SRO-FT). It is the second such body after the Fintech Association for Consumer Empowerment (FACE), which was recognised in August 2024. UFF had 118 members as of end-June 2026.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The RBI issued its framework for recognising SROs in the FinTech sector in May 2024.
  • The RBI was set up on 1 April 1935 under the RBI Act, 1934, and is headquartered in Mumbai.

Quick question: Which body did the RBI recognise in September 2026 as the second Self-Regulatory Organisation in the FinTech sector?

  1. Unified Fintech Forum
  2. Payments Council of India
  3. Fintech Association for Consumer Empowerment
  4. Internet and Mobile Association of India
Show answer

(A) Unified Fintech Forum The Unified Fintech Forum (formerly Digital Lenders Association of India) is the second SRO-FT; FACE was the first, in August 2024.

Summits / Days5/5

World Suicide Prevention Day: 'Changing the Narrative on Suicide'

World Suicide Prevention Day was observed on 10 September 2026 with the theme 'Changing the Narrative on Suicide' and the call to action 'Start the Conversation'; 2026 is the third and final year of this theme. According to the WHO, more than 7,20,000 people die by suicide every year. In India, help is available on the Tele-MANAS mental health helpline 14416 (or 1800-89-14416).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • World Suicide Prevention Day was started in 2003 by the International Association for Suicide Prevention (IASP) together with the WHO.
  • Tele-MANAS, India's national tele-mental health programme, was launched on 10 October 2022 (World Mental Health Day).

Quick question: What is the number of India's Tele-MANAS mental health helpline, highlighted on World Suicide Prevention Day 2026?

  1. 14416
  2. 1098
  3. 112
  4. 14567
Show answer

(A) 14416 Tele-MANAS can be reached at 14416 (or 1800-89-14416); 1098 is Childline, 112 the emergency number and 14567 the Elderline.

Source: Sahi Sarkari Jobs daily current affairs (WHO), 10 Sep 2026

RBI ने यूनिफ़ाइड फ़िनटेक फ़ोरम को दूसरा फ़िनटेक SRO माना

भारतीय रिज़र्व बैंक ने यूनिफ़ाइड फ़िनटेक फ़ोरम (UFF), जिसका पुराना नाम डिजिटल लेंडर्स एसोसिएशन ऑफ़ इंडिया था, को फ़िनटेक क्षेत्र के स्व-नियामक संगठन (SRO-FT) के रूप में मान्यता दी है। अगस्त 2024 में मान्यता पाने वाले फ़िनटेक एसोसिएशन फ़ॉर कंज़्यूमर एम्पावरमेंट (FACE) के बाद यह ऐसा दूसरा संगठन है। जून 2026 के अंत तक UFF के 118 सदस्य थे।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • RBI ने फ़िनटेक क्षेत्र में SRO को मान्यता देने का ढाँचा मई 2024 में जारी किया था।
  • RBI की स्थापना RBI अधिनियम, 1934 के तहत 1 अप्रैल 1935 को हुई थी और इसका मुख्यालय मुंबई में है।

  1. यूनिफ़ाइड फ़िनटेक फ़ोरम
