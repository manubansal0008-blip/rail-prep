India celebrates third National Space Day; main event at SAC Ahmedabad

India celebrated its third National Space Day on 23 August 2026, marking the anniversary of the Chandrayaan-3 landing on the Moon on 23 August 2023. The national event was hosted by ISRO's Space Applications Centre (SAC) in Ahmedabad on the theme 'Towards Viksit Bharat: Innovating, Collaborating and Aspiring to Global Space Leadership'. ISRO Chairman Dr V. Narayanan said work on the Bharatiya Antariksh Station is under way, with its first module approved and a target of 2035.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Chandrayaan-3's Vikram lander touched down near the Moon's south pole at the site named Shiv Shakti Point; India became the fourth country to soft-land on the Moon and the first near its south pole.
  • Chandrayaan-3 was launched on 14 July 2023 by LVM3 from Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota; National Space Day was first celebrated in 2024.

BWF World Championships end in New Delhi; Alex Lanier and An Se-young win singles titles

The 30th BWF World Championships ended at the Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi on 23 August 2026. France's Alex Lanier beat Japan's Kodai Naraoka to become the first French player to win a world singles title, while South Korea's An Se-young beat Japan's Akane Yamaguchi for her second world title. India's medal came from Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand, who won bronze in women's doubles, India's first medal in the category since 2011.

Cochin Shipyard delivers anti-submarine shallow water craft 'Mangrol' to the Navy

Cochin Shipyard Limited (CSL) delivered Mangrol, the third of eight Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft (ASW SWC) it is building, to the Indian Navy at Kochi, as reported on 23 August 2026. The about 78-metre vessel can reach 25 knots, has a range of 1,800 nautical miles and carries lightweight torpedoes, indigenous anti-submarine rockets and shallow-water sonar. It has more than 80% indigenous content.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Navy ordered 16 ASW Shallow Water Craft in all: eight from Cochin Shipyard and eight from Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers (GRSE), Kolkata.
  • Cochin Shipyard built India's first indigenous aircraft carrier INS Vikrant, commissioned on 2 September 2022.

Himachal Pradesh signs MoU with ISB to tap ₹22,600 crore forest economy

The Himachal Pradesh Forest Department signed a five-year MoU on 20 August 2026 with the Bharti Institute of Public Policy of the Indian School of Business (ISB-BIPP) to develop the state's forest economy, in the presence of Chief Minister Sukhvinder Singh Sukhu. Economy / Banking5/5

DGFT eases rupee invoicing rules for exporters under Foreign Trade Policy

Through a notification dated 20 August 2026, the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) amended the Foreign Trade Policy 2023 to align it with the RBI's FEMA (Manner of Receipt and Payment) Regulations, 2023. Export contracts with countries outside the Asian Clearing Union can now be invoiced in any foreign currency or in Indian rupees. Rupee payments for exports to countries other than Nepal and Bhutan will now qualify for FTP benefits and count towards export obligations, and exports under EXIM Bank or Government of India lines of credit can be invoiced in rupees.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The DGFT works under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry; the Foreign Trade Policy 2023 came into force on 1 April 2023 and has no fixed end date.
  • The Asian Clearing Union (ACU), set up in 1974, has its headquarters in Tehran, Iran.
