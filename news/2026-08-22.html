Neeraj Chopra finishes second at Lausanne Diamond League with season-best 88.05 m

Neeraj Chopra finished second in the men's javelin at the Lausanne Diamond League in Switzerland on 21 August 2026 with a season-best throw of 88.05 m. Sri Lanka's Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage won with 88.14 m, just 9 cm ahead, while Grenada's Anderson Peters was third with 86.69 m. The result brought Chopra back into the 88-metre range after a difficult start to the 2026 season.

BRICS Tourism Ministers adopt the Jaipur Declaration

BRICS Tourism Ministers unanimously adopted the Jaipur Declaration at their meeting in Jaipur, Rajasthan, which concluded on 21 August 2026 under India's BRICS Chairship. Union Tourism and Culture Minister Gajendra Singh Shekhawat presided over the meeting, held on the theme 'Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability'. The declaration sets four priorities: AI and tourism, sustainable and responsible tourism, tourism skilling, and tourism exchanges with seamless travel facilitation.

  • Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage City in 2019.

Syed Akbaruddin, Annie George Mathew and Janmejaya Kumar Sinha named to RBI Central Board

The Appointments Committee of the Cabinet on 22 August 2026 cleared the appointment of Syed Akbaruddin, Annie George Mathew and Janmejaya Kumar Sinha as part-time, non-official directors on the Central Board of the Reserve Bank of India for four years. Akbaruddin is a former Indian Permanent Representative to the UN, Mathew is a former Special Secretary in the Department of Expenditure, and Sinha is a management consultant formerly with Boston Consulting Group. The appointments are made under Section 8(1)(c) of the RBI Act, 1934.

Chhattisgarh becomes first inland state to export value-added Tilapia

According to a Fisheries Ministry release (PIB, 20 August 2026), Chhattisgarh has become the first inland state in India to begin exporting value-added Tilapia fish products, with 382 tonnes already exported to the USA. The state is developing 34 fisheries clusters under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) for production, processing and export. A Tilapia hatchery supplies quality seed and is developing a genetically improved black-strain Tilapia.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana was launched in September 2020 with an outlay of ₹20,050 crore for the fisheries sector.
  • Chhattisgarh was formed on 1 November 2000; its capital is Raipur.

International Day Commemorating the Victims of Acts of Violence Based on Religion or Belief observed

The International Day Commemorating the Victims of Acts of Violence Based on Religion or Belief was observed on 22 August 2026. The UN day condemns violence and terrorism against people targeted for their religion or beliefs. It also stresses support for victims, interfaith dialogue, human rights education and state protection of religious minorities.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The day was declared by UN General Assembly resolution A/RES/73/296, adopted on 28 May 2019, and was first observed on 22 August 2019.
  • In India, Articles 25 to 28 of the Constitution guarantee the Right to Freedom of Religion.
