Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkiye sign Mecca Joint Defence Agreement

Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkiye signed the Mecca Joint Defence Agreement on 7 August 2026 at the 'Mecca Al-Mukarramah Summit for Joint Defence', The Hindu reported on 8 August 2026. Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan and Pakistan Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif attended. Under the pact, an armed attack on any one signatory will be treated as an attack on all three, with commitments to collective defence and deterrence.

  • Riyadh is the capital of Saudi Arabia, Ankara of Turkiye and Islamabad of Pakistan.

Parliament passes MSME Development (Amendment) Bill, 2026

The Lok Sabha passed the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (Amendment) Bill, 2026 by voice vote on 7 August 2026, completing its passage in Parliament after the Rajya Sabha approved it on 3 August. The Bill amends the MSMED Act, 2006: Central Public Sector Enterprises must settle MSME invoices through TReDS, mediation in delayed-payment cases must finish within 90 days, and courts can order payment of at least 50% of an award if an appeal is pending for over six months. It also makes the Udyam Registration Portal a permanent, free and voluntary digital platform.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • MSMEs are classified on the basis of investment in plant and machinery and annual turnover; Udyam registrations have risen to 9.16 crore.
  • TReDS (Trade Receivables Discounting System) is an RBI-regulated platform for financing MSME invoices; invoice discounting on it reached ₹3.47 lakh crore in 2025-26.

Quick question: The MSME Development (Amendment) Bill, 2026 amends which Act?

  1. Companies Act, 2013
  2. Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016
  3. Factories Act, 1948
  4. MSMED Act, 2006
Show answer

(D) MSMED Act, 2006 The Bill amends the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, 2006.

Oil palm mission covers 2.73 lakh hectares, 42% of target, says Centre

The National Mission on Edible Oils–Oil Palm (NMEO-OP) brought 2.73 lakh hectares under oil palm between 2021-22 and 2025-26, about 42% of its 6.5 lakh hectare target, the Agriculture Ministry told the Rajya Sabha, as reported on 8 August 2026. The centrally sponsored mission has an outlay of ₹11,040 crore, of which the Centre's share is ₹8,844 crore, and it is being implemented in 15 States. Annual action plans worth ₹1,143.9 crore have been approved for 2026-27.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • NMEO-OP was launched in 2021-22; oil palm farmers get price assurance through a Viability Gap Payment (VGP).
  • The National Mission on Edible Oils–Oilseeds (NMEO-Oilseeds) was approved on 3 October 2024 for seven years to raise domestic oilseed output.

DAE plans to sharply cut land needed for small nuclear reactors

The Department of Atomic Energy (DAE) has told Parliament that land needed for a twin 220 MWe reactor site could fall from about 330 hectares to about 90 hectares, the Indian Express reported on 8 August 2026. The minimum exclusion zone, now about 1 km, is proposed to be reduced to 500 m for 220 MWe reactors and 700 m for 700 MWe reactors, which AERB and DAE have agreed to in principle. NPCIL has issued a request for proposals for the 220 MWe Bharat Small Reactor (BSR), while BARC is developing the Bharat Small Modular Reactor and the 55 MWe SMR-55.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Department of Atomic Energy was set up in 1954 and works directly under the Prime Minister; BARC is at Trombay, Mumbai.
  • The Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (AERB), India's nuclear safety regulator, was set up in 1983; India aims to have at least five indigenous small modular reactors running by 2033.

  2. 220 MWe
  3. 700 MWe
  4. 1,000 MWe
Show answer

(B) 220 MWe The Bharat Small Reactor is a 220 MWe design; BARC's SMR-55 is a 55 MWe reactor.

संसद ने MSME विकास (संशोधन) विधेयक, 2026 पारित किया

लोकसभा ने 7 अगस्त 2026 को सूक्ष्म, लघु और मध्यम उद्यम विकास (संशोधन) विधेयक, 2026 ध्वनिमत से पारित कर दिया; राज्यसभा इसे 3 अगस्त को ही मंज़ूरी दे चुकी थी, इसलिए अब यह संसद से पारित हो गया। यह विधेयक MSMED अधिनियम, 2006 में संशोधन करता है: केंद्रीय सार्वजनिक उपक्रमों को MSME के बिलों का भुगतान TReDS के ज़रिए करना होगा, देर से भुगतान के मामलों में मध्यस्थता 90 दिन में पूरी करनी होगी और अपील छह महीने से ज़्यादा लंबित रहने पर अदालत फ़ैसले की कम से कम 50% राशि का भुगतान करवा सकेगी। उद्यम रजिस्ट्रेशन पोर्टल को भी स्थायी, मुफ़्त और स्वैच्छिक डिजिटल मंच बनाया गया है।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • MSME का वर्गीकरण प्लांट और मशीनरी में निवेश और सालाना कारोबार (टर्नओवर) के आधार पर होता है; उद्यम पंजीकरण बढ़कर 9.16 करोड़ हो गए हैं।
  • TReDS (ट्रेड रिसीवेबल्स डिस्काउंटिंग सिस्टम) MSME बिलों के वित्तपोषण का RBI द्वारा विनियमित मंच है; 2025-26 में इस पर बिल डिस्काउंटिंग ₹3.47 लाख करोड़ तक पहुंची।

छोटा सवाल: MSME विकास (संशोधन) विधेयक, 2026 किस अधिनियम में संशोधन करता है?

  1. कंपनी अधिनियम, 2013
  2. दिवाला एवं शोधन अक्षमता संहिता, 2016
  3. कारख़ाना अधिनियम, 1948
  4. MSMED अधिनियम, 2006
उत्तर देखें

(D) MSMED अधिनियम, 2006 यह विधेयक सूक्ष्म, लघु और मध्यम उद्यम विकास (MSMED) अधिनियम, 2006 में संशोधन करता है।

ऑयल पाम मिशन के तहत 2.73 लाख हेक्टेयर क्षेत्र, लक्ष्य का 42%: केंद्र
