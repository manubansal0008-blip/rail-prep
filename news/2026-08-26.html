e-Shram portal for unorganised workers completes five years

The e-Shram portal of the Ministry of Labour and Employment completed five years on 26 August 2026. Over 31.89 crore unorganised workers have registered on it and received a 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN), and women make up 54.28% of registrations, with Uttar Pradesh and Bihar the top states. Its One-Stop Solution, launched in October 2024, links 15 social security and welfare schemes, and the portal is available in 22 languages through Bhashini.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • e-Shram was launched on 26 August 2021 as India's first national database of unorganised workers seeded with Aadhaar.
  • Workers aged 16 or above in the unorganised sector who are not income-tax payers or EPFO/ESIC members can register free of cost.

Mizoram declares 79 monuments and archaeological sites as State Protected Monuments

Mizoram has declared 79 monuments and archaeological sites as State Protected Monuments under the Mizoram Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites and Remains Act, 2001, Art and Culture Minister C. Lalsawivunga told the State Assembly on 26 August 2026. Heritage sites at Vangchhia and Lianpui villages in Champhai district are already Monuments of National Importance under the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI); Kawtchhuah Ropui at Vangchhia has over 170 carved standing stones. The government is also considering protecting four more sites.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Mizoram became a full state on 20 February 1987; its capital is Aizawl.
  • The Archaeological Survey of India was founded in 1861 with Alexander Cunningham as its first Director-General; it works under the Ministry of Culture.

Outward remittances under LRS rise 6.5% in June to $2.55 billion

Outward remittances by resident Indians under the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) rose 6.46% to $2,551.34 million in June 2026 from $2,396.42 million in May, according to RBI data reported on 26 August 2026. Travel was the biggest head at $1,366.81 million, more than half of the total, followed by overseas investments. Under LRS, a resident individual can send up to $250,000 abroad in a financial year.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Liberalised Remittance Scheme was introduced by the RBI in 2004 and works under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999.
  • The Reserve Bank of India was set up on 1 April 1935 and has its headquarters in Mumbai.

  4. $250,000

At UNCCD COP17 in Mongolia, India calls for proactive drought resilience

Union Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav urged a shift from reactive drought relief to proactive, technology-enabled drought resilience at a ministerial dialogue of the 17th Conference of Parties (COP17) of the UN Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD) in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, The Hindu reported on 26 August 2026. COP17 is being held from 17 to 28 August with the theme 'Restoring Land. Restoring Hope.' and delegates of 197 parties. Yadav called drought a 'defining development challenge' and described India's model of early warning, satellite monitoring and catchment forestry.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The UNCCD was adopted in Paris in 1994 and its secretariat is in Bonn, Germany; World Day to Combat Desertification and Drought is observed on 17 June.
  • India hosted UNCCD COP14 at Greater Noida in September 2019; Ulaanbaatar is the capital of Mongolia.

H1N1 makes up 98% of Influenza A cases in India, says ICMR chief

H1N1 accounts for about 98% of Influenza A cases in India and H3N2 for only about 2–3%, ICMR Director-General Rajiv Bahl said, as reported on 26 August 2026. He said there is no cause for panic because the circulating strain is the H1N1 pdm09 virus first seen in the 2009 pandemic, and most infections are mild. ICMR tracks influenza through a network of 73 sentinel hospitals and 73 Virus Research and Diagnostic Laboratories (VRDLs).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), headquartered in New Delhi, began in 1911 as the Indian Research Fund Association and was renamed ICMR in 1949.
  • The H1N1 'swine flu' pandemic was declared by the World Health Organization in 2009.
