India hosts first BIMSTEC Home Ministers' Meeting; Delhi Declaration adopted

India hosted the first-ever BIMSTEC Home Ministers' Meeting in New Delhi on 13 October 2026, chaired by Union Home Minister Amit Shah. The members endorsed the Delhi Declaration for joint action against terrorism and terror financing, drug and human trafficking, cybercrime and for the return of criminal fugitives.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • BIMSTEC was formed on 6 June 1997 through the Bangkok Declaration; its secretariat is in Dhaka, Bangladesh.
  • Its seven members are Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka and Thailand.

Railways3/5

Railway freight loading grows 6.14% in September

Indian Railways loaded 135.35 million tonnes of freight in September 2026, 6.14% more than 127.52 million tonnes in September 2025, as per figures released on 13 October. Cumulative loading for April–September 2026 reached 833.7 million tonnes (up 4.1%), and an average of 443 coal rakes a day were supplied, up 11.6%.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Coal is the biggest commodity carried by Indian Railways' freight trains.
  • India's two Dedicated Freight Corridors are the Eastern DFC (Ludhiana–Sonnagar) and the Western DFC (Dadri–JNPT).

  3. 135.35 million tonnes
  4. 150.2 million tonnes
Show answer

The International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction was observed on 13 October 2026. The UN day promotes a global culture of reducing risks from disasters such as floods, cyclones, earthquakes and heatwaves.
