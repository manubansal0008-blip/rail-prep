India commits over €7 million as co-partner in Italy's Elettra 2.0 synchrotron upgrade

On 13 November 2026, during a visit to Elettra Sincrotrone Trieste in Italy, Science & Technology Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh announced that India will invest more than 7 million euros over five years from 2026-27 as a co-partner in the Elettra 2.0 programme.
