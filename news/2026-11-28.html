Breakthrough achieved in the 6.59-km Tunnel-14 of the Rishikesh–Karnaprayag rail project

Indian Railways has achieved the main breakthrough of Tunnel-14 of the Rishikesh–Karnaprayag new rail line in Uttarakhand, it was announced on 27 November 2026. The 6.59-km tunnel lies in Rudraprayag district and joins the two sections dug from Portal-1 at Sumerpur and Portal-2 at Marora. Main tunnel digging had begun in March 2024 after ground-settlement and land issues at Marora were solved.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • India's longest transport tunnel, Tunnel-8 (about 14.5 km, between Devprayag and Janasu), is also on the Rishikesh–Karnaprayag line
  • Rudraprayag stands at the confluence of the Alaknanda and Mandakini rivers in Uttarakhand

  2. 6.59 km
  3. 14.5 km
  4. 1.79 km
Show answer

(B) 6.59 km Tunnel-14 in Rudraprayag district is 6.59 km long; its breakthrough was announced on 27 November 2026.

Defence2/5

Vindhyagiri, the seventh and final Project 17A stealth frigate, delivered to the Indian Navy

Vindhyagiri (Yard 3024), the seventh and last Nilgiri-class (Project 17A) stealth frigate, was delivered to the Indian Navy at Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers (GRSE), Kolkata, on 27 November 2026. It is the third Project 17A ship built by GRSE, was completed in 68 months and has 75% indigenous content. All seven ships of the class have been delivered within 23 months of the first ship, INS Nilgiri (20 December 2024).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Project 17A frigates were designed by the Indian Navy's Warship Design Bureau (WDB)
  • The ship is named after the Vindhyagiri hill range in Karnataka and carries forward the name of an older Leander-class frigate (1981–2012)

  3. Project 28
  4. Project 17A

International3/5

India to host the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference in 2030

At the close of the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026 (PP-26) in Doha, Qatar, on 27 November 2026, member states agreed that India will host the next Plenipotentiary Conference (PP-30) in 2030. Communications Minister Jyotiraditya Scindia invited member states to attend. PP-26 was attended by 179 member states and more than 3,000 delegates; Uzbekistan will host the World Telecommunication Standardization Assembly in Samarkand in 2028.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • ITU (International Telecommunication Union), founded in 1865, is the UN agency for information and communication technologies, with its headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland
  • The Plenipotentiary Conference is ITU's highest policy-making body and meets every four years

  2. Qatar
  3. Uzbekistan
  4. Switzerland
Show answer

(A) India At PP-26 in Doha (ended 27 November 2026), member states agreed that India will host PP-30 in 2030.

Tributes paid to G. V. Mavalankar, first Speaker of the Lok Sabha, on his birth anniversary

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla paid floral tributes to G. V. Mavalankar on his birth anniversary at the Central Hall of Samvidhan Sadan, New Delhi, on 27 November 2026. Rajya Sabha Deputy Chairman Harivansh and MPs also paid tributes. Born on 27 November 1888 in Baroda, Mavalankar was unanimously elected Speaker of the First Lok Sabha on 15 May 1952 and served until his death on 27 February 1956.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Before Independence, Mavalankar was Speaker of the Bombay Legislative Assembly (1937–1946) and presided over the Central Legislative Assembly
  • The Lok Sabha chooses its Speaker under Article 93 of the Constitution

Kashi Tamil Sangamam 5.0 begins today with the theme 'Thirukkural Payilvom'

The fifth edition of Kashi Tamil Sangamam runs from 28 November to 12 December 2026, on the theme 'Thirukkural Payilvom – Thirukkural Abhyas Karen' (Let us learn the Thirukkural). It is organised by the Ministry of Education with IIT Madras, Banaras Hindu University and the governments of Tamil Nadu and Uttar Pradesh. About 1,400 participants from Tamil Nadu, in seven groups of about 200, will visit Varanasi, Prayagraj and Ayodhya.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • The Thirukkural, a classic Tamil work of 1,330 couplets, was written by Thiruvalluvar

  • रुद्रप्रयाग उत्तराखंड में अलकनंदा और मंदाकिनी नदियों के संगम पर स्थित है

  2. 6.59 किमी
  3. 14.5 किमी
