Design of India's first indigenous bullet train B28 is ready: Railway Minister

Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said on 1 November 2026 in Bengaluru, after inspecting the work at BEML, that the design of India's first indigenous (swadeshi) bullet train is ready and manufacturing is progressing fast. The high-speed train, named B28, is being developed by a BEML-led consortium for the Mumbai–Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor and future corridors. The first prototype may roll out early next year.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The B28 is designed jointly by the Integral Coach Factory (ICF), Chennai and BEML, and is being built at BEML's Bengaluru facility.
  • The Mumbai–Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor is about 508 km long and is being built by NHSRCL (National High Speed Rail Corporation Limited).

Dhinidhi Desinghu becomes first Indian female swimmer to win a Youth Olympics medal

16-year-old Dhinidhi Desinghu won bronze in the women's 100m freestyle at the Youth Olympic Games 2026 in Dakar, Senegal, on 2 November 2026, clocking 56.27 seconds. Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 2 November 2026. Defence4/5

CISF switches to sector-based commands for power, coal, steel and ports

From 1 November 2026, the Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) replaced its territorial (area-wise) commands with a nationwide, sector-based structure approved by the Ministry of Home Affairs. Installations with similar security risks now come under one command: Power-I (New Delhi), Power-II (Kolkata), Coal (Ranchi), Steel (Bhilai), Petroleum & Natural Gas (Guwahati), West Coast Port (Mumbai), East Coast Port (Chennai) and Government Buildings, Museums, Monuments & Metro (New Delhi).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • CISF was raised in 1969 and works under the Ministry of Home Affairs; it observes its Raising Day on 10 March.
  • Airports, space and atomic energy installations already had their own sector-specific CISF commands.

Quick question: Under CISF's new sector-based structure effective 1 November 2026, where is the headquarters of the Steel sector?

  1. Ranchi
  2. Rourkela
  3. Bhilai
  4. Jamshedpur
Show answer

(C) Bhilai The Steel sector is headquartered at Bhilai; the Coal sector is at Ranchi.

Source: India TV News, 1 Nov 2026 · The Week, 19 Oct 2026
