India's 51st Doppler Weather Radar inaugurated at Sambalpur, Odisha

Union Minister Dr Jitendra Singh virtually inaugurated India's 51st Doppler Weather Radar at Sambalpur, Odisha, on 17 September 2026, to improve weather monitoring in western Odisha and nearby regions. India's Doppler radar network has grown from 14 radars in 2014 to 51 in 2026, and about 40 more are planned under Mission Mausam. Odisha now has three such radars, at Sambalpur, Gopalpur and Paradip.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The India Meteorological Department (IMD) was set up in 1875 and is headquartered in New Delhi; it works under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.
  • A Doppler Weather Radar uses the Doppler effect to track rain, storms and wind speed, helping forecast cyclones and heavy rainfall.

US Congress passes Russia–Iran sanctions bill allowing tariffs of up to 100%

The US House of Representatives passed the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 by 262 votes to 159, after earlier approval by the Senate, and the bill has been sent to the President for signature, as reported on 16 September 2026. It provides primary and secondary sanctions on Russian officials, banks, energy interests and the 'shadow fleet', and on Iran's energy and weapons sectors. 📌 Remember for the exam

  • Secondary sanctions are penalties on third countries or firms that do business with a sanctioned country.
