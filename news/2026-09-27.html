India's first LNG-powered train flagged off from Sabarmati

Union Home Minister Amit Shah flagged off India's first LNG-powered train from Sabarmati Railway Station, Ahmedabad, on 27 September 2026. Its two 1,400 HP Driving Power Cars run on a dual-fuel diesel-LNG system that can replace about 40% of diesel, after more than 2,000 km of trials. He also inaugurated railway projects worth ₹1,472 crore, including a ₹700 crore WAG-12 loco maintenance shed and Kavach on the Sabarmati–Kalol–Sabarmati section (₹48 crore).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • LNG stands for Liquefied Natural Gas; it burns cleaner than diesel and cuts CO2, NOx and particulate emissions.
  • Kavach is India's indigenous Automatic Train Protection system, developed by RDSO (Lucknow).

World Tourism Day: National Digital Tourism Stack launched

World Tourism Day was observed on 27 September 2026 with the theme 'Digital Agenda and Artificial Intelligence to Redesign Tourism'; El Salvador hosted the global celebrations. In New Delhi, Tourism Minister Gajendra Singh Shekhawat launched the National Digital Tourism Stack at Bharat Mandapam, built with ONDC, which aims to bring 23,000 offerings (14,000 experiences and 9,000 homestays) online by the end of 2028.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • UN Tourism (formerly UNWTO) is headquartered in Madrid, Spain.

Chess Olympiad: India win Open silver and Women's bronze, retain Gaprindashvili Trophy

The 46th Chess Olympiad ended in Samarkand, Uzbekistan, on 27 September 2026. India's men won silver in the Open section after a final-round draw with Hungary, and the women won bronze after drawing with Georgia. Hosts Uzbekistan won Open gold and China won the Women's gold; India retained the Nona Gaprindashvili Trophy for the best combined performance for the third straight edition.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • FIDE, the world chess body, was founded in 1924 in Paris; it is now based in Lausanne, Switzerland.

Foundation stone laid for India's first port-based e-methanol plant at Kandla

Union Ports Minister Sarbananda Sonowal, Gujarat CM Bhupendra Patel and Assam CM Himanta Biswa Sarma laid the foundation stone of India's first port-based e-methanol plant at Deendayal Port, Kandla (Kutch), on 26 September 2026. The ₹2,300 crore plant of 150 tonnes per day capacity is being set up by Deendayal Port Authority and Assam Petro-Chemicals Ltd to supply green fuel for ships, and is expected to create over 3,500 jobs.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Methanol (CH3OH) is the simplest alcohol; 'e-methanol' is made from green hydrogen and captured carbon dioxide.
  • Deendayal Port at Kandla in Gujarat's Kutch district was developed after Partition to replace Karachi port.

AFSPA extended for six months in parts of Manipur, Nagaland and Arunachal

The Union Home Ministry has extended the Armed Forces (Special Powers) Act, 1958 in notified 'disturbed areas' of Manipur, Nagaland and Arunachal Pradesh for six months, from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027. In Arunachal it covers Tirap, Changlang and Longding districts and parts of Namsai; in Manipur, areas under 13 police stations in five districts stay outside the disturbed-area declaration.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Section 3 of AFSPA lets the government declare an area 'disturbed'.
  • The capital of Nagaland is Kohima and the capital of Arunachal Pradesh is Itanagar.

देश की पहली LNG से चलने वाली ट्रेन साबरमती से रवाना

केंद्रीय गृह मंत्री अमित शाह ने 27 सितंबर 2026 को अहमदाबाद के साबरमती रेलवे स्टेशन से देश की पहली LNG से चलने वाली ट्रेन को हरी झंडी दिखाई। इसकी दो 1,400 HP की ड्राइविंग पावर कारें डीज़ल-LNG दोहरे ईंधन सिस्टम पर चलती हैं, जो लगभग 40% डीज़ल की जगह ले सकता है; इसका 2,000 किमी से ज़्यादा परीक्षण हुआ। उन्होंने ₹1,472 करोड़ की रेल परियोजनाओं का भी उद्घाटन किया, जिनमें ₹700 करोड़ का WAG-12 लोको मेंटेनेंस शेड और साबरमती–कलोल–साबरमती खंड पर कवच (₹48 करोड़) शामिल हैं।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • LNG का पूरा नाम लिक्विफ़ाइड नेचुरल गैस (द्रवीकृत प्राकृतिक गैस) है; यह डीज़ल से साफ़ जलती है और CO2, NOx व कणीय प्रदूषण घटाती है।
  • कवच भारत की स्वदेशी स्वचालित ट्रेन सुरक्षा प्रणाली है, जिसे RDSO (लखनऊ) ने विकसित किया है।
