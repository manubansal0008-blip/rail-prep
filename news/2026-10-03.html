Forex reserves fall $18.3 billion to $747.56 billion

India's foreign exchange reserves fell by $18.343 billion to $747.557 billion in the week ended 25 September 2026, as per RBI data released on 2 October 2026. Foreign currency assets fell $15.57 billion to $615.411 billion and gold reserves fell $2.591 billion to $108.701 billion. International5/5

WHO: global life expectancy back to 73.3 years

The World Health Organization's Global Health Estimates, reported on 2 October 2026, show that global life expectancy recovered to 73.3 years in 2023, almost back to the pre-COVID level of 73.4 years in 2019. Healthy life expectancy was 62.8 years. Noncommunicable diseases caused 74% of deaths in 2023, and ischaemic heart disease remained the leading cause of death, with about 9.5 million deaths.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • The WHO is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland.
