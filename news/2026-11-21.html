Karnataka beat Railways in the final to win its first Senior Women's T20 Trophy title

Diving Support Craft 'DSC A21', built by Titagarh Rail Systems, delivered to the Indian Navy

On 20 November 2026, 'DSC A21' (Yard 326) of the Diving Support Craft project was delivered to the Indian Navy at the Naval Dockyard, Mumbai. Botswana-born cheetah gives birth to four cubs in the wild; India's cheetah count rises to 62

On 20 November 2026, KOMG 3, a two-and-a-half-year-old female cheetah brought from Botswana, gave birth to four cubs in the wild under Project Cheetah, Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav said. She is the first Botswana cheetah to give birth in India. With the new cubs, India's cheetah population has risen to 62, including 43 born in India.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • This is only the second recorded cheetah birth in the wild since Project Cheetah began in 2022
  • Cheetahs were brought to India from Namibia, South Africa and Botswana; first released at Kuno National Park (Madhya Pradesh)

  4. 72
Show answer

Source: PIB (Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change), 20 Nov 2026

Economy / Banking4/5

Index of Core Industries grows 10.9% in October 2026; iron ore leads with 26.4%

On 20 November 2026, the Commerce Ministry said the Index of Core Industries (ICI, base year 2022-23) grew by 10.9% in October 2026 compared with October 2025, up from 8.2% in September 2026. Iron ore grew the fastest (26.4%), followed by coal (23.1%), electricity (21.6%) and cement (15.4%), while natural gas, crude oil and fertilizers fell.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The new ICI series (base 2022-23) has nine industries; electricity has the biggest weight (30.932)
  • ICI growth for April–October 2026: 6.0% (provisional)

Quick question: Which core industry recorded the highest year-on-year growth in October 2026 as per the Index of Core Industries?

  1. Coal
  2. Electricity
  3. Cement
  4. Iron ore
Show answer

(D) Iron ore In October 2026, iron ore grew 26.4%, the highest among core industries, ahead of coal (23.1%), electricity (21.6%) and cement (15.4%). The overall index grew 10.9%.

Source: PIB (Ministry of Commerce & Industry), 20 Nov 2026
