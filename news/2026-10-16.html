Navy commissions INS Samarthak, its first multi-purpose vessel

Vice-President C. P. Radhakrishnan commissioned INS Samarthak into the Indian Navy at Naval Base Karwar (INS Kadamba), Karnataka, on 16 October 2026. The 107-metre ship was built by Larsen & Toubro at Kattupalli, Tamil Nadu, with over 75% indigenous content. It will be used for surveillance, patrol, testing of weapons and sensors, support of unmanned systems and disaster relief.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • INS Samarthak is the Indian Navy's first Multi-Purpose Vessel (MPV) and the first of four such ships.
  • Naval Base Karwar (INS Kadamba) is on the Karnataka coast and was developed under Project Seabird.

Show answer

(C) Larsen & Toubro INS Samarthak, the Navy's first Multi-Purpose Vessel, was built by Larsen & Toubro at Kattupalli, Tamil Nadu, and commissioned at Karwar on 16 October 2026.

States3/5

Sukhna Wetland and Sauj Jheel become Ramsar sites; India now has 103

Union Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav announced on 15 October 2026 that Sukhna Wetland in Chandigarh and Sauj Jheel in Mainpuri district, Uttar Pradesh, have been added to the Ramsar list of wetlands of international importance. This takes India's number of Ramsar sites to 103.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Mainpuri is the only district of Uttar Pradesh with two Ramsar sites: Sauj Jheel and Saman Bird Sanctuary.
  • The Ramsar Convention on wetlands was signed in Ramsar, Iran, in 1971.

Quick question: Sauj Jheel, declared a Ramsar site in October 2026, is in which state?

  1. Haryana
  2. Punjab
  3. Rajasthan
  4. Uttar Pradesh
Show answer

(D) Uttar Pradesh Sauj Jheel is in Mainpuri district, Uttar Pradesh. It and Chandigarh's Sukhna Wetland took India's Ramsar sites to 103.

Source: ThePrint (PTI), 15 Oct 2026 · GKToday, 16 Oct 2026

Economy / Banking4/5

PLFS: unemployment rate steady at 5.0% in September 2026

The monthly Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) bulletin released on 15 October 2026 put India's unemployment rate (age 15+, current weekly status) at 5.0% for September 2026, the same as August. The rate was 4.2% in rural areas and 6.9% in urban areas, while urban women's labour force participation rose to 25.9%.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • PLFS is conducted by the National Statistical Office (NSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI).
  • The labour force participation rate (age 15+) was 55.5% in September 2026.

  3. Ministry of Finance

PLFS: सितंबर 2026 में बेरोज़गारी दर 5.0% पर स्थिर

15 अक्टूबर 2026 को जारी आवधिक श्रम बल सर्वेक्षण (PLFS) के मासिक बुलेटिन के अनुसार सितंबर 2026 में देश की बेरोज़गारी दर (15 वर्ष से ऊपर, वर्तमान साप्ताहिक स्थिति) 5.0% रही, जो अगस्त के बराबर है। गाँवों में यह 4.2% और शहरों में 6.9% रही, जबकि शहरी महिलाओं की श्रम बल भागीदारी बढ़कर 25.9% हो गई।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • PLFS सांख्यिकी और कार्यक्रम कार्यान्वयन मंत्रालय (MoSPI) के अधीन राष्ट्रीय सांख्यिकी कार्यालय (NSO) करता है।
  • सितंबर 2026 में श्रम बल भागीदारी दर (15 वर्ष से ऊपर) 55.5% रही।

छोटा सवाल: आवधिक श्रम बल सर्वेक्षण (PLFS) का बुलेटिन कौन-सा मंत्रालय जारी करता है?

  1. श्रम और रोज़गार मंत्रालय
  2. सांख्यिकी और कार्यक्रम कार्यान्वयन मंत्रालय
  3. वित्त मंत्रालय
  4. नीति आयोग
उत्तर देखें

(B) सांख्यिकी और कार्यक्रम कार्यान्वयन मंत्रालय PLFS को MoSPI के अधीन NSO करता है। 15 अक्टूबर 2026 के बुलेटिन में सितंबर 2026 की बेरोज़गारी दर 5.0% रही।
