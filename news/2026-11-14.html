Ambekar Meetei and Gunjan win India's first-ever boxing gold medals at the Youth Olympics

On 12 November 2026, at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games in Senegal, India's Lairenlakpam Ambekar Meetei (men's 50 kg) and Gunjan (women's 48 kg) won gold medals. These are India's first boxing golds in Youth Olympics history. Ambekar beat Ireland's Kristian Jubani 3–2 and Gunjan beat Algeria's Bouthaina Bensalah 5–0. They were also India's first two golds at Dakar 2026.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Gunjan is the first Indian woman to win a boxing gold at the Youth Olympics
  • India's earlier best in Youth Olympic boxing: Shiva Thapa's silver at Singapore 2010

  1. Nanjing
  2. Singapore
  3. Dakar
  4. Buenos Aires
Show answer

(C) Dakar The 2026 Youth Olympic Games are being held in Dakar, Senegal. Ambekar Meetei (50 kg) and Gunjan (48 kg) won India's first-ever Youth Olympic boxing golds there on 12 November 2026. Singapore (2010), Nanjing (2014) and Buenos Aires (2018) hosted earlier Summer Youth Olympics.

Source: The Hindu, 12 Nov 2026 · Olympics.com, Nov 2026

Indian contingent leaves for 12th India–Sri Lanka Army exercise 'Mitra Shakti 2026' at Maduru Oya

On 13 November 2026, a 170-member Indian Defence Forces contingent left for Sri Lanka for the 12th edition of the India–Sri Lanka joint military exercise 'Mitra Shakti 2026'. PM Modi inaugurates COSMOS-Mysuru, India's first LED dome planetarium

India's total exports hit a record of about US$ 90 billion in October 2026, up 29.9%

Data released by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry on 13 November 2026 show India's total exports (goods + services) in October 2026 at an estimated US$ 89.99 billion, 29.90% more than in October 2025. Merchandise exports were US$ 49.47 billion and services exports an estimated US$ 40.51 billion. Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal called it an all-time high. Total exports for April–October 2026-27 were about US$ 568.84 billion, up 16.66%.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • October 2026: merchandise US$ 49.47 bn + services US$ 40.51 bn (estimated)
  • Engineering goods exports rose 36.01% to US$ 12.74 bn
