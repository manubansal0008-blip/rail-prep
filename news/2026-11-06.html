DRDO flight-tests indigenous quasi-ballistic missile Pralay off Odisha coast

The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) on 5 November 2026 successfully flight-tested the indigenously developed quasi-ballistic missile Pralay from Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island off the Odisha coast at about 11:10 a.m. The test was part of user evaluation trials; all objectives were met, as confirmed by range instruments of the Integrated Test Range, Chandipur. Research Centre Imarat, Hyderabad, led the development with other DRDO labs, and Bharat Dynamics Limited produced the system; induction is now said to be imminent.

Government scheme3/5

Cabinet approves ₹2,814 crore CAPEX scheme for lithium and nickel processing

The Union Cabinet on 4 November 2026 approved the CAPEX Support Scheme to Promote Critical Mineral Processing, detailed by PIB on 5 November 2026, with an outlay of ₹2,814 crore from FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31. It offers a 30% capital subsidy to set up two lithium plants (at least 30 kt each) and two nickel plants (at least 50 kt each), expected to anchor about ₹10,000 crore of investment. Support is capped at ₹500 crore per lithium beneficiary and ₹900 crore per nickel beneficiary.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • India has identified 24 critical minerals, including lithium, nickel, cobalt, graphite and rare earths.
  • The scheme is administered under the Ministry of Mines with a three-tier governance structure.

  4. ₹2,814 crore
