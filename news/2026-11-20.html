Economy / Banking3/5

EPFO board cuts minimum PF balance to 10% and approves a Systematic Withdrawal Plan for retirees

On 19 November 2026, the 240th meeting of the Central Board of Trustees (CBT) of EPF, chaired by Labour Minister Dr. Mansukh Mandaviya in New Delhi, reduced the minimum balance that members must keep in their EPF account from 25% to 10%. The waiting period for final PF withdrawal was cut from 12 months to 2 months. The Board also approved a Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) so that retiring members can take their PF money in monthly, quarterly or half-yearly instalments.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • EPF minimum balance: 25% → 10%; waiting period for final withdrawal: 12 months → 2 months
  • CBT of EPF is chaired by the Union Labour & Employment Minister

India's first indigenous H5N1 bird flu vaccine for chickens, made by ICAR-NIHSAD Bhopal, to be dedicated to the nation

Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan is to dedicate India's first indigenous inactivated H5N1 (Clade 2.3.2.1a) bird flu vaccine for chickens to the nation on 20 November 2026 at ICAR-National Institute of High Security Animal Diseases (ICAR-NIHSAD), Bhopal, which developed it. Bird flu (Highly Pathogenic Avian Influenza) can kill nearly 100% of infected birds.

  • EPF के केंद्रीय न्यासी बोर्ड (CBT) के अध्यक्ष केंद्रीय श्रम एवं रोज़गार मंत्री होते हैं
