Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY completes 8 years

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY) completed eight years on 23 September 2026 and now covers more than 60 crore beneficiaries. The scheme gives health cover of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation. It is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme funded 60:40 by the Centre and States (90:10 for the North-Eastern states, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand and J&K).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • PM Narendra Modi launched AB PM-JAY on 23 September 2018 in Ranchi, Jharkhand.
  • The scheme is implemented by the National Health Authority (NHA); since 2024 all senior citizens aged 70 and above are covered regardless of income.

  4. ₹5 lakh

(D) ₹5 lakh PM-JAY gives up to ₹5 lakh health cover per family per year; it completed eight years on 23 September 2026 and covers more than 60 crore beneficiaries.

Visegrád Group backs India's bid for permanent UNSC seat at first V4–India ministers' meeting

The Visegrád Group (V4) — the Czech Republic, Hungary, Poland and Slovakia — supported India's bid for permanent membership of the UN Security Council at the first V4–India Foreign Ministers' Meeting, held on the sidelines of the 81st UN General Assembly in New York, as reported on 24 September 2026. The two sides agreed to hold foreign ministers' meetings and a business forum every year. They also agreed to widen cooperation in technology, defence, space, connectivity, research and education.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Visegrád Group was formed on 15 February 1991 at Visegrád in Hungary; all four members are in the European Union and NATO.
  • The UN Security Council has 15 members: 5 permanent members with veto power and 10 non-permanent members elected for two-year terms.

  2. Austria
  3. Hungary
  4. Slovakia
Show answer

(B) Austria The V4 consists of the Czech Republic, Hungary, Poland and Slovakia; Austria is not a member.

Economy / Banking3/5

RBI revises valuation norms for REIT and InvIT units held by banks

The Reserve Bank of India issued revised directions on 22 September 2026, effective immediately, on how regulated entities must value units of Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) and Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs) they hold. The rules apply to All India Financial Institutions, commercial banks, small finance banks, payments banks and local area banks. Separately, India's REIT market has reached US$17.7 billion, overtaking Hong Kong to become Asia's fourth-largest.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • SEBI notified the REIT Regulations and the InvIT Regulations in 2014; India's first listed REIT, Embassy Office Parks, came in 2019.
  • The Reserve Bank of India was established on 1 April 1935 and is headquartered in Mumbai.

Quick question: In REIT and InvIT, for which RBI revised valuation norms in September 2026, what does 'InvIT' stand for?

  1. Infrastructure Investment Trust
  2. Investment in Value-added Industrial Trust
  3. Indian Venture Investment Trust
  4. Integrated Valuation of Investment Trust
Show answer

(A) Infrastructure Investment Trust InvIT means Infrastructure Investment Trust; REIT means Real Estate Investment Trust. Both are regulated by SEBI.

Asian Games: Indian shooters win bronze in men's and women's skeet team events

Indian shooters won two bronze medals in the skeet team events at the 20th Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, on 23 September 2026. The women's team of Parinaaz Dhaliwal, Raiza Dhillon and Maheshwari Chauhan and the men's team of Anantjeet Singh Naruka, Bhavtegh Singh Gill and Mairaj Ahmad Khan both finished third. Summits / Days5/5

Birth anniversary of Madam Bhikaji Cama on 24 September

24 September 2026 marks the 165th birth anniversary of Madam Bhikaji Rustom Cama, the freedom fighter born on this day in 1861 in Bombay. She is best remembered for unfurling an early version of India's national flag at the International Socialist Congress in Stuttgart, Germany, in 1907. 24 September is also observed as the birth anniversary of Guru Ram Das, the fourth Sikh Guru.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Bhikaji Cama hoisted the flag in Stuttgart on 22 August 1907; it carried the words 'Vande Mataram'.
  • Guru Ram Das (born 1534) founded the city of Amritsar.

Quick question: Madam Bhikaji Cama, born on 24 September 1861, unfurled an early Indian national flag in 1907 in which city?

  1. London
  2. Paris
  3. Berlin
  4. Stuttgart
Show answer

(D) Stuttgart She unfurled the flag at the International Socialist Congress in Stuttgart, Germany, on 22 August 1907.

RBI ने बैंकों के पास मौजूद REIT और InvIT यूनिट्स के मूल्यांकन नियम बदले

भारतीय रिज़र्व बैंक ने 22 सितंबर 2026 को तत्काल प्रभाव से संशोधित निर्देश जारी किए कि विनियमित संस्थाएँ अपने पास रखी रियल एस्टेट इन्वेस्टमेंट ट्रस्ट (REIT) और इन्फ्रास्ट्रक्चर इन्वेस्टमेंट ट्रस्ट (InvIT) की यूनिट्स का मूल्यांकन कैसे करेंगी। ये नियम अखिल भारतीय वित्तीय संस्थानों, वाणिज्यिक बैंकों, लघु वित्त बैंकों, पेमेंट्स बैंकों और लोकल एरिया बैंकों पर लागू होंगे। इसके अलावा भारत का REIT बाज़ार 17.7 अरब अमेरिकी डॉलर का हो गया है और हांगकांग को पीछे छोड़कर एशिया का चौथा सबसे बड़ा बाज़ार बन गया है।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • SEBI ने REIT और InvIT विनियम 2014 में अधिसूचित किए थे; भारत का पहला सूचीबद्ध REIT, एम्बेसी ऑफ़िस पार्क्स, 2019 में आया।
  • भारतीय रिज़र्व बैंक की स्थापना 1 अप्रैल 1935 को हुई थी और इसका मुख्यालय मुंबई में है।

छोटा सवाल: सितंबर 2026 में RBI ने REIT और InvIT के मूल्यांकन नियम बदले। 'InvIT' का पूरा नाम क्या है?
