World Ozone Day observed with the theme 'Global Action for a Cooler Planet'

The International Day for the Preservation of the Ozone Layer (World Ozone Day) is being observed on 16 September 2026 with the theme 'Global Action for a Cooler Planet'. The year marks 10 years of the Kigali Amendment to the Montreal Protocol, adopted in 2016. At the national programme for the 32nd World Ozone Day in Mumbai, Minister of State for Environment Kirti Vardhan Singh released the 28th edition of 'The Montreal Protocol: India's Success Story' and launched the Net Zero Portal and the NAPCC Dashboard.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Montreal Protocol was agreed on 16 September 1987 and came into force on 1 January 1989; the UN General Assembly declared 16 September as Ozone Day in 1994.
  • The Kigali Amendment (Kigali, Rwanda, 2016) phases down hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs); India ratified it in 2021.

Kumik in Zanskar declared Ladakh's first Heritage Village

Ladakh Lieutenant Governor Vinai Kumar Saxena declared Kumik village in Zanskar as Ladakh's first Heritage Village on 15 September 2026, during the Ladakh Zanskar Festival. The village is known for its traditional mud architecture, sacred spaces, farming traditions and Kanjur (Kangyur) manuscripts of Tibetan Buddhist scriptures. Falling water availability has led many families to leave the village.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Ladakh became a Union Territory without a legislature on 31 October 2019 under the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019; Leh and Kargil are its main towns.
  • In 2026 Ladakh created five new districts, including Zanskar, taking its total from two to seven.

₹3,406 crore silicon carbide chip unit cleared for Odisha's Khordha district

The proposal of SiCSem Private Limited to set up a silicon carbide (SiC) semiconductor unit at IDCO Infovalley-II in Khordha district, Odisha, was approved at the 217th meeting of the Unit Approval Committee, as announced on 15 September 2026. The ₹3,406.25 crore plant on 21.889 acres will make 4.8 million SiC diodes and 91.2 million SiC MOSFETs a year and create about 1,270 jobs. It is Odisha's second semiconductor unit.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Silicon carbide chips handle high power and heat, and are used in electric vehicles, renewable energy, railways and defence electronics.
  • The India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) works under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY); Odisha's capital is Bhubaneswar.

22nd India–US exercise Yudh Abhyas begins at Auli and Mahajan

The 22nd edition of the India–US joint military exercise Yudh Abhyas began on 15 September 2026 at the Auli Foreign Training Node in Uttarakhand, with activities running at the same time at the Mahajan Field Firing Range in Rajasthan. About 600 soldiers from each army are taking part. The exercise focuses on using Integrated Battle Groups in mountain and semi-mountain terrain, along with drones and autonomous systems.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Yudh Abhyas has been held every year since 2004, alternately in India and the USA; the 2025 edition was held at Fort Wainwright, Alaska.
  • Auli is in Chamoli district of Uttarakhand; Mahajan Field Firing Range is in Bikaner district of Rajasthan.

16 सितंबर 2026 को ओज़ोन परत संरक्षण का अंतरराष्ट्रीय दिवस (विश्व ओज़ोन दिवस) 'ग्लोबल एक्शन फॉर अ कूलर प्लैनेट' (Global Action for a Cooler Planet) थीम के साथ मनाया जा रहा है। इस साल 2016 में अपनाए गए मॉन्ट्रियल प्रोटोकॉल के किगाली संशोधन के 10 साल पूरे हो रहे हैं। मुंबई में 32वें विश्व ओज़ोन दिवस के राष्ट्रीय कार्यक्रम में पर्यावरण राज्य मंत्री कीर्ति वर्धन सिंह ने 'द मॉन्ट्रियल प्रोटोकॉल: इंडियाज़ सक्सेस स्टोरी' का 28वां संस्करण जारी किया और नेट ज़ीरो पोर्टल व NAPCC डैशबोर्ड की शुरुआत की।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • मॉन्ट्रियल प्रोटोकॉल पर 16 सितंबर 1987 को सहमति बनी और यह 1 जनवरी 1989 से लागू हुआ; संयुक्त राष्ट्र महासभा ने 1994 में 16 सितंबर को ओज़ोन दिवस घोषित किया।
  • किगाली संशोधन (किगाली, रवांडा, 2016) हाइड्रोफ्लोरोकार्बन (HFC) के उपयोग को चरणबद्ध रूप से घटाता है; भारत ने 2021 में इसका अनुमोदन किया।
