Varanasi–New Delhi Vande Bharat Sleeper Express starts

Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw and UP Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath flag off new Vande Bharat Sleeper Express services between Varanasi and New Delhi from Varanasi on 31 October 2026. The overnight train stops at Prayagraj, Kanpur Central, Aligarh and Ghaziabad and runs six days a week.

Science / Space2/5

ISRO's GSLV-F18 launches navigation satellite NVS-03

ISRO's GSLV-F18 placed the NVS-03 satellite in orbit on 30 October 2026 at 23:51 from the Second Launch Pad, Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota. NVS-03 is the third next-generation satellite for India's navigation system NavIC and carries an indigenous atomic clock.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • GSLV-F18 was the 20th flight of the GSLV rocket.
  • NavIC (IRNSS) is India's own satellite navigation system; NVS-03 adds the L1 signal along with L5 and S band.

Quick question: NVS-03, launched by ISRO on 30 October 2026, belongs to which satellite system?

  1. GSAT
  2. NavIC
  3. RISAT
  4. Cartosat
Show answer

(B) NavIC NVS-03 is the third next-generation satellite of NavIC, India's regional navigation system (IRNSS). It was launched on GSLV-F18.

Source: ISRO, NVS-03 orbit update, 31 Oct 2026 · ISRO, GSLV-F18/NVS-03 mission page

Summits / Days5/5

Rashtriya Ekta Diwas: 151st birth anniversary of Sardar Patel

India observed Rashtriya Ekta Diwas (National Unity Day) on 31 October 2026, the 151st birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. The Prime Minister paid tributes at the Statue of Unity in Ekta Nagar, Gujarat, and the Union Home Minister flagged off the 'Run for Unity' in New Delhi.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Patel was born on 31 October 1875 in Nadiad, Gujarat; he was India's first Home Minister and joined more than 560 princely states with India.
  • 31 October has been observed as Rashtriya Ekta Diwas since 2014.

  3. Subhas Chandra Bose
  4. Dr. B. R. Ambedkar
Show answer

(B) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel 31 October is the birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (born 1875, Nadiad). It has been observed as Rashtriya Ekta Diwas since 2014.

Source: The Hindu, 31 Oct 2026 · PIB (Ministry of Home Affairs), 31 Oct 2026

ISRO के GSLV-F18 ने नेविगेशन सैटेलाइट NVS-03 लॉन्च किया

ISRO के GSLV-F18 रॉकेट ने 30 अक्टूबर 2026 को रात 23:51 बजे श्रीहरिकोटा के सतीश धवन अंतरिक्ष केंद्र के दूसरे लॉन्च पैड से NVS-03 सैटेलाइट को कक्षा में पहुँचाया। NVS-03 भारत के नेविगेशन सिस्टम NavIC का तीसरा नई पीढ़ी का सैटेलाइट है, जिसमें स्वदेशी परमाणु घड़ी लगी है।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • GSLV-F18, GSLV रॉकेट की 20वीं उड़ान थी।
  • NavIC (IRNSS) भारत का अपना सैटेलाइट नेविगेशन सिस्टम है; NVS-03 में L5 और S बैंड के साथ L1 सिग्नल भी है।

छोटा सवाल: 30 अक्टूबर 2026 को ISRO द्वारा लॉन्च किया गया NVS-03 किस सैटेलाइट सिस्टम का हिस्सा है?

  1. GSAT
  2. NavIC
  3. RISAT
  4. Cartosat
उत्तर देखें

(B) NavIC NVS-03 भारत के क्षेत्रीय नेविगेशन सिस्टम NavIC (IRNSS) का तीसरा नई पीढ़ी का सैटेलाइट है। इसे GSLV-F18 से लॉन्च किया गया।

स्रोत: ISRO, NVS-03 कक्षा अपडेट, 31 अक्टूबर 2026 · ISRO, GSLV-F18/NVS-03 मिशन पेज
