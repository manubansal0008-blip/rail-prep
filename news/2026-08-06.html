Railways begins shifting trains to upgraded, cloud-based Passenger Reservation System

Indian Railways has begun the phased migration of trains to its upgraded, cloud-enabled Passenger Reservation System (PRS) in August 2026, according to a PIB backgrounder released on 6 August 2026 to mark four decades of computerised reservation. The new PRS is expected to handle over 1.5 lakh ticket bookings per minute (against 32,000 now) and over 40 lakh enquiries per minute (against 4 lakh), with a multilingual interface. Between June 2025 and June 2026, 65.08 crore reserved tickets were booked, 89% of them online.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Computerised passenger reservation began on Indian Railways in 1986; the existing PRS, deployed in 2010, runs on Itanium servers and the OpenVMS platform.
  • The PRS is run by CRIS (Centre for Railway Information Systems), set up in 1986 with headquarters in New Delhi; the RailOne app, launched on 1 July 2025, had over 4.55 crore downloads by 22 July 2026.

(C) Over 1.5 lakh The new cloud-based PRS will handle over 1.5 lakh bookings per minute against 32,000 now; 40 lakh is the enquiry capacity.

10th BRICS Industry Ministers' Meeting in Jaipur adopts joint declaration

The Tenth BRICS Industry Ministers' Meeting concluded in Jaipur, Rajasthan, on 6 August 2026 under India's BRICS Chairship, with members adopting a Joint Declaration on industrial cooperation under the BRICS Partnership on New Industrial Revolution (PartNIR). Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal chaired the meeting, whose theme was 'Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability'. It agreed a framework for a BRICS SME Working Group and a photovoltaic industry action plan, and India proposed a BRICS Incubator Network and Startup Innovation Fund.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The first BRIC summit was held in Yekaterinburg, Russia, in 2009; India holds the BRICS Chairship in 2026 after Brazil (2025).
  • The BRICS-led New Development Bank has its headquarters in Shanghai, China; Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan.

Sqn Ldr Bhawana Kanth becomes IAF's first woman Fighter Combat Leader ('Top Gun')

Squadron Leader Bhawana Kanth has become the first woman fighter pilot of the Indian Air Force to complete the Fighter Combat Leader (FCL) course, it was reported on 6 August 2026. The demanding five-month course is run by the Tactics and Air Combat Development Establishment (TACDE) in Gwalior, the IAF's 'Top Gun' school. Kanth, who hails from Darbhanga in Bihar and flies the Su-30 MKI, was commissioned in June 2016 in the IAF's first batch of women fighter pilots.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The first three women fighter pilots of the IAF, commissioned in June 2016, were Bhawana Kanth, Avani Chaturvedi and Mohana Singh.
  • TACDE is located at Gwalior Air Force Station, Madhya Pradesh; the Indian Air Force was founded on 8 October 1932.

Hiroshima Day: 81 years since the first atomic bombing

Hiroshima Day was observed on 6 August 2026, marking 81 years since the United States dropped the atomic bomb 'Little Boy' on the Japanese city of Hiroshima on 6 August 1945. The bomb was dropped from the B-29 bomber Enola Gay; an estimated 70,000–80,000 people died instantly and about 1,40,000 by the end of 1945. The day is observed to promote peace and a world free of nuclear weapons.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • A second atomic bomb, 'Fat Man', was dropped on Nagasaki on 9 August 1945; Japan announced its surrender on 15 August 1945.
  • The Hiroshima Peace Memorial (Genbaku Dome) is a UNESCO World Heritage Site; the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT) came into force in 1970, and India has not signed it.
