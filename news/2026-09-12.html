India sets Guinness World Record for world's longest steel slag road in Chhattisgarh

A 1.85 km four-lane road at Raigarh, Chhattisgarh, built with processed steel slag instead of natural stone aggregate, has entered the Guinness World Records as the world's longest steel slag road, Science and Technology Minister Dr Jitendra Singh said at the record conferment ceremony in New Delhi on 12 September 2026. The technology was developed by CSIR-Central Road Research Institute (CSIR-CRRI) and the road was built by Jindal Steel. The minister said the method can cut road construction costs by about 40%.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Steel slag is a by-product of steel making; using it saves mined stone and solves a waste-disposal problem.
  • CSIR-CRRI is in New Delhi; the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) was set up in 1942.

  2. Raigarh
  3. Korba
  4. Bilaspur
Show answer

(B) Raigarh The four-lane steel slag road is at Raigarh; the technology was developed by CSIR-CRRI.

Centre and Jharkhand sign ₹1,684 crore agreement under Amended BharatNet Programme

Digital Bharat Nidhi (DBN) of the Department of Telecommunications signed an agreement with the Government of Jharkhand at Sanchar Bhawan, New Delhi, to implement the Amended BharatNet Programme in the state under the State-led model, as reported on 12 September 2026. The Government of India will provide ₹1,684 crore. The project covers 4,395 Gram Panchayats, upgrading 4,387 of them from linear to ring topology, and will offer on-demand connectivity to about 26,777 villages through the SPV Jharkhand Digital Infrastructure Corporation Limited (JDICL).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • BharatNet began in 2011 as the National Optical Fibre Network (NOFN) and was renamed BharatNet in 2015.
  • Digital Bharat Nidhi replaced the Universal Service Obligation Fund (USOF) under the Telecommunications Act, 2023; Jharkhand was formed on 15 November 2000 with Ranchi as its capital.

NSO releases first district-level estimates of the unincorporated non-farm sector

The National Statistics Office (NSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation released 'Unincorporated Sector at the District Level: Insights from ASUSE 2025', the first district-level estimates from the Annual Survey of Unincorporated Sector Enterprises, as reported on 12 September 2026. The survey covers January–December 2025. The top 10 districts account for about one-tenth and the top 50 districts for about one-third of all establishments, workers and gross value added (GVA); the top 10 districts are in Gujarat, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal.

PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana completes seven years

The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY), a pension scheme for small and marginal farmers, completes seven years on 12 September 2026; it was launched on 12 September 2019 in Ranchi. Farmers aged 18 to 40 years with up to two hectares of land can join and get a pension of ₹3,000 a month after the age of 60, with the farmer and the Centre contributing equally. About 24.96 lakh farmers had enrolled by February 2026.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • If the subscriber dies, the spouse gets a family pension of ₹1,500 a month.
  • The Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) is the pension fund manager for the scheme.

Saragarhi Day: 129 years since the last stand of 21 Sikh soldiers

Saragarhi Day is observed on 12 September 2026 to honour the 21 soldiers of the 36th Sikhs who defended the Saragarhi signalling post on the North-West Frontier against thousands of tribesmen on 12 September 1897. Led by Havildar Ishar Singh, they fought to the last man. 12 September is also the UN Day for South-South Cooperation, with the 2026 theme 'Solidarity Rising: Global Alliance for Inclusive and Innovative Development'.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • All 21 soldiers were posthumously awarded the Indian Order of Merit, then the highest gallantry award for Indian soldiers; the 36th Sikhs is today the 4th Battalion of the Sikh Regiment.
  • Saragarhi is in present-day Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan; it was a post linking Fort Lockhart and Fort Gulistan.

Quick question: Who led the 21 soldiers of the 36th Sikhs in the Battle of Saragarhi on 12 September 1897?

  1. Havildar Ishar Singh
  2. Subedar Joginder Singh
  3. Naik Jadunath Singh
  4. Sepoy Kartar Singh
Show answer

(A) Havildar Ishar Singh Havildar Ishar Singh led the defence of the Saragarhi post; all 21 soldiers got the Indian Order of Merit posthumously.

छत्तीसगढ़ के रायगढ़ में प्राकृतिक पत्थर की गिट्टी की जगह प्रसंस्कृत स्टील स्लैग से बनी 1.85 किलोमीटर लंबी चार-लेन सड़क दुनिया की सबसे लंबी स्टील स्लैग सड़क के रूप में गिनीज़ वर्ल्ड रिकॉर्ड्स में दर्ज हुई है। विज्ञान एवं प्रौद्योगिकी मंत्री डॉ. जितेंद्र सिंह ने 12 सितंबर 2026 को नई दिल्ली में रिकॉर्ड प्रमाणपत्र समारोह में यह जानकारी दी। यह तकनीक CSIR-केंद्रीय सड़क अनुसंधान संस्थान (CSIR-CRRI) ने विकसित की है और सड़क जिंदल स्टील ने बनाई है; मंत्री के अनुसार इससे सड़क निर्माण की लागत करीब 40% घट सकती है।

  • CSIR-CRRI नई दिल्ली में है; वैज्ञानिक तथा औद्योगिक अनुसंधान परिषद (CSIR) की स्थापना 1942 में हुई थी।
