In a first, Railways to build six freight lines with private firms under Hybrid Annuity Model

Indian Railways will, for the first time, build new freight lines with private firms under the Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM) used for highways, the Indian Express reported on 31 August 2026. Six lines totalling 647 km — four in Odisha and one each in Telangana and Jharkhand — have been recommended by the Public Private Partnership Appraisal Committee (PPPAC) to carry mainly coal and iron ore; the total bid project cost is ₹15,976 crore. Railways will pay 40% of the cost as a grant during construction, and the private partner will finance the remaining 60% and get annuity payments.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Railways shifted these projects to HAM from the earlier DBFOT (Design, Build, Finance, Operate, Transfer) model to attract more private investment.
  • India's first passenger train ran from Bori Bunder (Mumbai) to Thane on 16 April 1853.

Science / Space2/5

Aditya-L1 data reveals early warning signs hours before big solar flares

A study using data from India's solar mission Aditya-L1 has found small, short-lived brightenings in the Sun's atmosphere hours before major solar flares, clustered around the spot where the big flare later erupts, The Hindu reported on 31 August 2026. Scientists from the Manipal Centre for Natural Sciences (Manipal Academy of Higher Education) and ISRO used simultaneous ultraviolet and X-ray observations from the SUIT, SoLEXS and HEL1OS payloads. Such warnings could improve space-weather forecasts that protect satellites, communication systems and astronauts.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Aditya-L1 was launched on 2 September 2023 by PSLV-C57 from Sriharikota and placed in a halo orbit around Lagrange point L1, about 1.5 million km from Earth, on 6 January 2024.
  • SUIT (Solar Ultraviolet Imaging Telescope) on Aditya-L1 was developed by IUCAA, Pune.

Sri Lanka removes legal hurdle to return of civil-war refugees without valid passports

Sri Lanka's Cabinet has removed the legal barrier to the voluntary return of refugees who fled the civil war without valid passports or through unauthorised departure points, the Indian Express reported on 31 August 2026. Refugees whose nationality is established and who left before 19 May 2009, the official end of the conflict, can now return without being prosecuted, after clearance by the State Intelligence Service and entry through an authorised port. About 90,000 Sri Lankan refugees live in India, over 58,000 of them in camps.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Palk Strait separates Tamil Nadu from Sri Lanka; Dhanushkodi was a common landing point for refugees crossing it.
  • Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte is the official (legislative) capital of Sri Lanka, while Colombo is its commercial capital.

Government scheme4/5

All rural job workers shifted to VB-G RAM G; e-KYC not mandatory for work, says ministry

The Ministry of Rural Development said all workers have been shifted to the new Viksit Bharat Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin), or VB-G RAM G, and that e-KYC is not mandatory to get work, The Hindu reported on 31 August 2026. Of about 10.84 crore active workers, 10.27 crore have completed e-KYC and about 57 lakh have not. The scheme, in force since 1 July 2026 under the VB-G RAM G Act, 2025, guarantees 125 days of wage employment a year to every rural household, up from 100 days under MGNREGA.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 repealed the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA), 2005.
  • MGNREGA was first implemented on 2 February 2006 from Bandlapalli in Anantapur district of Andhra Pradesh.

Economy / Banking5/5

India aims to wrap up trade agreement with Chile this year

India plans to conclude a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) with Chile this year, after talks in Santiago between Commerce Secretary Rajesh Agrawal and Chile's Vice-Minister Paula Estévez Weinstein, The Hindu reported on 31 August 2026. The Terms of Reference for the CEPA were signed on 8 May 2025, and the two countries already have a Preferential Trade Agreement in force since 2007 and expanded in 2017. In 2025-26, India's exports to Chile were $1.2 billion while imports from Chile rose 93% to $5 billion.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Santiago is the capital of Chile; Chile is a major world producer of copper and lithium, both important for India's supply-chain security.
  • A CEPA covers trade in goods and services, investment and regulatory cooperation, going beyond a traditional free trade agreement.
