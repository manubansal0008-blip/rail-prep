India–Kazakhstan Army exercise KAZIND-2026 begins in Oskemen

A 60-member Indian Armed Forces contingent left for the 9th edition of the India–Kazakhstan joint military exercise KAZIND-2026, held at Oskemen, Kazakhstan, from 28 September to 11 October 2026. The Indian troops come mainly from a battalion of the Garhwal Rifles, along with Indian Air Force personnel. The exercise focuses on joint counter-terrorism operations in a sub-conventional setting under a UN mandate, including raids, search-and-destroy missions and special heliborne operations.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The first edition of the KAZIND exercise was held in 2016.
  • The capital of Kazakhstan is Astana; the Garhwal Rifles Regimental Centre is at Lansdowne, Uttarakhand.

Pashudhan Bima Portal launched for livestock insurance

The Centre launched the Pashudhan Bima Portal in New Delhi on 28 September 2026 in the presence of Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh. Developed by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, it is a single digital platform for policy issuance, claims, monitoring and reporting under the Livestock Insurance Scheme. Farmers can apply online, and the portal is available in Hindi, English and 11 regional languages.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Livestock insurance is a component of the National Livestock Mission, launched in 2014-15.
  • The Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying is under the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, created in 2019.

Nation remembers Shaheed Bhagat Singh on 119th birth anniversary

The nation paid tribute to Shaheed Bhagat Singh on his 119th birth anniversary on 28 September 2026. Born on 28 September 1907 in Banga (Lyallpur district, now in Pakistan), he founded the Naujawan Bharat Sabha in 1926 and threw bombs in the Central Legislative Assembly with Batukeshwar Dutt in 1929. He was hanged with Rajguru and Sukhdev at Lahore Central Jail on 23 March 1931.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • 23 March is observed as Shaheed Diwas (Martyrs' Day) in memory of Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev.
  • Bhagat Singh was a member of the Hindustan Socialist Republican Association (HSRA), formed in 1928.

  3. Hindustan Republican Association
  4. Naujawan Bharat Sabha
Show answer

(D) Naujawan Bharat Sabha Bhagat Singh founded the Naujawan Bharat Sabha in 1926. His 119th birth anniversary was observed on 28 September 2026.

States4/5

Tamil Nadu launches 'Thai Maman Thanga Mothiram' gold ring scheme for newborns

Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay launched the 'Thai Maman Thanga Mothiram' (maternal uncle's gold ring) scheme at Government Rajaji Hospital, Madurai, on 28 September 2026. Every eligible baby born in a government hospital or primary health centre will get a one-gram, 22-carat gold ring with a unique serial number. Babies born since 22 June 2026 are covered, and mothers must be Tamil Nadu residents registered under PICME.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The capital of Tamil Nadu is Chennai; Madurai stands on the banks of the Vaigai river.
  • PICME stands for Pregnancy and Infant Cohort Monitoring and Evaluation, Tamil Nadu's mother-and-child registration system.

  4. Free health insurance of ₹5 lakh

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • तमिलनाडु की राजधानी चेन्नई है; मदुरै वैगई नदी के किनारे बसा है।
  • PICME का पूरा नाम Pregnancy and Infant Cohort Monitoring and Evaluation है – यह तमिलनाडु की माँ-शिशु पंजीकरण प्रणाली है।

  2. एक ग्राम 22 कैरेट सोने की अंगूठी

  4. ₹5 लाख का मुफ़्त स्वास्थ्य बीमा
उत्तर देखें

(B) एक ग्राम 22 कैरेट सोने की अंगूठी सरकारी अस्पताल में जन्मे हर पात्र शिशु को एक ग्राम, 22 कैरेट सोने की अंगूठी मिलती है; मुख्यमंत्री सी. जोसेफ़ विजय ने मदुरै में यह योजना शुरू की।
