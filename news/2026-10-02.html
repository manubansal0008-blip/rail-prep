Indian women win Asian Games hockey gold after 44 years

India beat China 1-0 in the women's hockey final at the 20th Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, on 2 October 2026. Lalremsiami scored the only goal in the 10th minute. Led by captain Salima Tete, the team also earned direct qualification for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • The Asian Games winner in hockey gets a direct berth at the next Olympic Games (LA 2028).

Nation remembers Mahatma Gandhi on his 157th birth anniversary

The nation paid homage to Mahatma Gandhi on his 157th birth anniversary on 2 October 2026. President Droupadi Murmu attended a Sarva Dharma Prarthana Sabha at Rajghat, and Vice President C P Radhakrishnan, Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla also paid tributes. The day is also the birth anniversary of former Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • Lal Bahadur Shastri was born on 2 October 1904 at Mughalsarai (UP) and gave the slogan 'Jai Jawan Jai Kisan'.

Gross GST collection rises 14.7% to over ₹2.03 lakh crore in September

Gross GST collections in September 2026 rose 14.7% to over ₹2,03,000 crore, against over ₹1,77,000 crore in September 2025, as per data released on 1 October 2026. Revenue from domestic transactions grew over 10% to about ₹1,38,000 crore, while GST on imports rose about 26% to ₹65,525 crore.

📌 Remember for the exam

Science / Space4/5

Indian start-ups' satellites reach orbit on SpaceX Transporter-18

SpaceX's Falcon 9 lifted off on the Transporter-18 rideshare mission from Vandenberg, California, at 12:02 am IST on 2 October 2026 with about 130 payloads, including several from Indian start-ups. Hyderabad-based Dhruva Space's LEAP-2 carries SatLeo Labs' TAPAS-1, called India's first dedicated commercial thermal payload, and TakeMe2Space's MOI-1A is a 14 kg satellite that runs AI models in orbit.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • IN-SPACe (Indian National Space Promotion and Authorization Centre) authorises private space activities in India; it is headquartered in Ahmedabad.
  • A rideshare mission carries satellites of many different customers on a single rocket.

  4. Ariane 6 VA265
Show answer

(C) Transporter-18 LEAP-2 flew on SpaceX's Transporter-18 rideshare mission on Falcon 9 from Vandenberg, California, on 2 October 2026.

Government scheme5/5

Guidelines for National Coastal Mission 2.0 launched

Union Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav launched the guidelines for National Coastal Mission (NCM) 2.0 in New Delhi. The ₹767 crore Central Sector Scheme runs up to 2030-31 and covers nine coastal States and four Union Territories. It focuses on coastal resilience, ecosystem restoration, pollution management, biodiversity conservation, sustainable tourism and spatial planning.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • A Central Sector Scheme is fully funded and implemented by the Central Government.
  • NCM 2.0 is run by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC).

Quick question: How many coastal States are covered under National Coastal Mission 2.0?

  1. Seven
  2. Nine
  3. Eleven
  4. Thirteen
Show answer

(B) Nine NCM 2.0 (₹767 crore, up to 2030-31) covers nine coastal States and four Union Territories.
