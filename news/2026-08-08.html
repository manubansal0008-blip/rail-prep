16th BRICS Trade Ministers' Meeting in Jaipur issues 'Jaipur Consensus'

India hosted the 16th BRICS Trade Ministers' Meeting in Jaipur on 7 August 2026 under its BRICS Chairship, chaired by Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal. Ministers issued the 'Jaipur Consensus', which includes studying a BRICS Invoice Discounting Mechanism to ease trade finance for smaller firms, and adopted a Workplan on Global Value Chains with a GVC Action Plan 2026–2030. India called for a reformed, WTO-centred multilateral trading system and a fully working dispute settlement system.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • BRICS was formed by Brazil, Russia, India and China; South Africa joined in 2011, turning BRIC into BRICS, and the group expanded further from 2024.
  • The World Trade Organization (WTO) was set up on 1 January 1995 and has its headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland.

Centre formally names 27 places in Arunachal Pradesh on official map

The Union Home Ministry on 7 August 2026 formally identified 27 places in Arunachal Pradesh by their standard names on Survey of India maps, in consultation with the State government. The list includes land features, passes, a lake and a monument, such as Long Ju, Thag La, Bisa village, Dzo La and Jairampur. The move counters China's repeated attempts to give its own names to places in the State, which it calls 'Zangnan'; China issued such lists in 2017, 2021 and 2023.

RBI places Tata Sons in Upper Layer of NBFCs for 2026-27

The Reserve Bank of India has classified Tata Sons Private Limited as an Upper Layer NBFC (NBFC-UL) for 2026-27 under its Scale Based Regulation (SBR) framework, it was reported on 8 August 2026. The 2026-27 Upper Layer list has 17 entities, which face closer supervision and bank-like norms. NBFCs placed in the Upper Layer must get listed within three years.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • The Reserve Bank of India was set up on 1 April 1935 under the RBI Act, 1934, and has its headquarters in Mumbai; Tata Sons is the holding company of the Tata Group.
