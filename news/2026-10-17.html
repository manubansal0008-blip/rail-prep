Sankoo becomes Ladakh's eighth district

Ladakh Lieutenant Governor Vinai Kumar Saxena announced the creation of Sankoo district on 16 October 2026, meeting a long-standing local demand. Sankoo is carved out of Kargil district, and Ladakh now has eight districts.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Five new districts – Sham, Nubra, Changthang, Zanskar and Drass – had been notified earlier, taking Ladakh from two districts (Leh, Kargil) to seven.
  • Ladakh became a Union Territory (without a legislature) on 31 October 2019.

India rolls over ₹30 billion currency swap for the Maldives

India approved the Maldives' request to roll over a ₹30 billion currency swap drawdown under the SAARC currency swap framework. On 16 October 2026 Maldivian Foreign Minister Iruthisham Adam thanked External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar for the decision, which supports the Maldives' liquidity and financial stability.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The RBI signed the swap framework with the Maldives Monetary Authority during President Mohamed Muizzu's state visit to India in October 2024.
  • Malé is the capital of the Maldives and its currency is the Maldivian rufiyaa.

Science / Space3/5

ISRO completes Gaganyaan crew module–service module separation tests

ISRO completed the Crew Module–Service Module separation tests for the Gaganyaan mission at the Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre (VSSC), Thiruvananthapuram, on 16 and 17 October 2026. The tests checked that the two modules separate cleanly before the crew module re-enters the atmosphere, and that its avionics and heat shields withstand the shock.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Gaganyaan is India's human spaceflight programme; G1 will be its first uncrewed flight.
  • VSSC, in Thiruvananthapuram (Kerala), is ISRO's lead centre for launch vehicle development.

  4. SAC, Ahmedabad
Show answer

(A) VSSC, Thiruvananthapuram The separation tests were done at the Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre, Thiruvananthapuram, on 16–17 October 2026.

Summits / Days5/5

International Day for the Eradication of Poverty: 'Safe Homes for All'

The International Day for the Eradication of Poverty was observed on 17 October 2026 with the theme 'Safe Homes for All'. This year's focus is adequate housing as a human right; the UN says over 2.8 billion people face some form of inadequate housing.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The UN General Assembly declared 17 October as this day in 1992 (resolution 47/196).
  • It traces back to 17 October 1987, when about 1,00,000 people gathered at the Trocadéro in Paris to honour victims of extreme poverty.

Quick question: On which date is the International Day for the Eradication of Poverty observed?

  1. 16 October
  2. 17 October
  3. 24 October
  4. 10 December
Show answer

(B) 17 October It is observed on 17 October, as declared by the UN General Assembly in 1992. The 2026 theme was 'Safe Homes for All'.

सम्मेलन/दिवस5/5

अंतरराष्ट्रीय गरीबी उन्मूलन दिवस: 'Safe Homes for All'

17 अक्टूबर 2026 को अंतरराष्ट्रीय गरीबी उन्मूलन दिवस 'Safe Homes for All' (सबके लिए सुरक्षित घर) थीम के साथ मनाया गया। इस बार ज़ोर पर्याप्त आवास को मानव अधिकार मानने पर है; संयुक्त राष्ट्र के अनुसार 2.8 अरब से ज़्यादा लोग किसी न किसी तरह की आवास की कमी झेल रहे हैं।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • संयुक्त राष्ट्र महासभा ने 1992 में (प्रस्ताव 47/196) 17 अक्टूबर को यह दिवस घोषित किया।
  • इसकी शुरुआत 17 अक्टूबर 1987 से मानी जाती है, जब पेरिस के ट्रोकाडेरो में करीब 1,00,000 लोग अत्यधिक गरीबी के पीड़ितों को सम्मान देने जुटे थे।

छोटा सवाल: अंतरराष्ट्रीय गरीबी उन्मूलन दिवस किस तारीख को मनाया जाता है?

  1. 16 अक्टूबर
  2. 17 अक्टूबर
  3. 24 अक्टूबर
  4. 10 दिसंबर
उत्तर देखें

(B) 17 अक्टूबर यह 17 अक्टूबर को मनाया जाता है, जिसे संयुक्त राष्ट्र महासभा ने 1992 में घोषित किया था। 2026 की थीम 'Safe Homes for All' थी।
