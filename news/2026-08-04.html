Vishwesh Negi named India's next Ambassador to Iran

Vishwesh Negi, a 2002-batch Indian Foreign Service (IFS) officer now serving as Joint Secretary in the Ministry of External Affairs, was named India's next Ambassador to Iran on 3 August 2026. The appointment is part of diplomatic changes aimed at strengthening India's presence in West Asia, at a time of tension around the Strait of Hormuz.

Lucknow hosts BRICS Heads of National Statistical Offices meeting

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) hosted the two-day meeting of BRICS Heads of National Statistical Offices in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, on 3–4 August 2026 under India's BRICS Chairship. The theme of the statistics track was 'Quality Statistics as Driver of Change'.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • The National Statistics Office (NSO) under MoSPI was formed in 2019 by merging the Central Statistics Office (CSO) and the National Sample Survey Office (NSSO).

Maharashtra orders 'Zero Bureaucracy' reforms; departments to rise from 33 to 45

The Maharashtra government on 4 August 2026 issued a Government Resolution (GR) making five administrative reforms mandatory under its 'Zero Bureaucracy' drive. The number of administrative departments will rise from 33 to 45, decisions will be limited to three tiers, and Government Process Re-engineering (GPR) will be introduced. A state-level GPR committee chaired by the Chief Secretary will oversee the changes.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Maharashtra was formed on 1 May 1960 (Maharashtra Day); Mumbai is its capital and Nagpur its second (winter) capital.
  • The Chief Secretary is the senior-most civil servant of a state and heads the state secretariat.

Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026 introduced in Lok Sabha

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman introduced the Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026 in the Lok Sabha on 4 August 2026 to replace the Income-tax (Amendment) Ordinance, 2026 promulgated on 5 June. The Bill amends the Income-tax Act, 2025, the Finance Act, 2026 and the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007. Among other changes, it exempts sales of rough diamonds in notified zones from tax till 31 March 2041 and restores the dividend exemption for REIT and InvIT unit holders.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Under Article 123 the President can promulgate an ordinance when Parliament is not in session; it lapses six weeks after Parliament reassembles unless approved.
  • The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961 with effect from 1 April 2026.

17-year-old Tanvi Sharma wins Taipei Open, her maiden BWF World Tour title

Seventeen-year-old Indian shuttler Tanvi Sharma won her first BWF World Tour title at the Taipei Open Super 300 on 2 August 2026, beating Vietnam's Nguyen Thuy Linh 21-16, 21-16 in the women's singles final.
