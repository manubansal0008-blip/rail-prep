Vivaan Kapoor wins men's trap silver at ISSF World Championship, secures LA28 quota

On 12 November 2026, India's Vivaan Kapoor won silver in the men's trap event at the ISSF World Championship in Doha, Qatar. He and Kuwait's Abdulrahman Al-Faihan both hit 29 targets in the final; Al-Faihan won gold in the shoot-off and China's Qi Ying took bronze. The medal also gave India an Olympic quota place for LA 2028.

India's first Amrit Bharat Express 3.0, with AC coaches, runs on New Delhi–Patna route

Indian Railways ran its first Amrit Bharat Express 3.0 train from New Delhi to Patna at midnight on 11 November 2026 as a Chhath Puja special. Built by the Integral Coach Factory (ICF), Chennai, it is the first Amrit Bharat train with both AC and non-AC coaches and uses push-pull technology. The 22-coach rake has First AC, Second AC, Third AC, Sleeper and General coaches.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • 22 coaches: 1 First AC, 2 Second AC, 6 Third AC, 6 Sleeper, 4 General + pantry, power car, guard van
  • Amrit Bharat trains were launched in 2023 for affordable travel

(A) New Delhi – Patna The first Amrit Bharat Express 3.0 left New Delhi for Patna at midnight on 11 November 2026 as a Chhath Puja special. It was built by ICF, Chennai.

Retail inflation rises to 6.09% in October 2026, above RBI's 6% upper limit

Data released by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) on 12 November 2026 show that CPI-based retail inflation rose to 6.09% in October 2026 from 5.54% in September. Food inflation (CFPI) rose to 8.94%. Rural inflation was 6.63% and urban 5.41%. The new CPI series uses 2024 as the base year. RBI's target is 4% with a band of 2% to 6%.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • October 2026 CPI inflation 6.09%; food inflation 8.94% (base year 2024=100)
  • RBI inflation target: 4% ± 2%

Government scheme5/5

Tribal Affairs Ministry launches medicinal-plant conservation project in 16 tribal hotspots

On 12 November 2026, the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and CSIR–National Botanical Research Institute (CSIR-NBRI), Lucknow, launched a three-year project to conserve medicinal plants and create livelihoods for tribal communities, at the Central University of Odisha, Koraput. It covers 16 tribal hotspots in 13 States/UTs and plans 16 community nurseries and 16 seed banks.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • 16 tribal hotspots in 13 States/UTs; partner: CSIR-NBRI, Lucknow
  • Plans to document 200–300 medicinal plant species
