ISRO's GSLV-F17 launches earth observation satellite EOS-05 (GISAT-1A)

ISRO's GSLV-F17 lifted off from the Second Launch Pad at Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota, at 2:55 am on 4 September 2026 and placed the earth observation satellite EOS-05 (GISAT-1A) in a sub-geosynchronous transfer orbit. The satellite weighs about 2.3 tonnes and will watch the same part of the Earth continuously from geosynchronous orbit to help monitor cyclones, floods and forest fires. It was ISRO's first launch after a gap of seven months; ISRO Chairman V. Narayanan led the mission.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • GSLV (Geosynchronous Satellite Launch Vehicle) uses an indigenous cryogenic upper stage; Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SHAR) is in Andhra Pradesh.
  • ISRO was founded on 15 August 1969 and is headquartered in Bengaluru.

Teachers' Day observed on Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan's birth anniversary

India observed Teachers' Day on 5 September 2026, the birth anniversary of Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, philosopher and teacher. Radhakrishnan was born on 5 September 1888 at Tiruttani in present-day Tamil Nadu. He served as India's first Vice President (1952–1962) and second President (1962–1967).

Giorgia Meloni becomes Italy's longest continuously serving post-war Prime Minister

Giorgia Meloni on 4 September 2026 became Italy's longest continuously serving Prime Minister since the Second World War, completing 1,413 consecutive days in office. She passed the record of 1,412 days set by Silvio Berlusconi. Meloni, leader of the Brothers of Italy party, took office on 22 October 2022 as Italy's first woman Prime Minister, and Prime Minister Narendra Modi congratulated her on X.

Tamil Nadu announces 'Olympic City' at Semmencheri in Chennai

Tamil Nadu announced an 'Olympic City' at Semmencheri on Chennai's Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR) corridor, in a statement made in the Assembly under Rule 110, as reported on 4–5 September 2026.
