Parliament passes Bill to rename Kerala as 'Keralam'

Parliament passed the Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026 on 12 August 2026, when the Rajya Sabha approved it a day after the Lok Sabha. The Bill changes the state's name from Kerala to Keralam in the First Schedule of the Constitution. Minister of State for Home Nityanand Rai said the Kerala Assembly had asked for the change because the state is called Keralam in Malayalam, and the Union Cabinet had cleared the proposal in February 2026.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The First Schedule of the Constitution lists the names and territories of the States and Union Territories; under Article 3, Parliament can change the name of a state by law.
  • Kerala was formed on 1 November 1956 under the States Reorganisation Act; its capital is Thiruvananthapuram.

Railways2/5

Railways approves Kavach 4.0 on 660 route km of Izzatnagar Division at ₹295 crore

Indian Railways has approved Kavach Version 4.0 on 660 route kilometres of the Izzatnagar Division of North Eastern Railway at an estimated cost of ₹295 crore, All India Radio reported on 12 August 2026. The work comes under the umbrella project for Kavach with a Long-Term Evolution (LTE) communication backbone on the remaining sections of Indian Railways. Kavach automatically applies the brakes when needed to prevent trains from passing a red signal and to avoid collisions, including in dense fog.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Kavach is India's own Automatic Train Protection (ATP) system, developed by the Research Designs and Standards Organisation (RDSO) of the Railways with Indian industry.
  • The North Eastern Railway zone has its headquarters at Gorakhpur; Izzatnagar Division is based in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh.

Sports Ministry suspends Table Tennis Federation of India weeks before Asian Games

The Union Sports Ministry suspended its recognition of the Table Tennis Federation of India (TTFI) as the national sports federation with immediate effect on 12 August 2026, citing poor governance, failure to follow government directions and concerns over athlete selection. It asked the Indian Olympic Association (IOA) to set up an ad-hoc committee to run Indian table tennis for now. The move came weeks before the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The 20th Asian Games are being held in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, from 19 September to 4 October 2026.
  • A suspended federation loses government funding and other benefits of recognition; the IOA arranges an interim panel so that players are not affected.

Defence4/5

Navy's Next Generation Offshore Patrol Vessel 'Shruti' launched at GRSE, Kolkata

Yard 3037 'Shruti', a Next Generation Offshore Patrol Vessel (NGOPV) for the Indian Navy, was launched at Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE), Kolkata, on 11 August 2026, the Defence Ministry said. NGOPVs are being designed and built in India at two shipyards, GRSE Kolkata and Goa Shipyard. They will be used for sea patrol, search and rescue, protection of offshore assets, disaster relief and anti-piracy missions. The name 'Shruti' refers to the four Vedas.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The ship's crest shows the Ursa Major (Saptarishi) constellation and a red-and-white lighthouse.
  • GRSE is a Defence Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Defence, headquartered in Kolkata.

Summits / Days5/5

International Youth Day 2026 observed with theme 'Different Contexts, Common Aspirations'

International Youth Day was observed on 12 August 2026 with the UN theme 'Different Contexts, Common Aspirations'. The theme says that young people live in very different conditions but share the same hopes for dignity, decent work, good education and a say in decisions. It gives special attention to youth in the least developed countries, landlocked developing countries and small island states. In the Lok Sabha, Speaker Om Birla also greeted the country on the day.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • International Youth Day was declared by the UN General Assembly in December 1999 and was first observed on 12 August 2000.
  • India's National Youth Day is observed on 12 January, the birth anniversary of Swami Vivekananda.
