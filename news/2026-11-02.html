Bengaluru–Mumbai Vande Bharat Sleeper Express flagged off

Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw flagged off the Bengaluru–Mumbai Vande Bharat Sleeper Express on 1 November 2026, the day of Karnataka Rajyotsava. The 16-coach train runs between Sir M. Visvesvaraya Terminal (SMVT), Bengaluru and Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT), Mumbai in about 16 hours, via Anantapur, Guntakal, Raichur, Kalaburagi, Solapur, Pune and Kalyan. Gross GST collection rises nearly 23% to ₹2.31 lakh crore in October

Gross GST collections for October 2026 rose nearly 23% year-on-year to over ₹2.31 lakh crore, as per data released on 1 November 2026. Revenue from domestic sales grew 15.8% to over ₹1.59 lakh crore, while revenue from imports jumped 42.2% to ₹72,246 crore.

Bhutan raises 108 stupas in a single day at Gelephu Mindfulness City

Bhutan raised 108 sacred Buddhist stupas, each 15 metres tall, in a single day at Gelephu Mindfulness City on 1 November 2026, in a ceremony led by King Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck.
