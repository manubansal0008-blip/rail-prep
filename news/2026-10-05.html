Medicine Nobel 2026 for optogenetics pioneers

The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine was announced on 5 October 2026 for Karl Deisseroth (USA), Peter Hegemann (Germany) and Georg Nagel (Germany) "for discoveries concerning light-gated ion channels and optogenetics". Optogenetics uses light to switch specific nerve cells on or off, helping scientists study how the brain works. The three laureates share the prize of 12 million Swedish crowns.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine is chosen by the Nobel Assembly at Karolinska Institutet, Stockholm, Sweden.
  • The Nobel Prizes were first awarded in 1901, under the will of Swedish scientist Alfred Nobel, the inventor of dynamite.

Swiss President Guy Parmelin begins three-day state visit to India

Swiss President Guy Parmelin arrived in New Delhi on 5 October 2026 for a state visit from 5 to 7 October, his first state visit to India. He was given a ceremonial welcome at Rashtrapati Bhavan by President Droupadi Murmu and Prime Minister Narendra Modi and is to hold talks with PM Modi on trade, investment, science, technology and mobility. FPIs pull out ₹35,861 crore from Indian equities in September

Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) were net sellers of Indian equities worth ₹35,861 crore in September 2026, as per depository data reported on 4 October 2026, after positive flows in July and August. They sold ₹45,537 crore through stock exchanges but invested ₹9,676 crore through the primary market. Geopolitical uncertainty, high US bond yields and crude oil prices were cited as reasons.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • FPIs in India are regulated by SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India), headquartered in Mumbai.
  • SEBI was set up in 1988 and got statutory powers under the SEBI Act, 1992.

World Teachers' Day marks 60 years of the 1966 teachers' charter

World Teachers' Day was observed on 5 October 2026, marking 60 years of the ILO/UNESCO Recommendation concerning the Status of Teachers, adopted on 5 October 1966. The Recommendation sets global standards on teachers' rights, training, recruitment and working conditions. UNESCO estimates that 44 million more teachers are needed worldwide to achieve universal primary and secondary education by 2030.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • World Teachers' Day has been celebrated on 5 October since 1994; UNESCO is headquartered in Paris.
  • India celebrates its Teachers' Day on 5 September, the birth anniversary of Dr Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan.

ऑप्टोजेनेटिक्स के अग्रदूतों को 2026 का चिकित्सा नोबेल

5 अक्टूबर 2026 को घोषित 2026 का फ़िज़ियोलॉजी या मेडिसिन का नोबेल पुरस्कार कार्ल डाइसरोथ (अमेरिका), पीटर हेगेमन (जर्मनी) और जॉर्ज नागेल (जर्मनी) को "प्रकाश से नियंत्रित आयन चैनलों और ऑप्टोजेनेटिक्स से जुड़ी खोजों" के लिए मिला। ऑप्टोजेनेटिक्स में रोशनी की मदद से ख़ास तंत्रिका कोशिकाओं को चालू या बंद किया जाता है, जिससे वैज्ञानिक समझ पाते हैं कि दिमाग़ कैसे काम करता है। तीनों विजेता 1.2 करोड़ स्वीडिश क्रोना की पुरस्कार राशि बाँटेंगे।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • फ़िज़ियोलॉजी या मेडिसिन के नोबेल का चयन स्टॉकहोम (स्वीडन) के कारोलिंस्का इंस्टीट्यूट की नोबेल असेंबली करती है।
  • नोबेल पुरस्कार पहली बार 1901 में डायनामाइट के आविष्कारक, स्वीडिश वैज्ञानिक अल्फ़्रेड नोबेल की वसीयत के तहत दिए गए।

  2. ऑप्टोजेनेटिक्स
