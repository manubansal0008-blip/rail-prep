Science / Space3/5

Astronomers confirm Elias 2-24 b, a Jupiter-sized planet less than a million years old — the youngest known

As reported on 26 November 2026, astronomers have confirmed a Jupiter-sized planet named Elias 2-24 b that is less than one million years old, making it the youngest planet known so far. It was observed with the coronagraph of the W. M. Keck Observatory in Hawaii. The planet is roughly as massive as Jupiter and orbits a star about 450 light-years from Earth.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • A light-year is a unit of distance — the distance light travels in one year (about 9.46 trillion km)
  • Jupiter is the largest planet of our Solar System

  4. W. M. Keck Observatory, Hawaii

Maldives hosts the 8th NSA-level meeting of the Colombo Security Conclave; roadmap for 2026-27 adopted

The 8th National Security Adviser-level meeting of the Colombo Security Conclave (CSC) was hosted by the Maldives on 25 November 2026, with NSA Ajit Doval leading India's delegation. Maldives, India, Mauritius and Sri Lanka signed the Agreed Minutes and adopted the Roadmap of Cooperation for 2026-2027, while Bangladesh was also represented and Australia and Malaysia joined as guests. Government aims to turn the Department of Posts from a cost centre into a profit centre by 2030

Communications Minister Jyotiraditya Scindia said in New Delhi on 26 November 2026 that the government aims to make the Department of Posts a profit centre, instead of a cost centre, by 2030. The department's annual revenue rose from about ₹13,200 crore to nearly ₹15,500 crore in 2025-26, and it targets over ₹19,800 crore in the current financial year. He also welcomed the Cabinet's decision to continue the Postal Operations Scheme and the Estates Management Scheme.

26 नवंबर 2026 की ख़बर के अनुसार, खगोलविदों ने बृहस्पति के आकार के ग्रह Elias 2-24 b की पुष्टि की है, जिसकी आयु दस लाख (एक मिलियन) वर्ष से भी कम है; यह अब तक ज्ञात सबसे युवा ग्रह है। इसे हवाई (अमेरिका) स्थित डब्ल्यू. एम. केक वेधशाला के कोरोनाग्राफ से देखा गया। यह ग्रह लगभग बृहस्पति जितना भारी है और पृथ्वी से करीब 450 प्रकाश-वर्ष दूर एक तारे की परिक्रमा करता है।

  1. देवस्थल वेधशाला, उत्तराखंड

  3. हानले वेधशाला, लद्दाख
