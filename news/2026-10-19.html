Railways1/5

Namo Bharat completes three years; 4.5 crore journeys on Delhi–Meerut corridor

NCRTC celebrated Namo Bharat Diwas 2026 to mark three years of Namo Bharat (RRTS) train operations. Union Housing and Urban Affairs Minister Manohar Lal said the 82-km Delhi–Ghaziabad–Meerut corridor has recorded over 4.5 crore passenger journeys. NCRTC said it expects approval soon for two new corridors: Delhi–Panipat–Karnal and Delhi–Gurugram–Behror.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Namo Bharat services began on the Sahibabad–Duhai stretch in October 2023; the full 82-km corridor was dedicated to the nation on 22 February 2026.
  • NCRTC is a joint venture of the Government of India and the states of Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh, under the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

Defence2/5

India–Australia Army exercise AUSTRAHIND 2026 begins in Thiruvananthapuram

The 5th edition of the India–Australia joint military exercise AUSTRAHIND began on 19 October 2026 at the Foreign Training Node, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, and will run until 1 November. About 120 soldiers from each side are taking part — the Indian contingent from the Garhwal Rifles and the Australian contingent from 13 Brigade. The theme is sub-conventional operations in island and coastal terrain under a UN Chapter VII mandate.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • AUSTRAHIND is an annual exercise held alternately in India and Australia.
  • Chapter VII of the UN Charter deals with action with respect to threats to peace and acts of aggression.

Appointments4/5

Dhal Singh Bisen sworn in as the ninth Governor of Uttarakhand

Dr Dhal Singh Bisen took oath as the ninth Governor of Uttarakhand on 19 October 2026 at Lok Bhavan, Dehradun. Acting Chief Justice of the Uttarakhand High Court Manoj Kumar Tiwari administered the oath. Bisen, a former Lok Sabha MP from Balaghat (Madhya Pradesh), succeeds Lt Gen Gurmit Singh (Retd), who served for over five years. President Droupadi Murmu had announced his appointment on 12 October.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The oath to a Governor is administered by the Chief Justice of the state High Court (or the senior-most judge available) under Article 159.
  • Uttarakhand was formed on 9 November 2000; its first Governor was Surjit Singh Barnala.
