East Bengal beat Mohun Bagan 4-1 to win 135th Durand Cup

East Bengal won the 135th Durand Cup by beating Mohun Bagan Super Giant 4-1 in the final at the Vivekananda Yuba Bharati Krirangan (Salt Lake Stadium) in Kolkata. Edmund Lalrindika scored a first-half hat-trick and Bipin Singh also scored, while Manvir Singh got Mohun Bagan's goal. It is East Bengal's first Durand Cup title since 2004 and takes the two Kolkata giants level at a record 17 titles each.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Durand Cup, first held in 1888 and named after Sir Mortimer Durand, is Asia's oldest football tournament.
  • It is organised by the Durand Football Tournament Society along with the Indian Armed Forces and the All India Football Federation (AIFF).

First fully indigenous AK-203 'Sher' rifle test-fired at Korwa, Amethi

Indo-Russian Rifles Pvt Ltd (IRRPL) produced and test-fired India's first AK-203 'Sher' assault rifle made entirely from Indian components and input materials at its Korwa facility in Amethi, Uttar Pradesh. The rifle passed firing tests, including burst fire, and prototypes are slated for Army trials in September 2026. IRRPL targets producing one rifle every 100 seconds and is supplying 6,01,427 rifles under a ₹5,200 crore contract.

3.5-billion-year-old evidence of microbial life found in rock from India's Singhbhum Craton

A study published in the journal PNAS reported evidence of microbial life about 3.5 billion years old in carbon-bearing chert from Bhitardari in the Singhbhum Craton of eastern India. Four of eight zircon grains from the rock were dated to about 3,497 million years. Carbon isotope patterns and degraded organic matter support a biological origin, making it among the oldest directly dated evidence of life in Indian rocks.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Singhbhum Craton, spread over Jharkhand and Odisha, is one of the oldest pieces of continental crust in India.
  • The Earth is about 4.54 billion years old.

  3. Singhbhum Craton
  4. Aravalli Craton
Show answer
