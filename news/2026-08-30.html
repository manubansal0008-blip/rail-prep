India and Uzbekistan raise ties to Comprehensive Strategic Partnership during PM Modi's Tashkent visit

PM Narendra Modi, on a state visit to Uzbekistan (29–30 August 2026), held talks with President Shavkat Mirziyoyev in Tashkent, and the two sides upgraded their ties to a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership. Eleven agreements were signed, including on long-term supply of uranium to India, letting Indian UPI apps scan Uzbekistan's UZQR codes, and conservation of the Buddhist sites of Fayaz Tepa and Kara Tepa in Termez. India also gave $1 million for afforestation in the Aral Sea region, and both sides set a target of $5 billion in trade by 2030.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Tashkent is the capital of Uzbekistan; the Tashkent Declaration between India and Pakistan was signed there on 10 January 1966, and Lal Bahadur Shastri died in Tashkent the next day.
  • Uzbekistan is a member of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO); its currency is the som.

Centre notifies Legal Metrology (Indian Standard Time) Rules, 2026

The Department of Consumer Affairs notified the Legal Metrology (Indian Standard Time) Rules, 2026 on 27 August 2026, making Indian Standard Time (UTC+5:30) the common reference time for legal, administrative and commercial purposes across India. The rules aim at accurate and consistent time-stamping in critical digital systems such as digital payments, banking, telecom, computer networks, railways, airports, power systems and government records. IST will be disseminated through Indian institutions and Legal Metrology laboratories.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • IST is based on the 82.5° East longitude, which passes near Mirzapur in Uttar Pradesh.
  • CSIR-National Physical Laboratory (NPL), New Delhi, is the official timekeeper of India.

Tamil Nadu to set up safe houses in every district for inter-caste couples

Tamil Nadu will set up safe houses in every district for inter-caste couples facing threats, Social Justice Minister Vanni Arasu announced in the State Assembly, The Hindu reported on 30 August 2026. The step aims to curb so-called honour killings and follows the Supreme Court's directions in Shakti Vahini vs Union of India (2018). The right to choose a life partner is protected under Articles 19 and 21 of the Constitution.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Inter-caste marriages are valid under the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 and the Special Marriage Act, 1954.
  • Article 21 of the Constitution guarantees the right to life and personal liberty.

Government likely to name first winner of Sardar Patel National Unity Award this year

The government is likely to name the first recipient of the Sardar Patel National Unity Award this year, as Sardar Patel's 150th birth anniversary celebrations end, the Indian Express reported on 30 August 2026. Instituted in September 2019 and managed by the Ministry of Home Affairs, the award has so far not been given to anyone; over 150 nominations were received between 1 June and 31 July. It carries a medal and citation but no cash, and a committee appointed by the Prime Minister selects the winners.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel was born on 31 October 1875 at Nadiad, Gujarat; his birth anniversary is observed as Rashtriya Ekta Diwas (National Unity Day).
  • The Statue of Unity, the world's tallest statue at 182 m, stands at Ekta Nagar (Kevadia) in Gujarat.

Economy / Banking5/5

As IBC turns 10, bank haircuts hit five-year high

As the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016 completes 10 years, bank haircuts under it have reached a five-year high, the Indian Express reported on 30 August 2026. The National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) approved a repayment plan under which Zee group founder Subhash Chandra will pay ₹6.5 crore against admitted claims of ₹22,006.57 crore, a 99.97% haircut. Recovery against admitted claims fell to 20% in FY26, the lowest in five years, while 1,077 cases resolved between FY22 and FY26 saw average recovery of about 29%.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) was set up on 1 October 2016 to regulate insolvency proceedings under the IBC.
  • A loan becomes a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) when interest or principal remains overdue for more than 90 days.

IBC के 10 साल पूरे, बैंकों का हेयरकट पाँच साल के ऊँचे स्तर पर

इंडियन एक्सप्रेस में 30 अगस्त 2026 को छपी ख़बर के अनुसार दिवाला और शोधन अक्षमता संहिता (IBC), 2016 के 10 साल पूरे होने पर इसके तहत बैंकों का हेयरकट (कर्ज़ में छूट) पाँच साल के सबसे ऊँचे स्तर पर पहुँच गया है। राष्ट्रीय कंपनी क़ानून न्यायाधिकरण (NCLT) ने एक भुगतान योजना मंज़ूर की, जिसके तहत ज़ी समूह के संस्थापक सुभाष चंद्रा ₹22,006.57 करोड़ के स्वीकृत दावों के बदले ₹6.5 करोड़ चुकाएँगे, यानी 99.97% हेयरकट। वित्त वर्ष 2026 में स्वीकृत दावों के मुकाबले वसूली घटकर 20% रह गई, जो पाँच साल में सबसे कम है, जबकि FY22 से FY26 के बीच निपटे 1,077 मामलों में औसत वसूली करीब 29% रही।
