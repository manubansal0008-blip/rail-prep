Railway Minister reviews preparations for Cyclone Arnab

Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw reviewed Indian Railways' preparations on 27 October 2026 ahead of Cyclone Arnab's expected landfall on the Andhra Pradesh coast. About 600 km of track in the Vijayawada and Visakhapatnam divisions is under close watch, war rooms are working round the clock at zonal, divisional and Railway Board levels, and relief wagons, diesel locomotives and ambulances are on standby.

President's assent to the Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority Bill

President Droupadi Murmu gave assent on 25 October 2026 to the Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority (FNTA) Bill, 2026, which the Nagaland Assembly had passed unanimously on 3 September. The Authority will cover six eastern districts – Tuensang, Mon, Longleng, Kiphire, Noklak and Shamator – and will have 62 members and powers over 46 subjects.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • Nagaland became a state on 1 December 1963; its capital is Kohima.

80th Infantry Day observed on 27 October

The Indian Army observed the 80th Infantry Day (Shaurya Diwas) on 27 October 2026 with wreath-laying ceremonies at war memorials. The day marks 27 October 1947, when the 1st Battalion of the Sikh Regiment was flown from Delhi and landed at Srinagar airfield to defend Jammu and Kashmir against Pakistani raiders.

WMO: carbon dioxide in the air hit a record 426.8 ppm in 2025

The World Meteorological Organization's Greenhouse Gas Bulletin (No. 22), released on 27 October 2026, says the average carbon dioxide (CO₂) level reached a record 426.8 parts per million (ppm) in 2025, up 2.8 ppm from 2024. Methane reached 1948 parts per billion (ppb) and nitrous oxide 339.1 ppb, also records.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • CO₂ is now 153% of its pre-industrial (1750) level; methane is 267%.
  • The WMO is a UN agency with its headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland.

विश्व मौसम विज्ञान संगठन (WMO) के 27 अक्टूबर 2026 को जारी ग्रीनहाउस गैस बुलेटिन (संख्या 22) के अनुसार 2025 में कार्बन डाइऑक्साइड (CO₂) का औसत स्तर रिकॉर्ड 426.8 पार्ट्स पर मिलियन (ppm) पहुँच गया, जो 2024 से 2.8 ppm ज़्यादा है। मीथेन 1948 पार्ट्स पर बिलियन (ppb) और नाइट्रस ऑक्साइड 339.1 ppb पर पहुँची – ये भी रिकॉर्ड हैं।

  1. 415.7 ppm
  2. 426.8 ppm
  3. 433.2 ppm
  4. 450.0 ppm
