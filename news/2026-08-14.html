President approves 78 gallantry awards, including 9 Kirti Chakras, on eve of 80th Independence Day

President Droupadi Murmu approved 78 gallantry awards, 13 of them posthumous, for personnel of the Armed Forces and Central Armed Police Forces on 14 August 2026, the eve of the 80th Independence Day. They include 9 Kirti Chakras (7 posthumous), 1 Bar to Shaurya Chakra, 19 Shaurya Chakras, 5 Bars to Sena Medal, 36 Sena Medals (Gallantry), 3 Nao Sena Medals and 5 Vayu Sena Medals. The President also approved 89 Mentions-in-Despatches: 75 for the Army, 4 for the Navy and 10 for the Air Force.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Ashoka Chakra, Kirti Chakra and Shaurya Chakra are India's peacetime gallantry awards, in that order of precedence.
  • The Param Vir Chakra is India's highest wartime gallantry award, followed by the Maha Vir Chakra and the Vir Chakra.

1,057 police, fire, home guard and correctional personnel get medals for Independence Day 2026

The Ministry of Home Affairs announced on 14 August 2026 that 1,057 personnel of the Police, Fire, Home Guard and Civil Defence and Correctional Services will receive Gallantry and Service medals on Independence Day 2026. They include 301 Medals for Gallantry (272 police and 29 fire service), 92 President's Medals for Distinguished Service and 664 Medals for Meritorious Service. Of the gallantry medals, 197 went to personnel from Left Wing Extremism-affected areas and 51 from Jammu and Kashmir; three were awarded posthumously.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • Police medals are announced twice a year, on Republic Day (26 January) and Independence Day (15 August).

International3/5

India and Southern African Customs Union sign Terms of Reference for a Preferential Trade Agreement

India and the Southern African Customs Union (SACU) signed the Terms of Reference (ToR) for negotiating a Preferential Trade Agreement at Vanijya Bhawan, New Delhi, AffairsCloud reported on 14 August 2026; it is India's first such ToR with the African region. The talks will cover eight chapters, including market access for goods, rules of origin, customs, trade remedies and dispute settlement. Focus sectors include engineering goods, automobiles, pharmaceuticals, textiles and agriculture.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • SACU, set up in 1910 and headquartered in Windhoek (Namibia), is regarded as the world's oldest customs union.
  • SACU has five members: South Africa, Botswana, Namibia, Lesotho and Eswatini.

Bank of America to buy up to 49.9% of Jio Credit for ₹18,268 crore

Bank of America (BofA) and Jio Financial Services Limited (JFSL) have signed a joint venture agreement under which BofA may acquire up to 49.9% of Jio Credit Limited, JFSL's NBFC lending arm, for ₹18,268 crore (about USD 1.9 billion), AffairsCloud reported on 14 August 2026. BofA will first take a 26.5% stake worth ₹6,612.90 crore through a preferential allotment, with the rest through warrants. The deal needs regulatory approvals, and both partners will have equal representation on the board.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) in India are regulated by the Reserve Bank of India under the RBI Act, 1934.
  • Jio Financial Services was demerged from Reliance Industries and listed on the stock exchanges in August 2023.

Science / Space5/5

Delhi University scientists discover three new cascade frog species in the Northeast and Bengal

A team led by Prof. S. D. Biju of the University of Delhi has described three new species of cascade frogs, AffairsCloud reported on 14 August 2026. They are Ammachi's Cascade Frog (Amolops ammachi) from Tripura's Jampui Hills, the Sendenyu Cascade Frog (Amolops sendenyu) from Nagaland and the Beautiful Cascade Frog (Amolops bella) from West Bengal. The study, based on over 15 years of fieldwork, was published in the Bulletin of the Museum of Comparative Zoology, Harvard University, and also recorded five cascade frog species new to India.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Cascade frogs of the genus Amolops live near fast-flowing hill streams and waterfalls.
  • Agartala is the capital of Tripura and Kohima is the capital of Nagaland.
