PM dedicates last 326 km of Western Dedicated Freight Corridor

Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 8 September 2026 launched projects worth over ₹35,000 crore in Vadodara, Gujarat, including three sections of the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor (WDFC) – Sanand (North)–Makarpura, New Umbergaon–New Saphale and New Saphale–JNPA – covering 326 route km at a cost of over ₹20,700 crore. These sections complete the WDFC and give freight trains direct access to the Jawaharlal Nehru Port near Mumbai. He also dedicated the Jobat–Tanda Road line and laid foundation stones for over 329 km of rail projects, including Vadodara–Ratlam 3rd and 4th lines.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Western DFC runs about 1,506 km from Dadri (Uttar Pradesh) to JNPA (Maharashtra); the Eastern DFC runs about 1,337 km from Ludhiana (Punjab) to Sonnagar (Bihar).
  • The corridors are built by the Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd (DFCCIL), set up in 2006 under the Ministry of Railways.

Summits / Days2/5

International Literacy Day marks 60 years

International Literacy Day was observed on 8 September 2026 with the theme 'Literacy for people, the planet and prosperity'. This year marks 60 years since UNESCO proclaimed the day, and UNESCO's global celebration is being held on 8–9 September in Chiapas, Mexico, where the UNESCO International Literacy Prizes are presented. At least 739 million youth and adults worldwide still lack basic literacy skills.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • UNESCO proclaimed 8 September as International Literacy Day on 26 October 1966 at its 14th General Conference; it was first observed in 1967.
  • UNESCO is headquartered in Paris, France. India's literacy rate in the 2011 Census was 74.04%.

Quick question: In which year did UNESCO proclaim 8 September as International Literacy Day?

  1. 1945
  2. 1961
  3. 1966
  4. 1975
Show answer

(C) 1966 UNESCO proclaimed the day on 26 October 1966; it was first observed in 1967. 2026 marks 60 years since the proclamation.

PM inaugurates Global Fintech Fest 2026 in Mumbai

Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the seventh edition of the Global Fintech Fest (GFF) in Mumbai on 8 September 2026; the four-day event ends on 11 September. Its theme is 'Potential to Impact: Agentic AI, Tokenisation, Quantum – Trusted, Connected, Global Systems for Inclusive Finance'. India and Morocco hold first Joint Defence Committee meeting

The first meeting of the India–Morocco Joint Defence Committee (JDC) was held in New Delhi on 8 September 2026. It was co-chaired by Amitabh Prasad, Joint Secretary (International Cooperation), Ministry of Defence, and Brigadier General Abdellah Athmani of the Moroccan Royal Armed Forces. The two sides discussed military training, peacekeeping, joint exercises, cyber defence, military medicine and defence-industry co-production.

📌 Remember for the exam

CWMA directs Karnataka to release 6,000 cusecs of Cauvery water to Tamil Nadu

At its 57th meeting in New Delhi on 8 September 2026, chaired by S K Haldar, the Cauvery Water Management Authority (CWMA) upheld the Cauvery Water Regulation Committee's recommendation that Karnataka ensure a flow of 6,000 cusecs to Tamil Nadu for 15 days. Tamil Nadu had sought nearly 20,000 cusecs, while Karnataka said it faces a distress situation. On 25 August the CWMA had upheld an order for 9,000 cusecs a day.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The CWMA was set up in 2018 following the Supreme Court's February 2018 verdict on the Cauvery dispute; the Cauvery Water Disputes Tribunal was formed in 1990.
  • Water released by Karnataka is measured at Biligundlu on the Karnataka–Tamil Nadu border.
