New daily express between Mumbai CSMT and Sawantwadi Road flagged off

Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw, Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal and Maharashtra Deputy Chief Minister Eknath Shinde on 3 September 2026 flagged off the inaugural run of a new daily train between Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT), Mumbai and Sawantwadi Road. Train No. 15087/15088 runs about 510 km on the Konkan route with an LHB rake and 22 stops, serving Raigad, Ratnagiri and Sindhudurg districts. Vaishnaw said 350 special trains are running for the festival season this year and that 238 new AC local rakes have been sanctioned for Mumbai.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Konkan Railway route is about 741 km long and is fully electrified; Konkan Railway Corporation has its headquarters at CBD Belapur, Navi Mumbai.
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (formerly Victoria Terminus) is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the headquarters of Central Railway.

Economy / Banking2/5

RBI's special dollar swap window mobilises $136.38 billion

The Reserve Bank of India's special USD–INR swap facility, launched on 8 June 2026 to support the rupee, has brought in about $136.38 billion, as reported on 3–4 September 2026. Of this, $127.23 billion came from FCNR(B) deposits, $5.26 billion from overseas foreign currency borrowings and $3.89 billion from external commercial borrowings. The FCNR(B) window, originally open till 30 September, closed early on 31 August, and the inflows pushed the banking system's liquidity surplus to about ₹9.7 lakh crore.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • FCNR(B) stands for Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Bank) deposits, which NRIs hold in foreign currency with Indian banks.
  • The RBI was established on 1 April 1935 and is headquartered in Mumbai.

  2. External commercial borrowings
  3. Foreign portfolio investment
  4. Sovereign bonds
Show answer

(A) FCNR(B) deposits $127.23 billion came from FCNR(B) deposits; the window closed early on 31 August 2026.

Ig Nobel Prizes held outside the US for the first time; Indian scientists among winners

The 36th Ig Nobel Prize ceremony was held on 3 September 2026 at the Zurich Convention Center in Switzerland, the first time it took place outside the United States. Indian scientists Sanchari Banerjee and Subrahmanyan Ramaswamy won the Chemistry prize for their work on protein crystals in cockroach milk. Dr. Tokuji Unno was honoured posthumously in Medicine for research on airflow while blowing the nose.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Ig Nobel Prizes, started in 1991 by the magazine Annals of Improbable Research, honour research that 'makes people laugh, and then think'.
  • Zurich is the largest city of Switzerland; the Swiss capital is Bern.

Science / Space4/5

New stingless bee species Tetragonula nagalandensis found in Nagaland

Researchers have described a new species of stingless bee, Tetragonula nagalandensis, from Medziphema in Nagaland, as reported in early September 2026. The discovery raises the number of stingless bee species known from India to 19. Stingless bees are important pollinators of crops and wild plants and are also kept for honey.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Nagaland became India's 16th state on 1 December 1963; its capital is Kohima.
  • Bees belong to the insect order Hymenoptera, which also includes ants and wasps.

States5/5

Centre defers Census operations in Manipur

The Centre has deferred Census operations in Manipur, as reported on 3–4 September 2026. Meitei, Pangal and Naga groups have demanded that the National Register of Citizens (NRC) be updated first with 1951 as the base year, while the Kuki-Zo Council rejected the NRC demand and welcomed the Census. Petitions on the issue have also reached the Manipur High Court.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Census 2027 will be India's first digital census, conducted under the Census Act, 1948.
  • The NRC was first prepared in 1951; so far it has been updated only in Assam. Imphal is the capital of Manipur.

RBI की विशेष डॉलर स्वैप सुविधा से 136.38 अरब डॉलर जुटे

3–4 सितंबर 2026 की ख़बरों के अनुसार रुपये को सहारा देने के लिए 8 जून 2026 को शुरू की गई भारतीय रिज़र्व बैंक की विशेष USD–INR स्वैप सुविधा से करीब 136.38 अरब डॉलर आए हैं। इनमें 127.23 अरब डॉलर FCNR(B) जमा से, 5.26 अरब डॉलर विदेशी मुद्रा उधारी से और 3.89 अरब डॉलर बाहरी वाणिज्यिक उधारी (ECB) से आए। FCNR(B) खिड़की 30 सितंबर तक खुली रहनी थी, पर 31 अगस्त को ही बंद कर दी गई; इस आवक से बैंकिंग प्रणाली में नकदी का अधिशेष करीब ₹9.7 लाख करोड़ हो गया।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • FCNR(B) का मतलब फ़ॉरेन करेंसी नॉन-रेज़िडेंट (बैंक) जमा है, जिसे प्रवासी भारतीय भारतीय बैंकों में विदेशी मुद्रा में रखते हैं।
  • RBI की स्थापना 1 अप्रैल 1935 को हुई थी और इसका मुख्यालय मुंबई में है।

छोटा सवाल: 2026 में RBI की विशेष स्वैप सुविधा से जुटे 136.38 अरब डॉलर में से सबसे ज़्यादा राशि किस स्रोत से आई?

  1. FCNR(B) जमा
  2. बाहरी वाणिज्यिक उधारी
  3. विदेशी पोर्टफ़ोलियो निवेश
  4. सॉवरेन बॉन्ड
उत्तर देखें

(A) FCNR(B) जमा 127.23 अरब डॉलर FCNR(B) जमा से आए; यह खिड़की 31 अगस्त 2026 को समय से पहले बंद कर दी गई।

  • मधुमक्खियां कीटों के हाइमेनोप्टेरा गण में आती हैं, जिसमें चींटियां और ततैया भी शामिल हैं।

3–4 सितंबर 2026 की ख़बरों के अनुसार केंद्र ने मणिपुर में जनगणना का काम फ़िलहाल टाल दिया है। मैतेई, पंगल और नगा समूहों की मांग है कि पहले 1951 को आधार वर्ष मानकर राष्ट्रीय नागरिक रजिस्टर (NRC) अपडेट किया जाए, जबकि कुकी-ज़ो काउंसिल ने NRC की मांग ठुकराकर जनगणना का स्वागत किया है। इस मुद्दे पर मणिपुर उच्च न्यायालय में भी याचिकाएं दायर हुई हैं।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें

  • NRC पहली बार 1951 में तैयार हुआ था और अब तक केवल असम में अपडेट हुआ है। इंफ़ाल मणिपुर की राजधानी है।

  2. 1949

  4. 1971
