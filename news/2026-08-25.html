Rajnath Singh approves transfer of technology of all conventional DRDO missile systems to industry

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh on 25 August 2026 approved the transfer of technology for all conventional missile systems developed by the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO). Indian industry will be able to manufacture these missiles after meeting qualification and certification requirements. The move aims to shift missiles to large-scale domestic production and reduce dependence on defence imports.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • DRDO was set up in 1958 and has its headquarters in New Delhi; it works under the Department of Defence Research and Development, Ministry of Defence.
  • The Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP), led by Dr A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, began in 1983 and produced missiles such as Prithvi, Agni, Akash, Trishul and Nag.

  1. All DRDO radar systems
  2. All conventional DRDO missile systems
  3. All DRDO naval sonars
  4. All DRDO light combat aircraft designs
Show answer

(B) All conventional DRDO missile systems Industry can make all conventional DRDO missile systems after qualification and certification.

The 25th round of the India–China Special Representatives (SR) talks on the boundary question began in Beijing on 25 August 2026. National Security Adviser Ajit Doval represents India and Foreign Minister Wang Yi represents China. The talks cover the western, middle and eastern sectors of the border.

Economy / Banking3/5

UPI completes 10 years; July 2026 sees 2,366 crore transactions

The Unified Payments Interface (UPI) completed a decade on 25 August 2026. In July 2026, UPI handled 2,366 crore transactions worth ₹29.88 lakh crore. The platform now has 741 participating banks and works in 11 countries.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • UPI was developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), which was set up in 2008 by the RBI and the Indian Banks' Association and is headquartered in Mumbai.
  • UPI 123PAY, launched in March 2022, lets feature-phone users make UPI payments.

  1. Reserve Bank of India
  2. State Bank of India
  3. National Payments Corporation of India
  4. NITI Aayog
Show answer

(C) National Payments Corporation of India UPI was developed by NPCI, set up in 2008 by the RBI and the Indian Banks' Association.

Science / Space5/5

CSIR-NAL unveils three indigenous micro gas turbine engines for drones and missiles

CSIR-National Aerospace Laboratories (CSIR-NAL) unveiled three indigenous micro gas turbine engines in New Delhi on 25 August 2026: NJ-05 (5 kg thrust), NJ-50 (50 kg thrust) and NJ-100 (100 kg thrust). The engines are meant for tactical unmanned aerial vehicles, drone interceptors and compact missiles. They work on the Brayton cycle, the thermodynamic cycle used by jet engines.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • CSIR-NAL, headquartered in Bengaluru, was set up in 1959 and developed the HANSA trainer aircraft and the SARAS light transport aircraft.
  • The Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) was set up in 1942.

Quick question: Which laboratory unveiled the NJ-05, NJ-50 and NJ-100 micro gas turbine engines on 25 August 2026?

  1. CSIR-National Aerospace Laboratories
  2. DRDO's Gas Turbine Research Establishment
  3. HAL Engine Division
  4. ISRO Propulsion Complex
Show answer

(A) CSIR-National Aerospace Laboratories CSIR-NAL, Bengaluru, unveiled the three engines for UAVs, drone interceptors and compact missiles.

Source: Adda247 (Bankersadda), 25 Aug 2026

महाराष्ट्र के मुख्यमंत्री देवेंद्र फडणवीस और हेस्से के मिनिस्टर-प्रेसिडेंट बोरिस राइन ने 22 अगस्त 2026 को मुंबई के सह्याद्रि अतिथि गृह में समझौता ज्ञापन (MoU) पर हस्ताक्षर किए। इसमें निवेश, उद्योग, नवाचार, रोज़गार और कौशल विकास शामिल हैं। फडणवीस ने कहा कि हेस्से महाराष्ट्र के लिए जर्मनी और यूरोप का दरवाज़ा बनेगा।
