Six states sign agreement for Kishau Multipurpose Project on Tons river

The Chief Ministers of Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Rajasthan, Delhi and Haryana signed the agreement for the Kishau Multipurpose Project on 15 September 2026 in the presence of Union Home Minister Amit Shah and Jal Shakti Minister C.R. Patil. Declared a National Project, it will be funded about 90% by the Centre and 10% by the states. It includes a 232.6-metre-high concrete gravity dam on the Tons river and is expected to irrigate about 97,000 hectares and generate about 1,476 million units of hydropower, while improving water flows in the Yamuna.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Tons is the largest tributary of the Yamuna; the Kishau dam site lies on the Uttarakhand–Himachal Pradesh border.
  • The six states had reached consensus on the project MoU on 16 June 2026; Delhi is expected to benefit most from the extra Yamuna water.

(C) Tons The project has a 232.6 m concrete gravity dam on the Tons, the largest tributary of the Yamuna; the Centre bears about 90% of the cost.

Sanjukta Parashar becomes first woman Inspector General of CRPF's CoBRA

IPS officer Sanjukta Parashar has become the first woman Inspector General of the CRPF's Commando Battalion for Resolute Action (CoBRA), as reported on 15 September 2026. She is a 2006-batch officer of the Assam-Meghalaya cadre and succeeds Danesh Rana. CoBRA is the CRPF's specialised jungle-warfare force deployed mainly against Left-wing extremism.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • CoBRA was raised by the CRPF in 2008–09 for guerrilla and jungle warfare operations.
  • The CRPF was raised on 27 July 1939 as the Crown Representative's Police; it is the largest Central Armed Police Force and works under the Ministry of Home Affairs.

Engineers' Day marks M. Visvesvaraya's birth anniversary

India observes Engineers' Day on 15 September 2026 to honour Bharat Ratna Sir Mokshagundam Visvesvaraya, born on this day in 1861, and to recognise engineers' contribution to infrastructure, technology and national development. 15 September is also the International Day of Democracy, which highlights democratic values, public participation and strong democratic institutions.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Visvesvaraya was born at Muddenahalli in present-day Karnataka, served as Diwan of Mysore and designed the Krishna Raja Sagara dam; he received the Bharat Ratna in 1955.
  • The UN General Assembly declared the International Day of Democracy in 2007; it was first observed in 2008.

  4. M. Visvesvaraya
Show answer

(D) M. Visvesvaraya Sir M. Visvesvaraya, born on 15 September 1861, designed the Krishna Raja Sagara dam and received the Bharat Ratna in 1955.

IFFCO Chairman Dileep Sanghani elected to International Cooperative Alliance Global Board

IFFCO Chairman Dileep Sanghani has been elected an At-Large Director of the Global Board of the International Cooperative Alliance (ICA) for the 2026–2030 term, as reported on 15 September 2026. The election took place at the ICA General Assembly held in Panama. The ICA is the global apex body representing cooperatives.

  • IFFCO (Indian Farmers Fertiliser Cooperative Limited) was set up in 1967 and is one of the world's largest cooperatives; India has had a separate Ministry of Cooperation since 2021.

Sports5/5

46th Chess Olympiad begins in Samarkand, Uzbekistan; India defend both titles

The 46th Chess Olympiad opened in Samarkand, Uzbekistan, on 15 September 2026, with 11 rounds from 16 to 27 September. India enter as defending champions in both the Open and Women's sections, having won both team gold medals at the 45th Olympiad in Budapest, Hungary, in 2024. The Olympiad is a team event organised by FIDE, the world chess body.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • FIDE was founded in Paris in 1924 and is headquartered in Lausanne, Switzerland.
  • India hosted the 44th Chess Olympiad at Mamallapuram, near Chennai, Tamil Nadu, in 2022.

  • CRPF की स्थापना 27 जुलाई 1939 को क्राउन रिप्रेज़ेंटेटिव्स पुलिस के रूप में हुई; यह सबसे बड़ा केंद्रीय सशस्त्र पुलिस बल है और गृह मंत्रालय के अधीन है।

एम. विश्वेश्वरैया की जयंती पर अभियंता दिवस

भारत 15 सितंबर 2026 को अभियंता दिवस (इंजीनियर्स डे) मना रहा है, जो 1861 में इसी दिन जन्मे भारत रत्न सर मोक्षगुंडम विश्वेश्वरैया के सम्मान में और ढाँचागत विकास, तकनीक व राष्ट्र निर्माण में इंजीनियरों के योगदान को याद करने के लिए है। 15 सितंबर को अंतरराष्ट्रीय लोकतंत्र दिवस भी है, जो लोकतांत्रिक मूल्यों, जन भागीदारी और मज़बूत लोकतांत्रिक संस्थाओं पर ज़ोर देता है।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • विश्वेश्वरैया का जन्म आज के कर्नाटक के मुद्देनहल्ली में हुआ; वे मैसूर के दीवान रहे और कृष्णराज सागर बाँध का डिज़ाइन बनाया; 1955 में उन्हें भारत रत्न मिला।
  • संयुक्त राष्ट्र महासभा ने 2007 में अंतरराष्ट्रीय लोकतंत्र दिवस घोषित किया; यह पहली बार 2008 में मनाया गया।
