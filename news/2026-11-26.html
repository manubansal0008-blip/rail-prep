Vaibhav Sooryavanshi hits 204 off 53 balls in Doha, setting world records for the fastest List A century and double century

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi scored 204 off just 53 balls as India 'A' beat the UAE by seven wickets in an Asia Cup Rising Stars group match in Doha, Qatar, on 24 November 2026. He reached his century in 28 balls and his double century in 52 balls, hitting 28 sixes and seven fours — world records for the fastest List A hundred and double hundred. Chasing 250, India 'A' won in only 14.2 overs, and Sooryavanshi was named Player of the Match.

  • Doha is the capital of Qatar

Defence Ministry adds two new exhibition hangars for Aero India 2027 in Bengaluru

The Ministry of Defence said on 25 November 2026 that it is expanding infrastructure for Aero India 2027 after demand from Indian and foreign exhibitors rose sharply. Government approves six brownfield urea plants worth about ₹54,000 crore under NIPU-2026

The Government on 25 November 2026 approved six brownfield urea projects of the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, with a total investment of about ₹54,000 crore, under the National Investment Policy for Urea (NIPU)-2026. Each plant will have an annual capacity of 12.7 lakh metric tonnes, at a cost of around ₹9,000 crore each. The plants are MFL Madras (Tamil Nadu), NFL Nangal (Punjab), KRIBHCO Fertilizers Shahjahanpur and IIPL Jagdishpur (Uttar Pradesh), Matix Panagarh (West Bengal) and CFCL Gadepan (Rajasthan).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • A brownfield project is built at an existing plant site, while a greenfield project starts on new land
  • Urea is the main nitrogen fertiliser and contains about 46% nitrogen

India wins the 'Best National Pavilion – Visitors' Lion' award at the Venice Art Biennale

India's pavilion at the Venice Art Biennale in Italy won the 'Best National Pavilion – Visitors' Lion' award, chosen by the votes of visitors from around the world, it was announced on 25 November 2026. Government scheme5/5

Cabinet approves ₹11,000 crore scheme to boost construction equipment manufacturing

The Union Cabinet on 25 November 2026 approved the Scheme for Enhancement of the Construction and Infrastructure Equipment (CIE) Manufacturing Ecosystem with an outlay of ₹11,000 crore for eight years, from 2026-27 to 2033-34. It will be run by the Ministry of Heavy Industries and covers seven mostly imported machines, including tunnel boring machines, excavators, cranes and piling rigs. India's CIE market, the world's third largest after China and the US, was about ₹97,500 crore in 2024-25.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The scheme has two parts: a sales-linked incentive and a grant for a CIE testing facility
  • The same Cabinet meeting approved a greenfield airport at Doloo, Silchar (Assam) for about ₹2,014 crore

  1. Ministry of Road Transport and Highways
  2. Ministry of Heavy Industries
  3. Ministry of Commerce and Industry
  4. Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs
Show answer

(B) Ministry of Heavy Industries The eight-year CIE scheme (2026-27 to 2033-34) will be implemented by the Ministry of Heavy Industries.
