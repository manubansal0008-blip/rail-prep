Economy / Banking3/5

Centre releases extra tax devolution of ₹1,09,019 crore to states

On 31 October 2026 the Union Government released an additional instalment of tax devolution of ₹1,09,019 crore to State Governments, over and above the regular monthly devolution. It was given ahead of the festive season and to help states speed up capital spending; it is the second additional instalment this financial year.
