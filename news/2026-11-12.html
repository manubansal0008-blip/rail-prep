India win women's 25m pistol team gold at ISSF World Championship in Doha

On 10 November 2026, Esha Singh (590), Manu Bhaker (587) and Divya Thadigol Subbaraju (584) won the women's 25m pistol team gold for India with a total of 1761 at the ISSF World Championship in Doha, Qatar. Korea (1748) took silver and Vietnam (1745) bronze. It was India's second gold at the championship. In the individual final Manu Bhaker finished fourth and just missed an LA 2028 Olympic quota.

Cabinet extends Stand-Up India Scheme for 5 more years, up to 31 March 2031

On 11 November 2026, the Union Cabinet chaired by PM Narendra Modi approved continuing the Stand-Up India Scheme for 5 years up to 31 March 2031. It gives bank loans above ₹10 lakh and up to ₹1 crore to women, SC and ST entrepreneurs for new (greenfield) enterprises, and aims to cover at least 2.5 lakh more beneficiaries. A credit guarantee corpus of ₹4,111 crore was approved. Since its launch in April 2016, it has given loans to more than 2.75 lakh entrepreneurs.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Loan size: above ₹10 lakh, up to ₹1 crore (up to 85% of project cost)
  • For women, SC and ST entrepreneurs; launched in April 2016

President Murmu presents Swachh Survekshan 2025-26 awards; Navi Mumbai, Surat, Vijayawada lead Super Swachh League

On 11 November 2026, President Droupadi Murmu presented the Swachh Survekshan 2025-26 awards, the 10th edition of the survey, at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi. First BIMSTEC Health Ministers' Meeting held in Colombo, adopts joint statement
