Santragachi–Khatipura (Jaipur) Express flagged off

Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw flagged off the new weekly Santragachi–Khatipura (Jaipur) Express through video conferencing on 26 October 2026, with events at Santragachi and Tatanagar stations. The train (18061/18062) links West Bengal, Jharkhand, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan via Tatanagar, Ranchi, Daltonganj, Sasaram, Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Jn, Prayagraj, Kanpur Central and Agra. Regular runs start on 4 November from Santragachi and 6 November from Khatipura.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The number of platforms at Tatanagar station is proposed to go up from five to nine.
  • Santragachi (Howrah district) is on South Eastern Railway (HQ: Kolkata); Khatipura in Jaipur is on North Western Railway (HQ: Jaipur).

Sports3/5

Ahmedabad to host the 2029 World Weightlifting Championships

The International Weightlifting Federation (IWF) has given the 2029 senior World Weightlifting Championships to Ahmedabad, Gujarat. The decision was taken at the IWF Executive Board meeting in Ningbo, China, and announced on 25 October 2026. It will be the first time India hosts the senior world championships; the 2030 edition went to Moscow.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Ahmedabad will also host the 2030 Commonwealth Games.
  • Mirabai Chanu won India's world weightlifting title in the 48 kg class in 2017 (Anaheim, USA).

Defence5/5

DAC clears defence proposals worth ₹2.12 lakh crore

The Defence Acquisition Council (DAC), chaired by Defence Minister Rajnath Singh, cleared capital acquisition proposals worth about ₹2.12 lakh crore on 26 October 2026. About 82% of the buying will be from Indian industry. The list includes Light Multi Role Helicopters, self-propelled tracked guns, very short-range air defence systems, counter-drone systems and long-range kamikaze drones for the Army, and helicopters and air defence systems for the Air Force.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The DAC is chaired by the Defence Minister; it was set up in 2001 after the Kargil war reforms.
  • The Navy items include Active Offboard Decoy Systems and platforms to be designed and built in India.
