India crosses 300 GW of non-fossil fuel power capacity

India's installed non-fossil fuel power capacity crossed 300 GW by 31 July 2026, more than 60% of the target of 500 GW by 2030, according to government data reported on 10 August 2026. Non-fossil sources now make up over 54% of the country's total installed capacity of 552 GW, led by solar at 164.59 GW and wind at 58.14 GW. A record 55.29 GW of non-fossil capacity was added in 2025-26.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The 500 GW non-fossil target by 2030 was announced as part of India's 'Panchamrit' pledges at COP26 in Glasgow in 2021, along with net-zero emissions by 2070.
  • The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) is the nodal ministry for renewable energy; the International Solar Alliance has its headquarters in Gurugram, Haryana.

Quick question: What is India's target for non-fossil fuel power capacity by 2030?

  1. 175 GW
  2. 300 GW
  3. 500 GW
  4. 1,000 GW
Show answer

(C) 500 GW India aims for 500 GW of non-fossil capacity by 2030; it crossed 300 GW by 31 July 2026.

CCI hosts meeting of BRICS competition authority heads in Udaipur

The Competition Commission of India (CCI) hosted the meeting of Heads of BRICS Competition Authorities in Udaipur, Rajasthan, on 8 August 2026 under India's BRICS Chairship. CCI Chairperson Ravneet Kaur chaired the meeting, which adopted a Joint Statement on 'Strengthening Cooperation to Promote Fair Competition, including in Renewable Energy Markets'. A BRICS study on the competition landscape in the renewable energy sector was presented, and Brazil's CADE briefed members on the 10th BRICS International Competition Conference due in 2027.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The CCI is a statutory body set up under the Competition Act, 2002, with its headquarters in New Delhi.
  • Udaipur, known as the 'City of Lakes', is in Rajasthan; the Competition Act, 2002 replaced the MRTP Act, 1969.

Supreme Court Collegium recommends Chief Justices for four High Courts

The Supreme Court Collegium headed by Chief Justice of India Surya Kant, at its meeting on 6 August 2026, recommended Chief Justices for four High Courts, The Hindu reported on 10 August 2026. Justice V. Kameswar Rao is recommended for Patna, Justice Ravindra V. Ghuge for Calcutta, Justice Mahesh Chandra Tripathi for Bombay and Justice Ashwani Kumar Mishra for Punjab and Haryana. Ghuge is currently the acting Chief Justice of the Bombay High Court.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • High Court judges, including Chief Justices, are appointed by the President under Article 217 of the Constitution.
  • Justice Surya Kant became the 53rd Chief Justice of India on 24 November 2025; the Calcutta High Court, set up in 1862, is India's oldest High Court.

Ladakh to begin population count with caste enumeration on 17 August

Ladakh will begin the Population Enumeration phase of Census 2027 on 17 August 2026, becoming the first region in independent India to conduct a caste count in the census, The Hindu reported on 10 August 2026. The Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India has notified early enumeration for Ladakh and other snow-bound areas, which will run until 30 September 2026, while the main national count is scheduled for February 2027. Respondents will declare their caste in an open-ended column, and Ladakh will use paper schedules instead of the digital mode.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The last full caste census in India was held in 1931; the census is conducted under the Census Act, 1948.
  • Ladakh became a Union Territory on 31 October 2019; Leh is its main administrative centre.

  4. 2011

World Lion Day: Gujarat carves out 100-hectare safe haven for conflict lions

World Lion Day is observed on 10 August, and ahead of it Gujarat has carved out a 100-hectare enclosure at the Ambardi Safari Park near Dhari in Amreli district to house Asiatic lions that have attacked humans, The Hindu reported on 10 August 2026. Such lions were earlier often shifted to the Sakkarbaug Zoo in Junagadh. Gujarat's Asiatic lion population rose to about 891 in 2025 from 674 in 2020.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Gir forest in Gujarat is the only wild home of the Asiatic lion, which is listed as Endangered by the IUCN.
  • The Asiatic lion is protected under Schedule I of the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972.

भारत की गैर-जीवाश्म ईंधन बिजली क्षमता 300 GW के पार

10 अगस्त 2026 को सामने आए सरकारी आंकड़ों के अनुसार 31 जुलाई 2026 तक भारत की गैर-जीवाश्म ईंधन आधारित स्थापित बिजली क्षमता 300 GW को पार कर गई, जो 2030 तक 500 GW के लक्ष्य का 60% से ज़्यादा है। देश की कुल 552 GW स्थापित क्षमता में अब गैर-जीवाश्म स्रोतों का हिस्सा 54% से ज़्यादा है; इसमें सौर ऊर्जा 164.59 GW और पवन ऊर्जा 58.14 GW के साथ सबसे आगे हैं। 2025-26 में रिकॉर्ड 55.29 GW गैर-जीवाश्म क्षमता जोड़ी गई।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें
  • 2030 तक 500 GW गैर-जीवाश्म क्षमता का लक्ष्य 2021 में ग्लासगो के COP26 में भारत के 'पंचामृत' संकल्पों में घोषित हुआ था, साथ ही 2070 तक नेट-ज़ीरो उत्सर्जन का लक्ष्य भी रखा गया।
  • नवीन और नवीकरणीय ऊर्जा मंत्रालय (MNRE) अक्षय ऊर्जा का नोडल मंत्रालय है; अंतरराष्ट्रीय सौर गठबंधन का मुख्यालय हरियाणा के गुरुग्राम में है।

  3. 500 GW
  4. 1,000 GW
