Gross GST collection rises 15.4% to over ₹2.11 lakh crore in July

Gross GST collection in July 2026 rose 15.4% year-on-year to over ₹2.11 lakh crore, up from ₹1.83 lakh crore in July 2025, according to data released on 1 August 2026. Net collection after refunds grew 15.8% to ₹1.81 lakh crore, while GST on imports jumped 28.8% to ₹66,511 crore and domestic gross revenue rose 10.1% to ₹1.45 lakh crore. Maharashtra recorded the highest state collection at ₹32,210 crore.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • GST came into force on 1 July 2017 through the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016; 1 July is observed as GST Day.
  • The GST Council (Article 279A) is chaired by the Union Finance Minister and includes the finance ministers of the states.

PM inaugurates Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport at Bhogapuram, Andhra Pradesh

Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the greenfield Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport at Bhogapuram in Vizianagaram district of Andhra Pradesh on 1 August 2026. Built at a cost of over ₹5,640 crore under the Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model, it can handle 6 million passengers a year in its first phase and has a 3.8-km runway for wide-body aircraft. Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu and Union Civil Aviation Minister K. Rammohan Naidu were present.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Alluri Sitarama Raju was a freedom fighter who led the Rampa Rebellion (1922–24) against British rule in the hill areas of the Godavari region.
  • Andhra State, formed on 1 October 1953, was India's first state created on a linguistic basis; Amaravati is the capital of Andhra Pradesh.

Vice Admiral A.N. Pramod takes charge as Deputy Chief of Naval Staff

Vice Admiral A.N. Pramod took charge as Deputy Chief of Naval Staff on 1 August 2026, succeeding Vice Admiral Tarun Sobti, who retired on 31 July after 38 years of service. Commissioned into the Navy on 1 July 1990, he is a Sea King air operations officer and a communication and electronic warfare specialist. He was earlier Director General Naval Operations during Operation Sindoor and holds the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal (2024) and Yudh Seva Medal (2025).

DSC A24, last of five Diving Support Craft for the Navy, launched in Kolkata

DSC A24, the fifth and final vessel of the Diving Support Craft project for the Indian Navy, was launched on 1 August 2026 at Titagarh Rail Systems Limited (TRSL), Kolkata. The craft has a catamaran (twin) hull and a displacement of roughly 300–400 tonnes. Diving support craft are used for diving operations close to the coast and in harbours.

Summits / Days5/5

World Breastfeeding Week 2026 begins on 1 August

World Breastfeeding Week is being observed from 1 to 7 August 2026 with the theme 'Breastfeeding for a Sustainable Start in Life: Strengthen What Works'. The week is coordinated by the World Alliance for Breastfeeding Action (WABA) together with WHO and UNICEF. It promotes starting breastfeeding within the first hour of birth, exclusive breastfeeding for six months and continued breastfeeding for two years or more.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The World Health Organization (WHO) was founded on 7 April 1948, observed as World Health Day; its headquarters is in Geneva, Switzerland.
  • UNICEF was set up in 1946 and is headquartered in New York.

1 अगस्त 2026 को जारी आंकड़ों के अनुसार जुलाई 2026 में सकल GST संग्रह पिछले साल की तुलना में 15.4% बढ़कर ₹2.11 लाख करोड़ से ज़्यादा रहा, जो जुलाई 2025 में ₹1.83 लाख करोड़ था। रिफ़ंड के बाद शुद्ध संग्रह 15.8% बढ़कर ₹1.81 लाख करोड़ रहा, जबकि आयात पर GST 28.8% उछलकर ₹66,511 करोड़ और घरेलू सकल राजस्व 10.1% बढ़कर ₹1.45 लाख करोड़ हो गया। राज्यों में सबसे ज़्यादा ₹32,210 करोड़ का संग्रह महाराष्ट्र में हुआ।

  • GST परिषद (अनुच्छेद 279A) की अध्यक्षता केंद्रीय वित्त मंत्री करते हैं और इसमें राज्यों के वित्त मंत्री भी शामिल होते हैं।

  • भारत के राष्ट्रपति सशस्त्र बलों के सर्वोच्च कमांडर होते हैं; भारतीय नौसेना का मुख्यालय नई दिल्ली में है।
