PM Modi and Polish PM Donald Tusk hold talks at Hyderabad House

Prime Minister Narendra Modi held talks with Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk at Hyderabad House, New Delhi, on 22 October 2026, during Tusk's three-day visit to India (21–23 October). The two leaders set a target of doubling bilateral trade by 2030, proposed a Defence Industry Dialogue, and called for early signing of the India–EU Free Trade Agreement. Poland announced it will join the Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure (CDRI).

Sunil Kumar named next Chairman of ONGC

The Appointments Committee of the Cabinet, by an order dated 21 October 2026, approved ONGC Executive Director Sunil Kumar as the next Chairman of Oil and Natural Gas Corporation. Government scheme3/5

Khet Bachao Abhiyan–Rabi 2026 launched from Karnal

Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan launched the nationwide 'Khet Bachao Abhiyan–Rabi 2026' from Karnal, Haryana, on 22 October 2026. The campaign runs till 30 November and promotes soil health, balanced use of fertilisers based on soil testing, crop diversification towards oilseeds and pulses, crop-residue management and natural farming. Krishi Vigyan Kendras, ICAR institutes and agricultural universities will hold Kisan Chaupals and field demonstrations down to the Gram Panchayat level.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Rabi crops such as wheat, mustard and gram are sown in October–December and harvested in March–April.
  • Karnal is home to ICAR's National Dairy Research Institute (NDRI).

Defence4/5

Defence Ministry signs ₹770 crore contract with BEL for low-level radars

The Ministry of Defence signed a contract worth about ₹770 crore with Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL) in New Delhi on 21 October 2026 for the Improved Low Level Light Weight Radar (LLLR-I). The radar can detect and track fighter-sized aircraft as well as mini and micro drones. The Indian Army will use it against threats from unmanned aerial systems.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • BEL is a Navratna defence PSU headquartered in Bengaluru, set up in 1954.
  • The contract was signed in the presence of Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh.

Sports5/5

Sheetal Devi and Toman Kumar win compound mixed team archery gold at Asian Para Games

Sheetal Devi and Toman Kumar won the compound mixed team archery gold at the Asian Para Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, on 22 October 2026, beating Indonesia 151-148 in the final. It was India's first archery gold of these Games and its 16th gold overall. Earlier the same day, Suvarna Raj won the women's discus throw F54 gold.
