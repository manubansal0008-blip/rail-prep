Amit Shah chairs 28th Western Zonal Council meeting in Panaji

Union Home Minister Amit Shah chaired the 28th meeting of the Western Zonal Council in Panaji, Goa, on 7 September 2026. Goa Chief Minister Pramod Sawant, Gujarat Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel and Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis attended. The meeting discussed issues between the Centre and member States that need coordination, such as fast-track special courts, the ERSS-112 emergency line, Ayushman Bharat–PM-JAY and railway projects.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Western Zonal Council has Goa, Gujarat, Maharashtra and the UT of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu as members.
  • Zonal Councils were set up under the States Reorganisation Act, 1956; the Union Home Minister is the chairman of each council.

India, China hold first Corps Commander-level talks in Arunachal Pradesh

India and China held their first-ever Corps Commander-level talks in the eastern sector on 6 September 2026, at the Wacha-Damai Border Personnel Meeting (BPM) point in the Kibithu sector of Anjaw district, Arunachal Pradesh. Until now such talks were held only at Chushul-Moldo in eastern Ladakh. The talks focused on easing local tensions along the Line of Actual Control (LAC) and came after NSA Ajit Doval met Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi in Beijing on 25 August.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Kibithu in Anjaw district is one of India's easternmost inhabited places; the capital of Arunachal Pradesh is Itanagar.
  • The LAC is the de facto India–China boundary and is about 3,488 km long; it has not been formally demarcated.

Clean Air Day: Lucknow tops 5th Swachh Vayu Sarvekshan

The Environment Ministry marked the International Day of Clean Air for Blue Skies as Swachh Vayu Diwas 2026 in New Delhi on 7 September and gave away the 5th Swachh Vayu Sarvekshan Awards. Lucknow ranked first among cities with over 10 lakh population, scoring 198 of 200 points and winning ₹1.5 crore, followed by Indore and Jabalpur. Rourkela topped the 3–10 lakh category and Kalinga Nagar the below-3 lakh category; 108 of 130 NCAP cities showed lower PM10 levels than in 2017-18.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The International Day of Clean Air for Blue Skies (7 September) was declared by the UN General Assembly in 2019 and first observed in 2020.
  • The National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) was launched in 2019; the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) works under the Environment Ministry.

Economy / Banking4/5

DGFT launches Open API for Certificates of Origin

The Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) on 7 September 2026 issued Trade Notice No. 25/2026-27, launching an Open API facility for Certificates of Origin (CoOs) on the Trade Connect e-Platform. Exporters can now file CoO applications directly from their ERP or accounting software through three APIs – for authentication, CoO filing and certificate verification. It covers preferential CoOs under trade pacts such as India–UAE CEPA, India–Australia ECTA, India–EFTA TEPA and India–UK CETA, as well as non-preferential CoOs.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • A Certificate of Origin shows the country where goods were made; it is needed to claim lower duty under a Free Trade Agreement.
  • DGFT works under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry and is headquartered in New Delhi.

Quick question: On which DGFT platform was the Open API facility for Certificates of Origin launched in September 2026?

  1. GeM
  2. ICEGATE
  3. Trade Connect e-Platform
  4. Niryat Bandhu
Show answer

(C) Trade Connect e-Platform The Open API for Certificates of Origin was launched on DGFT's Trade Connect e-Platform through Trade Notice No. 25/2026-27 dated 7 September 2026.

Bhagwat Prasad Makwana named Chairperson of NCSK

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment announced on 6 September 2026 the appointment of Bhagwat Prasad Makwana as Chairperson and Kaishab Bihari as Vice-Chairperson of the National Commission for Safai Karamcharis (NCSK). विदेश व्यापार महानिदेशालय (DGFT) ने 7 सितंबर 2026 को ट्रेड नोटिस संख्या 25/2026-27 जारी कर ट्रेड कनेक्ट ई-प्लेटफ़ॉर्म पर उत्पत्ति प्रमाणपत्र (Certificate of Origin – CoO) के लिए ओपन API सुविधा शुरू की। अब निर्यातक अपने ERP या अकाउंटिंग सॉफ़्टवेयर से सीधे CoO के लिए आवेदन कर सकेंगे; इसके लिए तीन API हैं – प्रमाणीकरण, CoO फ़ाइलिंग और प्रमाणपत्र सत्यापन। यह सुविधा भारत–UAE CEPA, भारत–ऑस्ट्रेलिया ECTA, भारत–EFTA TEPA और भारत–UK CETA जैसे व्यापार समझौतों के तहत रियायती CoO के साथ-साथ गैर-रियायती CoO पर भी लागू है।
