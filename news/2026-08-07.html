Nuclear-capable Agni-4 ballistic missile test-fired from Chandipur

India successfully test-fired the nuclear-capable Agni-4 ballistic missile from the Integrated Test Range (ITR), Chandipur, Odisha, on 6 August 2026. The launch was carried out under the Strategic Forces Command (SFC) with DRDO. Agni-4 is a two-stage, solid-fuel surface-to-surface missile with a strike range of about 4,000 km and can carry a payload of about 1,000 kg.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Strategic Forces Command, set up in 2003, manages India's nuclear arsenal and acts on the directions of the Nuclear Command Authority.
  • DRDO (Defence Research and Development Organisation) was formed in 1958 and has its headquarters in New Delhi; Chandipur is in Balasore district of Odisha.

National Handloom Day observed on 7 August

National Handloom Day was observed on 7 August 2026 to honour India's handloom weavers and mark the Swadeshi Movement launched on 7 August 1905. According to the Fourth All India Handloom Census (2019-20), the sector has 35.22 lakh weavers and allied workers, over 72% of them women. Handloom exports were about ₹1,330.96 crore in 2025-26, with the UAE, USA, France, UK and Spain as the top buyers.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The first National Handloom Day was celebrated on 7 August 2015 in Chennai, inaugurated by Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
  • The Handlooms (Reservation of Articles for Production) Act, 1985 reserves certain textile items for production only on handlooms; the sector comes under the Ministry of Textiles.

  1. Non-Cooperation Movement
  2. Quit India Movement
  3. Civil Disobedience Movement
  4. Swadeshi Movement
Show answer

(D) Swadeshi Movement The Swadeshi Movement was launched on 7 August 1905; National Handloom Day has been observed on that date since 2015.

Titli Maji wins silver, India's first medal at Asian Youth and Junior Weightlifting Championships

Titli Maji won the silver medal in the women's 45 kg category at the Asian Youth and Junior Weightlifting Championships in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, All India Radio reported on 7 August 2026; it was India's first medal of the event. GeM crosses ₹20 lakh crore in total transactions ahead of 10th anniversary

The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) has crossed a cumulative Gross Merchandise Value of ₹20 lakh crore in ten years, CEO Mihir Kumar said at a press conference in New Delhi on 7 August 2026. Business on the portal has grown from ₹422 crore in 2016-17, sellers from about 3,000 to over 25 lakh, and registered Micro and Small Enterprises from about 2,000 to over 12 lakh. All States are now on board, and GeM plans to extend to Urban Local Bodies and Gram Panchayats.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • GeM, the national public procurement portal, was launched on 9 August 2016 and works under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
  • Government purchases on GeM follow the General Financial Rules (GFR); an IIT Delhi study found GeM prices lower on 74% of items compared with major e-commerce sites.

  2. 15 August 2015
  3. 9 August 2016
  4. 1 April 2018
Show answer

(C) 9 August 2016 GeM was launched on 9 August 2016; by August 2026 its cumulative GMV had crossed ₹20 lakh crore.

Science / Space5/5

Landslide-hit Wayanad to get X-band Doppler weather radar by December 2026

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) is installing an X-band Doppler Weather Radar at Pulinjaly in Wayanad, Kerala, to be commissioned by December 2026, the Indian Express reported on 7 August 2026. The radar, on land given by Pazhassi Raja College, will improve forecasts of extreme rainfall and strengthen early warnings in the hilly district. Wayanad suffered major rain-triggered landslides in August 2019 and July 2024; the 2024 disaster killed about 420 people.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • X-band radars work at 8–12 GHz (wavelength 2–4 cm) and cover about 100–150 km, which suits hilly terrain.
  • The IMD, set up in 1875, works under the Ministry of Earth Sciences and has its headquarters at Mausam Bhawan, New Delhi.

  3. C-band
  4. L-band
Show answer

(B) X-band IMD is installing an X-band Doppler Weather Radar at Pulinjaly in Wayanad, to be commissioned by December 2026.

इंडियन एक्सप्रेस ने 7 अगस्त 2026 को बताया कि भारत मौसम विज्ञान विभाग (IMD) केरल के वायनाड में पुलिंजाली में X-बैंड डॉप्लर मौसम रडार लगा रहा है, जो दिसंबर 2026 तक चालू होगा। पझस्सी राजा कॉलेज की दी गई ज़मीन पर लगने वाला यह रडार इस पहाड़ी ज़िले में भारी बारिश के पूर्वानुमान और समय पर चेतावनी को बेहतर बनाएगा। वायनाड में अगस्त 2019 और जुलाई 2024 में बारिश से बड़े भूस्खलन हुए थे; 2024 की आपदा में करीब 420 लोगों की जान गई थी।
