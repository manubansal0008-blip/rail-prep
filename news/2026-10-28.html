Cyclone Arnab makes landfall on the Andhra Pradesh coast

Very severe cyclonic storm Arnab began crossing the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts near Kakinada at 1:20 am on 28 October 2026, the IMD said. Sustained winds were 115–125 km/h with gusts up to 140 km/h. Heavy rain hit coastal Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha, where a red warning was issued for five districts.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The name 'Arnab' was proposed by Bangladesh; it is from the list of 169 names (13 countries × 13 names) adopted in April 2020.
  • Yanam is part of the Union Territory of Puducherry, though it lies inside Andhra Pradesh.

Jio Platforms lists after India's biggest IPO

Jio Platforms shares started trading on the NSE and BSE on 28 October 2026. PM holds talks with Liechtenstein's Hereditary Prince Alois

Prime Minister Narendra Modi held delegation-level talks with Hereditary Prince Alois of Liechtenstein at Hyderabad House, New Delhi, on 27 October 2026.
