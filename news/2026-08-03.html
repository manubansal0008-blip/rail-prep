Indian Organ Donation Day: 'Jug Jug Jiyo' campaign and e-Pratyaropan platform launched

The 16th Indian Organ Donation Day was observed on 3 August 2026. Union Minister of State for Health Anupriya Patel launched the year-long 'Jug Jug Jiyo' campaign to honour deceased donors and their families, and the e-Pratyaropan digital platform for organ and tissue donation and transplantation, run by NOTTO. The day marks India's first successful deceased-donor heart transplant, performed at AIIMS, New Delhi, on 3 August 1994.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Organ donation and transplantation in India are governed by the Transplantation of Human Organs Act, 1994 (now the Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act).
  • NOTTO (National Organ and Tissue Transplant Organisation), under the Union Health Ministry, is located in New Delhi.

President gives assent to Maharashtra Freedom of Religion Act, 2026

President Droupadi Murmu has given her assent to the Maharashtra Freedom of Religion Act, 2026, which will come into force once the state government issues a notification. The law bars religious conversion through force, fraud, inducement or marriage, while allowing voluntary conversion after 60 days' advance notice to the district magistrate. The burden of proving that a conversion was voluntary lies on the person carrying it out, and offences can bring up to 7 years in jail (up to 10 years for repeat offences).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Article 25 of the Constitution guarantees freedom of conscience and the right to freely profess, practise and propagate religion, subject to public order, morality and health.
  • Under Article 200 a Governor can reserve a state Bill for the President's consideration; the President then acts under Article 201.

International5/5

Italy suspends Schengen border-free travel with Spain after Ceuta migrant surge

Italy has suspended Schengen border-free travel with Spain for one month and brought back document checks for air and sea arrivals from Spain, it was reported on 3 August 2026. The move followed a crisis in which about 60,000 migrants crossed from Morocco into Spain's North African enclave of Ceuta within 24 hours, and at least 57 people died. Italy is also working with France to tighten checks on the Franco-Italian land border.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Schengen Agreement was signed in 1985 at Schengen, a village in Luxembourg; the Schengen Area allows travel without internal border checks.
  • Ceuta and Melilla are Spain's two autonomous cities in North Africa and form the EU's only land border with Africa.
