India win first overall title at Commonwealth Fencing Championship 2026 with 35 medals

India won the overall title at the Commonwealth Fencing Championship 2026 in Lagos, Nigeria, for the first time, All India Radio and India Today reported on 13 August 2026. India finished with 35 medals: 13 gold, 8 silver and 14 bronze across the Under-23 and senior events. On the fourth and final day, Indian fencers won gold in five of the six senior team events (men's sabre, women's sabre, men's epee, women's epee and women's foil). India also got the Wilkinson Sword Trophy for the best overall nation.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • Fencing has three weapons: foil, epee and sabre.

Science / Space2/5

ISRO tests improved first stage of SSLV; payload to rise by about 100 kg

ISRO said on 12 August 2026 that it had successfully carried out a ground static test of an improved version of the solid booster first stage (SS1) of the Small Satellite Launch Vehicle (SSLV) on 11 August 2026 at Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota. The changes include a faster propellant burn rate in two motor segments, better heat protection, an easier-to-make nozzle and lower mass. More than 600 parameters were measured. With this SS1, SSLV can carry about 100 kg more to low Earth orbit.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • SSLV is ISRO's smallest rocket, made for quick, low-cost, launch-on-demand missions for small satellites; its technology is being transferred to Indian industry.
  • Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SHAR), India's main spaceport, is at Sriharikota in Andhra Pradesh.

  2. 10 kg
  3. 500 kg
  4. 1,000 kg
Show answer

(A) 100 kg ISRO estimates the improved first stage (SS1) will raise SSLV's low Earth orbit payload by about 100 kg.

Source: ISRO, 12 Aug 2026 · The Week, 13 Aug 2026

Law Ministry launches revamped India Code portal with AI-assisted search

Arjun Ram Meghwal, Minister of State (Independent Charge) for Law and Justice, launched the revamped India Code portal at Kartavya Bhavan 2, New Delhi, on 13 August 2026. India Code is a single digital store of Central Acts, State Acts and Union Territory laws with their rules. The new portal adds AI-assisted, meaning-based and voice search, easier navigation, amendment history, links with the e-Gazette and QR-based access. It also aims to give AI-assisted access to laws in all 22 languages of the Eighth Schedule.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Eighth Schedule of the Constitution lists 22 official languages.
  • Officials of the National Informatics Centre (NIC) and the IndiaAI Mission were present at the launch; India Code is run by the Legislative Department.

  4. 22
Show answer

(D) 22 It aims at AI-assisted access to laws in all 22 languages of the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution.

NITI Aayog report names four key sectors to make India a global manufacturing hub

NITI Aayog released a report titled 'Key Sectors to Position India as a Global Manufacturing Hub' on 13 August 2026. This edition studies four sectors: chemicals, textiles, telecom and networking equipment, and solar photovoltaic (PV) manufacturing, and gives sector-wise steps to raise competitiveness and exports. Reports on 8 more sectors will follow. It notes that textiles and apparel give about 2% of GDP and are the second-largest employer after agriculture.

IAF Rafales train with Malaysian jets in Exercise Udara Shakti 2026

The Indian Air Force and the Royal Malaysian Air Force are holding the bilateral Exercise Udara Shakti 2026 at Subang Air Base, Malaysia, from 10 to 14 August 2026, reports said on 13 August.
