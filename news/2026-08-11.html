Nation pays homage to Khudiram Bose on his martyrdom day

The nation paid tribute to young revolutionary Khudiram Bose on his martyrdom day on 11 August 2026; Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath called him a brave son of Mother India who gave his life for the country at just 18. Born on 3 December 1889 in Medinipur (now Paschim Medinipur, West Bengal), Bose was hanged by the British on 11 August 1908. He is remembered as the youngest freedom fighter to be executed during British rule.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • With Prafulla Chaki, Khudiram Bose threw a bomb at a carriage in Muzaffarpur (Bihar) on 30 April 1908, believing Magistrate Douglas Kingsford was inside; two British women were killed instead.
  • Prafulla Chaki shot himself to avoid arrest, while Khudiram Bose was caught, tried and sentenced to death.

Quick question: On which date was Khudiram Bose hanged by the British?

  1. 23 March 1931
  2. 30 April 1908
  3. 11 August 1908
  4. 3 December 1889
Show answer

(C) 11 August 1908 Khudiram Bose was hanged on 11 August 1908, at the age of 18; 30 April 1908 was the Muzaffarpur bomb attack and 3 December 1889 his birth date.

ICMR-SHINE 2026 opens health research labs to 30,000 school students at 83 locations

The second edition of ICMR-SHINE (Science and Health Innovation for NextGen Explorers) began on 11 August 2026 as a two-day nationwide open-day programme of the Department of Health Research (DHR) and the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR). About 30,000 students of Classes 9 to 12 from government and private schools are expected to visit 83 locations, including ICMR institutes and Model Rural Health Research Units. DHR Secretary and ICMR Director General Dr Rajiv Bahl inaugurated an exhibition of ICMR research, and the official SHINE website was launched.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • ICMR, headquartered in New Delhi, is India's apex body for biomedical research; it began in 1911 as the Indian Research Fund Association and was renamed ICMR in 1949.
  • The Department of Health Research (DHR) works under the Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare; its Secretary is also the Director General of ICMR.

Centre notifies 11 more international ports for entry of e-visa holders

The Union Home Ministry has notified 11 more international ports for the entry of foreigners holding an Indian e-visa, All India Radio reported on 11 August 2026. The new ports are 9 land ports and 2 airports, including Bhopal and Tirupati airports and the Attari and Agartala land ports. With this, e-visa holders can now enter India through 88 ports: 37 airports, 38 seaports and 13 land ports.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Attari land port is in Amritsar district, Punjab, on the India-Pakistan border; Agartala, capital of Tripura, has an Integrated Check Post on the India-Bangladesh border.
  • Visas and the entry of foreigners into India come under the Ministry of Home Affairs.

8th India-US Navy Explosive Ordnance Disposal exercise under way in Kochi

The eighth edition of the Indian Navy-US Navy Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) Exercise 2026 got under way at the Southern Naval Command in Kochi on 10 August 2026 and will run until 14 August, All India Radio reported on 11 August. Specialist diving and EOD teams of the two navies are taking part in the five-day exercise. It includes subject matter expert exchanges, cross-training, equipment demonstrations and scenario-based practical drills.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) teams detect, make safe and destroy mines, bombs and other explosive devices, including underwater.

11 अगस्त 2026 को देश ने युवा क्रांतिकारी खुदीराम बोस को उनके बलिदान दिवस पर श्रद्धांजलि दी; उत्तर प्रदेश के मुख्यमंत्री योगी आदित्यनाथ ने उन्हें भारत माता का वीर सपूत बताया, जिन्होंने सिर्फ़ 18 साल की उम्र में देश के लिए प्राण दे दिए। 3 दिसंबर 1889 को मेदिनीपुर (अब पश्चिम मेदिनीपुर, पश्चिम बंगाल) में जन्मे बोस को अंग्रेज़ों ने 11 अगस्त 1908 को फांसी दी थी। उन्हें ब्रिटिश शासन में फांसी पाने वाले सबसे कम उम्र के स्वतंत्रता सेनानी के रूप में याद किया जाता है।
