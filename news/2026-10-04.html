Asian Games end in Nagoya; India finish 4th with 85 medals

The 20th Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan (19 September–4 October 2026) ended on 4 October 2026 with the closing ceremony at Nagoya's Mizuho Park Athletic Stadium. Government scheme2/5

PM-SETU completes one year; 850 ITIs identified for upgrade

PM-SETU (Pradhan Mantri Skilling and Employability Transformation through Upgraded ITIs), launched on 4 October 2025, completed one year on 4 October 2026. In its first year, 850 government ITIs (172 hubs and 678 spokes) were identified, and investment plans worth ₹3,446 crore were approved for 14 ITI clusters. The ₹60,000 crore scheme aims to upgrade 1,000 government ITIs in a hub-and-spoke model of 200 hubs and 800 spokes.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The ₹60,000 crore outlay is shared by the Centre (₹30,000 crore), States (₹20,000 crore) and industry (₹10,000 crore).
  • PM-SETU is run by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship; ITIs come under its Directorate General of Training (DGT).

Quick question: How many government ITIs does the PM-SETU scheme aim to upgrade?

  1. 1,000
  2. 850
  3. 500
  4. 1,500
Show answer

(A) 1,000 PM-SETU (₹60,000 crore) aims to upgrade 1,000 government ITIs – 200 hubs and 800 spokes. 850 were identified in its first year.

Science / Space3/5

National Supercomputing Mission: 40 supercomputers with 68 petaflops

A government update on 4 October 2026 said 40 supercomputers with a combined capacity of 68 petaflops (PF) had been deployed under the National Supercomputing Mission (NSM) as of September 2026. The plan is to reach 50 systems with more than 123 PF. The Mission was launched in April 2015 with an outlay of about ₹4,500 crore and is steered jointly by the Department of Science and Technology and the Ministry of Electronics and IT.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • NSM is implemented by C-DAC (Centre for Development of Advanced Computing), Pune, and IISc, Bengaluru.
  • PARAM 8000, built by C-DAC in 1991, was India's first indigenous supercomputer.

  4. 52

Two captive-bred Great Indian Bustards released into the wild in Jaisalmer

Union Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav released two captive-bred male Great Indian Bustards at Sam in the Desert National Park, Jaisalmer, Rajasthan, on 4 October 2026. The birds were raised under India's conservation breeding programme, which has a breeding facility at Ramdevra. The minister called it an important step after nearly a decade of work by scientists and ground staff.

Summits / Days5/5

World Space Week 2026 begins with the theme 'Rocket Revolution'

World Space Week is being observed from 4 to 10 October 2026 with the theme 'Rocket Revolution', which highlights the new technologies and organisations changing how humanity reaches space. The week begins on 4 October, the date Sputnik 1 was launched in 1957, and ends on 10 October, the date the Outer Space Treaty came into force in 1967.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The UN General Assembly declared 4–10 October as World Space Week in 1999.
  • Sputnik 1, launched by the Soviet Union on 4 October 1957, was the world's first artificial satellite.

  4. Space and Entrepreneurship

'रॉकेट रेवोल्यूशन' थीम के साथ विश्व अंतरिक्ष सप्ताह 2026 शुरू

विश्व अंतरिक्ष सप्ताह 4 से 10 अक्टूबर 2026 तक 'रॉकेट रेवोल्यूशन' (Rocket Revolution) थीम के साथ मनाया जा रहा है, जो अंतरिक्ष तक पहुँचने के तरीक़े बदल रही नई तकनीकों और संस्थाओं को सामने लाती है। सप्ताह 4 अक्टूबर से शुरू होता है, जिस दिन 1957 में स्पुतनिक-1 लॉन्च हुआ था, और 10 अक्टूबर को ख़त्म होता है, जिस दिन 1967 में बाह्य अंतरिक्ष संधि लागू हुई थी।
