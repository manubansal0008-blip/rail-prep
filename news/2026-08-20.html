Cabinet approves four rail multitracking projects worth ₹9,450 crore adding about 410 km

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approved four Railway multitracking projects worth about ₹9,450 crore on 19 August 2026, covering eight districts in West Bengal, Odisha, Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh. They are the Kharagpur–Bhadrak (Ranital) 4th line (173 km), Bhadrak–Haridaspur 4th line (75 km), Gummidipundi–Gudur 3rd and 4th lines (90 km) and Cuttack–Paradeep (Badabandha) 3rd and 4th lines (72 km), adding about 410 km by 2030-31. They will serve about 6,448 villages (about 60 lakh people) and carry about 76 million tonnes of extra freight a year.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Kharagpur is in the South Eastern Railway zone (HQ Kolkata), while Bhadrak and Cuttack fall in the East Coast Railway zone (HQ Bhubaneswar).
  • Paradip Port in Odisha's Jagatsinghpur district is a major port on the east coast; Gudur is a junction on the Chennai–Vijayawada main line.

States2/5

Cabinet clears four-laning of NH-22 Muzaffarpur–Sitamarhi–Sonbarsa stretch in Bihar

The Union Cabinet on 19 August 2026 approved upgrading the 82.578 km Muzaffarpur–Sitamarhi–Sonbarsa section of NH-22 in Bihar to four lanes at a cost of ₹3,590.73 crore. The project will be built in the Hybrid Annuity Mode and will link the India–Nepal border at Sonbarsa with Muzaffarpur and the East-West Corridor (NH-27). Along with four railway projects, the Cabinet cleared works worth ₹13,041 crore that day.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Under the Hybrid Annuity Mode (HAM), the government pays 40% of the project cost during construction and the developer arranges the remaining 60%.
  • The East-West Corridor of the National Highways Development Project (NHDP) runs from Porbandar in Gujarat to Silchar in Assam.

World Mosquito Day observed on 20 August

World Mosquito Day was observed on 20 August 2026 to spread awareness about mosquito-borne diseases such as malaria and dengue and how to prevent them. The day marks the discovery by Sir Ronald Ross on 20 August 1897, in Secunderabad, that female Anopheles mosquitoes transmit malaria. 20 August is also observed in India as Sadbhavana Diwas, on the birth anniversary of former Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Ronald Ross received the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 1902 for his work on malaria.
  • Rajiv Gandhi was born on 20 August 1944; 20 August is also observed as Akshay Urja Diwas (renewable energy day) in India.

Army Chief Gen Dhiraj Seth concludes Nepal visit; made honorary General of Nepali Army

Chief of the Army Staff General Dhiraj Seth concluded a four-day visit to Nepal (16–19 August 2026) made at the invitation of Nepali Army chief General Ashok Raj Sigdel.
