Science / Space5/5

UN Biodiversity Conference COP17 to open in Yerevan, Armenia

The 17th Conference of the Parties (COP17) to the UN Convention on Biological Diversity opens in Yerevan, Armenia, on 19 October 2026 and runs till 30 October. It will carry out the first global review of progress on the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework adopted in 2022.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Convention on Biological Diversity was opened for signature at the Rio Earth Summit in 1992; its secretariat is in Montreal, Canada.
  • India hosted CBD COP11 in Hyderabad in 2012.

Quick question: Which city hosts COP17 of the Convention on Biological Diversity (19–30 October 2026)?

  1. Baku
  2. Yerevan
  3. Cali
  4. Tbilisi
Show answer

(B) Yerevan CBD COP17 is being held in Yerevan, Armenia, from 19 to 30 October 2026. (COP16 was in Cali, Colombia.)

Source: Convention on Biological Diversity, COP17 · FPWC, COP17 dates
