Over 2,200 trains shifted to Indian Railways' revamped Passenger Reservation System built by CRIS

Indian Railways has moved more than 2,200 trains to its upgraded Passenger Reservation System (PRS), a Railway Board official said (report of 15 November 2026). The shift began in August 2026 to revamp the 40-year-old PRS, and the new system is being developed by the Centre for Railway Information Systems (CRIS).

📌 Remember for the exam

  • About 89% of train ticket demand is now met online

National Health Accounts 2023-24: families' out-of-pocket share of health spending falls to 42.7%

The Union Health Ministry's National Health Accounts (NHA) estimates for 2023-24, released on 15 November 2026, show that out-of-pocket expenditure (OOPE) fell to 42.7% of total health expenditure, from 64.2% in 2013-14. Government health expenditure rose about three times, from ₹1.30 lakh crore to ₹4.14 lakh crore.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • OOPE share: 64.2% (2013-14) → 43.4% (2022-23) → 42.7% (2023-24)
  • Per capita government health spending: ₹1,042 (2013-14) → ₹2,959 (2023-24)

  3. 42.7%
  4. 48.8%
Show answer

(C) 42.7% NHA 2023-24 puts out-of-pocket expenditure at 42.7% of total health expenditure, down from 43.4% in 2022-23 and 64.2% in 2013-14.

Source: PIB (Health Ministry), 15 Nov 2026
