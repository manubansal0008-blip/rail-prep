Economy / Banking5/5

New Bankers' Books Evidence Act, 2026 to replace 135-year-old law from 1 October

The Bankers' Books Evidence Act, 2026 will come into force on 1 October 2026, replacing the Bankers' Books Evidence Act, 1891, as reported on 12 September 2026. The new law, which received Presidential assent on 13 August 2026, covers physical, electronic, digital and cloud-based bank records and allows digital signatures for certifying copies. The Centre can extend it to other financial entities by notification.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The 1891 Act let certified copies of entries in bank books be used as evidence in courts without producing the original ledgers.
  • A Bill becomes an Act after the President's assent under Article 111 of the Constitution.

Quick question: The Bankers' Books Evidence Act, 2026, effective from 1 October 2026, replaces an Act of which year?

  2. 1934
  3. 1891
  4. 1949
Show answer
