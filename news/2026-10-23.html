Ladakh notifies 100 constituencies for its Hill Development Councils

Ladakh Lieutenant Governor Vinai Kumar Saxena on 21 October 2026 notified 100 constituencies across eight Ladakh Autonomous Hill Development Councils (LAHDCs), up from 52. Leh gets 22 seats, Kargil 21, Sankoo 13, Changthang 10, Nubra, Sham and Zanskar 9 each, and Drass 7. The change came into force on its publication in the gazette.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Ladakh became a Union Territory on 31 October 2019.
  • The first hill council, LAHDC Leh, was set up in 1995; LAHDC Kargil followed in 2003.

NLC India signs MoUs for ₹37,000 crore investment in Odisha

NLC India Limited signed MoUs with Odisha's investment agency IPICOL in Mumbai on 22 October 2026 for investments of about ₹37,000 crore in Odisha. They cover a 3,200 MW thermal power plant (about ₹35,000 crore), a ₹2,000 crore beach sand mineral processing unit, and a feasibility study on coal gasification for fertiliser. Odisha Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi witnessed the signing.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • IPICOL is the Industrial Promotion and Investment Corporation of Odisha Limited.

Science / Space3/5

NASA's Perseverance rover sets new distance record on Mars

NASA announced on 12 October 2026 that its Perseverance rover had driven 45.22 km on Mars by 11 October, more than any other rover. It beat the 45.16 km record set by the Opportunity rover, helped by its autonomous navigation system.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Perseverance landed in Jezero Crater on Mars on 18 February 2021.
  • India's Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan) reached Mars orbit on 24 September 2014, making India the first Asian country to do so.

  1. Curiosity
  2. Spirit
  3. Opportunity
  4. Sojourner
Show answer

(C) Opportunity Perseverance has driven 45.22 km on Mars, beating the 45.16 km record of the Opportunity rover.

Defence4/5

DRDO shortlists two firms for India's first indigenous 20 mm aviation gun

As reported on 21 October 2026, DRDO's Armament Research and Development Establishment (ARDE) has shortlisted SSS Defence and the Kalyani Group as development-cum-production partners for a 20 mm aviation gun and turret system. The guns are meant for HAL's ALH Mk-IV 'Rudra' and the Light Combat Helicopter 'Prachand'. Development is expected to take about two years, with production split 60% to the lowest bidder and 40% to the other firm.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • ARDE is a DRDO laboratory located in Pune, Maharashtra.
  • LCH Prachand is the world's first attack helicopter able to land and take off at 5,000 m altitude; it was inducted into the IAF in October 2022.

Quick question: The indigenous 20 mm aviation gun being developed by DRDO's ARDE is meant for which helicopters?

  1. Chinook and Apache
  2. Rudra and Prachand
  3. Chetak and Cheetah
  4. Dhruv Mk-III and MH-60R
Show answer

(B) Rudra and Prachand The 20 mm aviation gun and turret system is for HAL's ALH Mk-IV 'Rudra' and the LCH 'Prachand'. SSS Defence and the Kalyani Group were shortlisted.

Summits / Days5/5

International Day of the Snow Leopard observed on 23 October

The International Day of the Snow Leopard was observed on 23 October 2026 to promote the protection of snow leopards and their mountain habitats. The UN General Assembly formally proclaimed the day through a resolution adopted on 12 December 2024; it had been observed since 2014.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The snow leopard is the state animal of Himachal Pradesh and Ladakh.
  • India's first scientific count (SPAI, released in 2024) estimated 718 snow leopards in the country, the most in Ladakh.

Quick question: International Day of the Snow Leopard is observed on which date?

  1. 29 July
  2. 3 March
  3. 23 October
  4. 4 October
Show answer

(C) 23 October International Day of the Snow Leopard is observed on 23 October. (29 July is International Tiger Day, 3 March World Wildlife Day, 4 October World Animal Day.)

NASA के पर्सिवियरेंस रोवर ने मंगल पर दूरी का नया रिकॉर्ड बनाया

NASA ने 12 अक्टूबर 2026 को बताया कि उसका पर्सिवियरेंस रोवर 11 अक्टूबर तक मंगल पर 45.22 किलोमीटर चल चुका था, जो किसी भी रोवर से ज़्यादा है। अपनी स्वचालित नेविगेशन प्रणाली की मदद से इसने ऑपर्च्युनिटी रोवर का 45.16 किलोमीटर का रिकॉर्ड तोड़ा।

📌 परीक्षा के लिए याद रखें

  1. क्यूरियोसिटी
  2. स्पिरिट
  3. ऑपर्च्युनिटी
  4. सोजर्नर
उत्तर देखें

(C) ऑपर्च्युनिटी पर्सिवियरेंस मंगल पर 45.22 किमी चल चुका है और उसने ऑपर्च्युनिटी रोवर का 45.16 किमी का रिकॉर्ड तोड़ा।
