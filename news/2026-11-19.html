Government scheme3/5

Export Promotion Mission: Commerce Department widens support for small exporters under 'Niryat Disha'

On 17 November 2026, the Department of Commerce amended the guidelines of four interventions under the Niryat Disha sub-scheme of the Export Promotion Mission (EPM). Airfare support for trade fairs is now up to 95% for micro and small exporters, the TRACE certificate list grows from 462 to 600, and LIFT freight support now covers low export-intensity districts in 34 States/UTs (earlier 13).

📌 Remember for the exam
  • EPM (outlay Rs 25,060 crore, FY 2025-26 to 2030-31) has two sub-schemes: Niryat Protsahan (trade finance) and Niryat Disha (market access)
  • LIFT now also covers waterways; air-freight support extended to Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh besides the North-East

  4. 36
