India's horticulture production to hit a record 386.9 million tonnes in 2025-26

India and Sweden launch LeadIT 3.0 at COP31; Brazil joins as newest member

On 17 November 2026, Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav announced the launch of LeadIT 3.0, the third phase of the Leadership Group for Industry Transition, at COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, co-chairing the event with Sweden.
