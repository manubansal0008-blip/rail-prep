Anuj Shrivatri becomes India's 100th chess Grandmaster

On 8 November 2026, 22-year-old Anuj Shrivatri became India's 100th chess Grandmaster by winning the 7th ASEAN+ Individual Open Chess Championship in Ozamiz City, the Philippines. He scored 7 points from 9 rounds after losing his opening game. He is also the first Grandmaster from Madhya Pradesh.

Economy / Banking2/5

Revised lower MRPs of 1,364 anti-cancer medicines come into effect

From 9 November 2026, revised MRPs of 1,364 anti-cancer formulations (121 drugs) came into effect after the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) capped trade margins on 137 anti-cancer drugs. 679 brands cut prices by more than 50%, the biggest cut being 96.45%. Consumers are expected to save ₹3,540.38 crore a year.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • NPPA works under the Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers
  • Prices can be checked on the Pharma Sahi Daam app; complaints via Pharma Jan Samadhan or helpline 1800-111-255

  2. CDSCO
  3. FSSAI
  4. ICMR
Show answer

(A) NPPA The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) capped trade margins on 137 anti-cancer drugs; revised MRPs of 1,364 formulations came into effect on 9 November 2026.

Source: PIB, 9 Nov 2026

South Coast Railway opens Nandyal–Gazulapalli double line in Andhra Pradesh

South Coast Railway (SCoR) commissioned the 15.12-km double-line section between Nandyal and Gazulapalli for trains at up to 100 km/h (announced 9 November 2026), after inspection by the Commissioner of Railway Safety (CRS). It is a major step in the Guntur–Guntakal doubling project. The section has 18 bridges, six new loop lines and three new high-level platforms.

Uttarakhand celebrates its 26th Foundation Day on 9 November

Uttarakhand celebrated its 26th Foundation Day (Statehood Day) on 9 November 2026. The state was carved out of Uttar Pradesh and came into existence on 9 November 2000.
