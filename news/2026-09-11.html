Southern Railway completes 100% electrification of its 5,068 km broad-gauge network

Southern Railway has electrified its entire broad-gauge network of 5,068 route kilometres, it was reported on 11 September 2026. The last stretch, the 71 km Pattukkottai–Karaikkudi section in Tamil Nadu, was cleared after the statutory inspection by Principal Chief Electrical Engineer Ganesh on 9 September. Trains in the zone will no longer need to change between electric and diesel locomotives, improving locomotive use and cutting fossil fuel dependence.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Southern Railway has its headquarters in Chennai; electric traction was introduced in Madras (Chennai) on 11 May 1931, the second metro city after Bombay to get it.
  • The 46 km metre-gauge Nilgiri Mountain Railway, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is not being electrified and continues with steam and heritage diesel engines.

Shivraj Singh Chouhan launches guidelines for DILRMP 3.0 with ₹565.50 crore outlay

Union Rural Development Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan launched the Operational Guidelines for the third phase of the Digital India Land Records Modernisation Programme (DILRMP 3.0) in New Delhi on 10 September 2026. The Central Sector Scheme has an outlay of ₹565.50 crore for 2026–31 with 100% central funding for all States and UTs. It will give every land parcel a 14-digit Bhu-Aadhaar (ULPIN) and convert 75 high-footfall Sub-Registrar Offices into Registration Seva Kendras.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Earlier phases digitised 99.9% of Records of Rights and 97% of cadastral maps; the NAKSHA pilot will issue Urban Property Cards for urban land.
  • DILRMP is run by the Department of Land Resources under the Ministry of Rural Development.

Economy / Banking3/5

Centre amends e-commerce rules: 'prior price' to be lowest price of last 30 days

The Department of Consumer Affairs has notified the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026, reported on 11 September 2026; they come into force on 1 January 2027. Platforms must show the reduced price along with the prior price, which must be the lowest price offered in the preceding 30 days, and must not manipulate search results. E-commerce entities must also join the National Consumer Helpline as convergence partners and clearly disclose sponsored listings.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The rules amend the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, framed under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019; National Consumer Day is observed on 24 December.
  • In 2025 the National Consumer Helpline received about 17.71 lakh grievances, nearly 29% of them (over 5.11 lakh) about e-commerce.

WHO South-East Asia Regional Committee adopts Dili Declaration; India honoured

The 79th session of the WHO Regional Committee for South-East Asia, held in Dili, Timor-Leste, from 7 to 10 September 2026, adopted the 'Dili Declaration on Human Resources for Health: Equity in Specialised Care'. India, represented by Union Health Minister J.P. Nadda, received WHO awards for sustaining the elimination of maternal and neonatal tetanus and for its work on non-communicable diseases. WHO noted that 94 million people with hypertension or diabetes in India were on standard care by December 2025, above the 75 million target.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The WHO South-East Asia Regional Office (SEARO) is in New Delhi; WHO was founded on 7 April 1948 (World Health Day) and is headquartered in Geneva.
  • Dili is the capital of Timor-Leste; the session was chaired by its Health Minister Dr Elia dos Reis Amaral.
