Indian Army contingent leaves for Thailand for Exercise MAITREE-XV

An Indian Army contingent left for Thailand on 17 August 2026 for the 15th edition of the India–Thailand joint military exercise MAITREE, to be held from 18 to 31 August 2026 at Vibhavadi Rangsit Camp, Surat Thani. Each side has 85 personnel – the Indian side mainly from the 9 Gorkha Rifles and the Thai side from the 3rd Battalion, 25th Infantry Brigade, 5th Division of the Royal Thai Army. The company-level exercise focuses on joint counter-insurgency and counter-terrorism operations in jungle and semi-urban terrain under Chapter VII of the UN Charter.

  • Bangkok is the capital of Thailand; its currency is the Baht.

Economy / Banking3/5

PLFS July 2026: labour force participation rises to 55.4%

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation released the 16th monthly Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) bulletin, for July 2026, on 17 August 2026. The overall Labour Force Participation Rate (LFPR) rose to 55.4% from 54.4% in June, and the female LFPR rose to 34.4% from 32.7%. The rural LFPR stood at 58.0% and the unemployment rate at 5.1%.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The PLFS is conducted by the National Statistics Office (NSO) under MoSPI; it was launched in April 2017 and monthly bulletins began in 2025.
  • LFPR is the share of people aged 15 years and above who are either working or looking for work.

Quick question: According to the PLFS bulletin for July 2026, what was the overall Labour Force Participation Rate?

  2. 54.4%
  3. 34.4%
  4. 58.0%
Show answer

(A) 55.4% Overall LFPR rose to 55.4% (from 54.4% in June); female LFPR was 34.4% and rural LFPR 58.0%.

Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme crosses 106 approved projects

The Ministry of Electronics and IT approved 31 new proposals under the Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS), taking the total to 106 approved projects, as announced on 17 August 2026. Cumulative investment has reached ₹69,548 crore against ₹59,350 crore originally envisaged, with projected production of ₹5,34,101 crore and 74,628 direct jobs across 15 States. Of these, 38 plants are already in production.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • ECMS was notified in April 2025 with an outlay of ₹22,919 crore to build a domestic electronics component supply chain.
  • The scheme is run by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).

  4. 74

PLFS जुलाई 2026: श्रम बल भागीदारी दर बढ़कर 55.4%

सांख्यिकी और कार्यक्रम कार्यान्वयन मंत्रालय ने 17 अगस्त 2026 को जुलाई 2026 का आवधिक श्रम बल सर्वेक्षण (PLFS) का 16वां मासिक बुलेटिन जारी किया। कुल श्रम बल भागीदारी दर (LFPR) जून के 54.4% से बढ़कर 55.4% हो गई और महिलाओं की LFPR 32.7% से बढ़कर 34.4% रही। ग्रामीण LFPR 58.0% और बेरोज़गारी दर 5.1% रही।

  • PLFS सांख्यिकी मंत्रालय (MoSPI) के राष्ट्रीय सांख्यिकी कार्यालय (NSO) द्वारा किया जाता है; यह अप्रैल 2017 में शुरू हुआ और इसके मासिक बुलेटिन 2025 से आने लगे।
  • LFPR 15 वर्ष और उससे अधिक उम्र के उन लोगों का प्रतिशत है जो काम कर रहे हैं या काम की तलाश में हैं।

  2. 54.4%
  3. 34.4%
  4. 58.0%

(A) 55.4% कुल LFPR जून के 54.4% से बढ़कर 55.4% हुई; महिला LFPR 34.4% और ग्रामीण LFPR 58.0% रही।
