RBI keeps repo rate unchanged at 5.25%

The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) voted unanimously on 5 August 2026 to keep the repo rate unchanged at 5.25%. The Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate stays at 5% and the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate and Bank Rate at 5.5%. The RBI projected CPI inflation for FY27 at 5.0% and real GDP growth at 6.7%; the next MPC meeting is scheduled for 5–7 October 2026.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The six-member MPC, headed by the RBI Governor, was set up under Section 45ZB of the RBI Act, 1934 (amended in 2016).
  • The RBI was established on 1 April 1935 and is headquartered in Mumbai.

Science / Space3/5

IIT Patna develops hybrid magneto-rheometer to test smart MR fluids

As per a PIB release of 3 August 2026, IIT Patna has developed a hybrid magneto-rheometer for testing magnetorheological (MR) fluids, smart fluids whose flow behaviour changes under a magnetic field. Built by a team led by Prof. Chiranjit Sarkar with support from the Department of Science and Technology (DST), the device measures both rheological (flow) and tribological (friction and wear) properties. It can test the fluids under compression and shear at the same time.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • IIT Patna was set up in 2008 and its campus is at Bihta, near Patna, Bihar.
  • The Department of Science and Technology (DST) was set up in May 1971 under the Ministry of Science and Technology.

Vande Bharat carries a live donor heart from Surat to Ahmedabad, a first for Indian Railways

For the first time, Indian Railways carried a live donor heart by train, taking it from Surat to Ahmedabad on the Mumbai Central–Gandhinagar Capital Vande Bharat Express (20901), as reported on 31 July 2026. The heart was taken to the U.N. Mehta Institute of Cardiology & Research Centre, Ahmedabad, for transplant, with a green corridor from Platform No. 1 of Ahmedabad station to the hospital. Railways, the Railway Protection Force (RPF), Gujarat Police and medical teams coordinated the mission.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The first Vande Bharat Express ran between New Delhi and Varanasi and was flagged off on 15 February 2019.
  • Vande Bharat trains were first built at the Integral Coach Factory (ICF), Chennai.
