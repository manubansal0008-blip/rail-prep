India win both kabaddi golds at Asian Games, beating Iran in the finals

India won both kabaddi gold medals at the 20th Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, on 26 September 2026. The women's team beat Iran 37-34 in the final to retain their title, and the men's team also beat Iran 40-34 in their final. The women's win was India's third gold of the Games.

📌 Remember for the exam

  • The 2026 win is the Indian men's ninth Asian Games kabaddi gold; men's kabaddi has been part of the Games since 1990 (Beijing).

CSIR marks 85th Foundation Day; science magazines launched in five languages

The Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) celebrated its 85th Foundation Day on 26 September 2026. Union Minister Dr Jitendra Singh launched five popular science magazines published by CSIR-NIScPR in Assamese (Bigyan Lahar), Bengali (Bigyan Brittanto), Marathi, Tamil and Telugu (Vigyan Velugu), to take science to readers in their own languages.

Economy / Banking5/5

Kandla, JNPA, Paradip and Mundra notified as India's first 'mega ports'

The Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways on 25 September 2026 notified Deendayal Port (Kandla), Jawaharlal Nehru Port (Nhava Sheva), Paradip Port and Mundra Port as mega ports under Section 73 of the Indian Ports Act, 2025. A port qualifies by handling 150 million tonnes of bulk cargo or 7.5 million TEUs of containers in the previous financial year, and the status is valid for five years. Mundra is the only privately run port on the list.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Paradip Port is in Odisha; it is the only one of the four on India's east coast.
  • Deendayal Port (Kandla) and Mundra Port are both in the Kutch district of Gujarat.

  1. Major Port Authorities Act, 2021
  2. Merchant Shipping Act, 1958
  3. Indian Ports Act, 2025
  4. Inland Vessels Act, 2021
Show answer

(C) Indian Ports Act, 2025 Kandla, JNPA, Paradip and Mundra were notified as mega ports on 25 September 2026 under Section 73 of the Indian Ports Act, 2025.
