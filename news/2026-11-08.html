Railways run about 18,000 festive special trains; Vaishnaw orders FOB survey at Anand Vihar Terminal

Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw inspected Delhi's Anand Vihar Terminal on 7 November 2026 amid the Diwali–Chhath rush and said about 18,000 special trains are being run this festive season, carrying more than 2 crore passengers. IAF's IL-76MD makes its highest-ever landing at Nyoma airbase in Ladakh (13,629 ft)

The Indian Air Force announced on 7 November 2026 that an IL-76MD heavy-lift transport aircraft landed at the Mudh-Nyoma airbase in eastern Ladakh at an altitude of 13,629 feet. The IAF called it the highest-altitude landing ever achieved by the IL-76MD anywhere in the world and said it reinforces India's strategic airlift capability. Nyoma lies close to the Line of Actual Control (LAC) with China and became a full-fledged airbase in 2025.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Mudh-Nyoma airbase is in eastern Ladakh near the LAC, close to Demchok and Pangong Tso.
  • The IL-76MD is a Russian-origin (Ilyushin) four-engine heavy-lift transport aircraft used by the IAF.

International3/5

India and EU conclude talks for India to join the €93.5 billion Horizon Europe research programme

The European Commission said on 6 November 2026 that negotiations on India's association with Horizon Europe, the EU's flagship research and innovation programme, have concluded; India's Ministry of Science and Technology confirmed it via PIB on 7 November 2026. Once the agreement is signed and ratified, Indian researchers, institutions and companies can get direct Horizon Europe funding and lead projects on the same terms as EU member states, in return for a financial contribution. The association is expected to take effect from January 2027.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Horizon Europe has a budget of €93.5 billion and runs from 2021 to 2027.
  • India–EU science cooperation rests on a 2001 Agreement on Science and Technology Cooperation.

Gurnoor Brar's 4/30 helps India beat New Zealand by 4 runs (DLS) in Wellington, lead ODI series 2–0
