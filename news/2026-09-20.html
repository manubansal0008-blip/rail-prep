20th Asian Games open in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan

The 20th Asian Games opened on 19 September 2026 in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, and will run till 4 October 2026. India has sent a contingent of 508 athletes across 37 sports. Prime Minister Narendra Modi posted his good wishes for the Indian team on X; Manipur alone has 36 athletes in the squad, including 12 in sepaktakraw.

  • The Asian Games are organised by the Olympic Council of Asia (OCA), headquartered in Kuwait City; India won 107 medals at the previous Games in Hangzhou (2023).

Government scheme3/5

Biometric Aadhaar check mandatory for subsidised LPG refills from 1 October

From 1 October 2026, domestic LPG consumers will have to complete biometric Aadhaar authentication to book subsidised cylinder refills, the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas said, as reported on 19 September 2026. About 27.43 crore consumers have already completed it; others can do so at the time of delivery, at the distributor's showroom or through the oil marketing company's app. The rule aims to stop subsidised cylinders being diverted for commercial use.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY), which gives free LPG connections to poor households, was launched on 1 May 2016 at Ballia, Uttar Pradesh.
  • LPG subsidy is paid directly into consumers' bank accounts under the PAHAL (DBTL) scheme, extended nationwide on 1 January 2015.

Quick question: From which date is biometric Aadhaar authentication mandatory for booking subsidised domestic LPG refills?

  1. 1 September 2026
  2. 2 October 2026
  3. 1 November 2026
  4. 1 October 2026
Show answer

(D) 1 October 2026 The Petroleum Ministry made biometric Aadhaar authentication mandatory from 1 October 2026; about 27.43 crore consumers had already completed it.

IIT Bombay to start chip design (EDA) course taught largely by industry experts

IIT Bombay plans to start an Electronic Design Automation (EDA) course in the coming academic year, to be taught largely by industry executives, Associate Professor Swaroop Ganguly said at SEMICON India 2026 in New Delhi, as reported on 19 September 2026.   • IIT Bombay was set up in 1958 at Powai, Mumbai, with assistance from UNESCO and the Soviet Union.
