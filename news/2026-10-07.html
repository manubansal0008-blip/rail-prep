Economy / Banking1/5

RBI raises repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50%

The RBI's Monetary Policy Committee, led by Governor Sanjay Malhotra, raised the repo rate by 25 basis points from 5.25% to 5.50% on 7 October 2026 – its first hike since February 2023. The stance was changed from 'neutral' to 'calibrated tightening'. The RBI raised its 2026-27 GDP growth forecast to 7.1%.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • After the hike, the Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate is 5.25% and the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate and Bank Rate are 5.75%.
  • The Monetary Policy Committee has 6 members and is chaired by the RBI Governor.

  3. 5.75%

Show answer

(B) 5.50% The RBI raised the repo rate by 25 basis points, from 5.25% to 5.50%, its first hike since February 2023.

₹661.50 crore contract for BrahMos fire control systems for Navy ships

The Ministry of Defence signed a ₹661.50 crore contract with BrahMos Aerospace Private Limited in New Delhi on 6 October 2026 for BrahMos fire control systems and launchers for Indian Navy warships. The deal is under the 'Buy (Indian-IDDM)' category and has at least 68% indigenous content.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • BrahMos is a supersonic cruise missile developed jointly by India and Russia; its name comes from the Brahmaputra and Moskva rivers.
  • IDDM stands for Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured.

Government scheme3/5

Cabinet approves ₹10,000 crore SME Growth Fund

The Union Cabinet on 6 October 2026 approved an SME Growth Fund with a government commitment of ₹10,000 crore. The money will go into an Alternative Investment Fund that makes direct equity investments in small and medium enterprises, mainly in manufacturing. The fund was announced in the Union Budget 2026-27.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) are regulated by SEBI under the SEBI (AIF) Regulations, 2012.
  • SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) is headquartered in Mumbai.

Science / Space4/5

HAL Koraput delivers its 2,000th aero-engine

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh marked the delivery of the 2,000th aero-engine by HAL's Koraput Division in Odisha on 6 October 2026. He also launched projects worth ₹553 crore there: a second production line for Shakti helicopter engines (₹218 crore) and a ₹335 crore expansion that will raise output of AL-31FP engines for the Su-30MKI from 30 to 50 a year.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • The Shakti engine powers the Dhruv Advanced Light Helicopter and the Prachand Light Combat Helicopter.
  • HAL's Koraput Division was set up in 1964; HAL is headquartered in Bengaluru.

Railways5/5

Cabinet approves Integrated Transport and Logistics Authority, covering railways

The Union Cabinet on 6 October 2026 approved the Integrated Transport and Logistics Authority (ITLA), set up as a Special Purpose Vehicle. It will prepare a National Transport Master Plan of 10 years or more covering railways, roads, ports, civil aviation, inland waterways, coastal shipping, urban mobility and logistics. ITLA will also do the technical appraisal of central infrastructure projects costing ₹500 crore or more, including rail projects.

📌 Remember for the exam
  • ITLA will build a National Transport Data Repository using GSTN e-way bills, FASTag, Vahan and GPS data.
  • 'Railways' is in the Union List (Entry 22) of the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution.

Quick question: Under ITLA, approved on 6 October 2026, central infrastructure projects above what cost will get technical appraisal?

  1. ₹100 crore
  2. ₹500 crore
  3. ₹1,000 crore
  4. ₹5,000 crore
Show answer

(B) ₹500 crore ITLA will carry out technical appraisal of Government of India infrastructure projects costing ₹500 crore or more.

गवर्नर संजय मल्होत्रा की अध्यक्षता में RBI की मौद्रिक नीति समिति ने 7 अक्टूबर 2026 को रेपो रेट 25 बेसिस पॉइंट बढ़ाकर 5.25% से 5.50% कर दिया – फ़रवरी 2023 के बाद यह पहली बढ़ोतरी है। नीति का रुख 'न्यूट्रल' से बदलकर 'कैलिब्रेटेड टाइटनिंग' किया गया। RBI ने 2026-27 के लिए GDP वृद्धि का अनुमान बढ़ाकर 7.1% कर दिया।

  • मौद्रिक नीति समिति में 6 सदस्य होते हैं और इसकी अध्यक्षता RBI गवर्नर करते हैं।

HAL कोरापुट ने अपना 2,000वाँ एयरो-इंजन सौंपा

रक्षा मंत्री राजनाथ सिंह ने 6 अक्टूबर 2026 को ओडिशा में HAL के कोरापुट डिवीज़न द्वारा 2,000वें एयरो-इंजन की डिलीवरी के कार्यक्रम में हिस्सा लिया। उन्होंने वहाँ ₹553 करोड़ की परियोजनाएँ भी शुरू कीं: शक्ति हेलिकॉप्टर इंजन की दूसरी प्रोडक्शन लाइन (₹218 करोड़) और ₹335 करोड़ का विस्तार, जिससे Su-30MKI के AL-31FP इंजनों का उत्पादन हर साल 30 से बढ़कर 50 हो जाएगा।
