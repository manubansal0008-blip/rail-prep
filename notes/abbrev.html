Common Abbreviations आम संक्षिप्त रूप (फ़ुल फ़ॉर्म)

Interactive version · इंटरैक्टिव नोटPractise this topic · प्रैक्टिस करें

English

'Common abbreviations' is a syllabus line in NTPC. Questions give a short form and ask for the full form, or ask which full form is wrong. Railway abbreviations are a favourite in RRB papers.

📌 Key concepts

Railway

  • PNR: Passenger Name Record
  • RAC: Reservation Against Cancellation
  • TTE: Travelling Ticket Examiner
  • RPF: Railway Protection Force
  • DRM: Divisional Railway Manager
  • IRCTC: Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation
  • RDSO: Research Designs and Standards Organisation
  • CRS: Commissioner of Railway Safety

Indian organisations

  • ISRO: Indian Space Research Organisation
  • DRDO: Defence Research and Development Organisation
  • BARC: Bhabha Atomic Research Centre
  • CAG: Comptroller and Auditor General
  • UPSC: Union Public Service Commission
  • SEBI: Securities and Exchange Board of India
  • NABARD: National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development
  • NITI: National Institution for Transforming India
  • CBI: Central Bureau of Investigation
  • NDRF: National Disaster Response Force
  • NCC: National Cadet Corps
  • AIIMS: All India Institute of Medical Sciences

Money and documents

  • GDP: Gross Domestic Product
  • GST: Goods and Services Tax
  • UPI: Unified Payments Interface
  • NEFT: National Electronic Funds Transfer
  • RTGS: Real Time Gross Settlement
  • IFSC: Indian Financial System Code
  • PAN: Permanent Account Number
  • PIN: Postal Index Number
  • KYC: Know Your Customer
  • ATM: Automated Teller Machine

World bodies

  • UNESCO: United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization
  • UNICEF: United Nations Children's Fund (first: International Children's Emergency Fund)
  • SAARC: South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation
  • ASEAN: Association of Southeast Asian Nations
  • OPEC: Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries
  • NATO: North Atlantic Treaty Organization
  • FIFA: Fédération Internationale de Football Association
  • IAEA: International Atomic Energy Agency

Science and technology

  • LASER: Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation
  • RADAR: Radio Detection And Ranging
  • SONAR: Sound Navigation And Ranging
  • LED: Light Emitting Diode
  • LPG / CNG: Liquefied Petroleum Gas / Compressed Natural Gas
  • DNA: Deoxyribonucleic Acid
  • AIDS: Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome
  • GPS: Global Positioning System

📋 Key facts

PIN code
6 digits; first digit = PIN region (India has 9 PIN zones, the 9th for the Army Postal Service)
PAN
10 characters, issued by the Income Tax Department
IFSC
11 characters (first 4 = bank code)
Aadhaar
12 digits, issued by UIDAI

✍️ Solved examples

Example 1

What is the full form of PNR, and how many digits does a PNR number have?

  1. PNR = Passenger Name Record
  2. It is the booking ID on a train ticket
  3. It has 10 digits

Answer: Passenger Name Record; 10 digits

Example 2

Which full form is wrong? (a) RADAR – Radio Detection And Ranging (b) LASER – Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation (c) SONAR – Sound Navigation And Radar

  1. (a) is correct
  2. (b) is correct
  3. (c) is wrong: SONAR = Sound Navigation And Ranging

Answer: (c)

Example 3

How many characters are there in a PAN, an IFSC code and an Aadhaar number together?

  1. PAN = 10 characters
  2. IFSC = 11 characters; Aadhaar = 12 digits
  3. Total = 10 + 11 + 12 = 33

Answer: 33

⚡ Quick tricks

  • RADAR and SONAR both end in 'Ranging'.
  • Railway set: PNR, RAC, TTE, RPF, DRM, CRS – learn as one group.
  • Lengths: PIN 6, PAN 10, PNR 10, IFSC 11, Aadhaar 12.
  • UNICEF kept its old letters even after the name changed to 'Children's Fund'.

⚠ Common mistakes

  • Writing RAC as 'Reservation After Cancellation' (it is Against).
  • Expanding NITI Aayog as 'National Institute…' (it is National Institution for Transforming India).
  • Writing SONAR's last word as 'Radar'.
  • Mixing up NEFT and RTGS (RTGS is Real Time Gross Settlement, for big amounts).

हिंदी

'आम संक्षिप्त रूप' NTPC सिलेबस की एक लाइन है। प्रश्नों में छोटा रूप देकर पूरा रूप पूछा जाता है, या पूछा जाता है कि कौन-सा पूरा रूप ग़लत है। रेलवे के संक्षिप्त रूप RRB पेपर में ख़ूब आते हैं। (पूरे रूप अंग्रेज़ी में ही याद करें, क्योंकि प्रश्न ऐसे ही आते हैं।)

✍️ हल किए गए उदाहरण

उदाहरण 1

PNR का पूरा रूप क्या है, और PNR नंबर में कितने अंक होते हैं?

  1. PNR = Passenger Name Record
  2. यह ट्रेन टिकट की बुकिंग ID है
  3. इसमें 10 अंक होते हैं

उत्तर: Passenger Name Record; 10 अंक

उदाहरण 2

कौन-सा पूरा रूप ग़लत है? (a) RADAR – Radio Detection And Ranging (b) LASER – Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation (c) SONAR – Sound Navigation And Radar

  1. (a) सही है
  2. (b) सही है
  3. (c) ग़लत है: SONAR = Sound Navigation And Ranging

उत्तर: (c)

उदाहरण 3

PAN, IFSC कोड और आधार नंबर में कुल मिलाकर कितने अक्षर-अंक होते हैं?

  1. PAN = 10
  2. IFSC = 11; आधार = 12 अंक
  3. कुल = 10 + 11 + 12 = 33

उत्तर: 33

⚡ शॉर्टकट ट्रिक

  • RADAR और SONAR दोनों 'Ranging' पर ख़त्म होते हैं।
  • रेलवे वाला समूह: PNR, RAC, TTE, RPF, DRM, CRS – एक साथ याद करें।
  • लंबाई: PIN 6, PAN 10, PNR 10, IFSC 11, आधार 12।
  • UNICEF ने नाम 'Children's Fund' होने के बाद भी पुराने अक्षर रखे।

⚠ आम गलतियाँ

  • RAC को 'Reservation After Cancellation' लिखना (सही है Against)।
  • NITI आयोग को 'National Institute…' लिखना (सही है National Institution for Transforming India)।
  • SONAR का आख़िरी शब्द 'Radar' लिखना।
  • NEFT और RTGS में गड़बड़ करना (RTGS = Real Time Gross Settlement, बड़ी रक़म के लिए)।
