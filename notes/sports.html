India's medals in 2026

  • Asian Games, Aichi-Nagoya: 85 medals (21 gold, 27 silver, 37 bronze), 4th place – India's second-best Asian Games ever. Athletics gave 24 medals; the men's hockey team won gold again. Flag-bearers: Manu Bhaker and Pawan Sehrawat.
  • Asian Para Games, Aichi-Nagoya: record 114 medals (30 gold, 35 silver, 49 bronze), 4th place – India's best ever. Para athletics gave 65 medals (22 gold); archer Sheetal Devi won 2 gold and 1 silver.
  • Commonwealth Games, Glasgow: 39 medals (13 gold, 17 silver, 9 bronze), 4th place. Boxing gave 10 medals (7 gold); Mirabai Chanu won weightlifting gold (48 kg). Wrestling, badminton, table tennis, hockey and shooting were not in this smaller Games.
  • T20 World Cup: captain Suryakumar Yadav; India's 3rd T20 title and the first team to defend it. Final: India 255/5, New Zealand 159. Player of the Match Jasprit Bumrah (4/15); Player of the Tournament Sanju Samson.
  • Winter Olympics: Arif Khan (alpine skiing) and Stanzin Lundup (cross-country skiing) took part; no medal. National Sports Awards 2026 (announced 18 Aug 2026): 17 Arjuna Awards and no Khel Ratna.

FIFA World Cup 2026 details

  • First World Cup with 48 teams and 104 matches; hosted by three countries.
  • Final on 19 July 2026 at New York New Jersey Stadium: Spain 1–0 Argentina after extra time (Ferran Torres, 106th minute). Spain's 2nd title (first in 2010).
  • Golden Ball: Rodri (Spain). Golden Boot: Kylian Mbappé (France, 10 goals). Golden Glove: Unai Simón (Spain).

✍️ Solved examples

Example 1

Where and when were the 2026 Asian Games held, and where did India finish?

  1. The 20th Asian Games were held in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, from 19 September to 4 October 2026.
  2. India won 85 medals: 21 gold, 27 silver, 37 bronze.
  3. India was 4th; China, Japan and South Korea were the top three.

Answer: Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, 19 Sep–4 Oct 2026; India 4th with 85 medals

Example 2

India won 13 gold, 17 silver and 9 bronze at the 2026 Commonwealth Games. Find the total, and what fraction of it was gold?

  1. Total = 13 + 17 + 9 = 39
  2. Gold share = 13 ÷ 39 = 1/3
  3. So one medal in every three was gold

Answer: 39 medals; 1/3 were gold

Example 3

Who won the 2026 FIFA World Cup, and how many teams played in it?

  1. The 2026 World Cup was hosted by the USA, Canada and Mexico with 48 teams (up from 32).
  2. In the final on 19 July 2026, Spain beat Argentina 1–0 after extra time.
  3. Ferran Torres scored in the 106th minute; it is Spain's 2nd title.

Answer: Spain (beat Argentina 1–0); 48 teams

  • Make a three-part line for each event: host – dates – India's result.
  • Asian Games and Asian Para Games 2026 had the same host: Aichi-Nagoya, Japan.
  • Milano Cortina = Winter Olympics and Winter Paralympics 2026 (Italy).
  • India's 2026 medal counts: 85 (Asian Games), 114 (Para Games), 39 (CWG).
  • Next hosts: LA 2028, Doha 2030 (Asian Games), Ahmedabad 2030 (CWG), Brisbane 2032.

⚠ Common mistakes

  • Writing Tokyo as the 2026 Asian Games host; it was Aichi-Nagoya.
  • Thinking India played the FIFA World Cup 2026 – India did not qualify.
  • Thinking the 2026 CWG had wrestling and shooting; they were dropped from Glasgow 2026.
  • Using the 2025 National Sports Awards list as current.

उदाहरण 1

2026 के एशियन गेम्स कहाँ और कब हुए, और भारत किस स्थान पर रहा?

  1. 20वें एशियन गेम्स आइची-नागोया, जापान में 19 सितंबर से 4 अक्टूबर 2026 तक हुए।
  2. भारत ने 85 पदक जीते: 21 स्वर्ण, 27 रजत, 37 कांस्य।
  3. भारत चौथे स्थान पर रहा; चीन, जापान और दक्षिण कोरिया पहले तीन पर रहे।

उत्तर: आइची-नागोया, जापान, 19 सितंबर–4 अक्टूबर 2026; भारत चौथे, 85 पदक

उदाहरण 2

2026 कॉमनवेल्थ गेम्स में भारत ने 13 स्वर्ण, 17 रजत और 9 कांस्य जीते। कुल पदक बताइए, और उनमें स्वर्ण कितना भाग था?

  1. कुल = 13 + 17 + 9 = 39
  2. स्वर्ण का भाग = 13 ÷ 39 = 1/3
  3. यानी हर तीन में से एक पदक स्वर्ण था

उत्तर: 39 पदक; 1/3 स्वर्ण

उदाहरण 3

2026 का FIFA वर्ल्ड कप किसने जीता, और उसमें कितनी टीमें खेलीं?

  1. 2026 वर्ल्ड कप की मेज़बानी अमेरिका, कनाडा और मेक्सिको ने की, 48 टीमों के साथ (पहले 32 थीं)।
  2. 19 जुलाई 2026 के फ़ाइनल में स्पेन ने अर्जेंटीना को एक्स्ट्रा टाइम में 1–0 से हराया।
  3. फ़ेरान टोरेस ने 106वें मिनट में गोल किया; यह स्पेन का दूसरा ख़िताब है।

उत्तर: स्पेन (अर्जेंटीना को 1–0 से हराया); 48 टीमें
